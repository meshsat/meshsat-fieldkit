# Building a V2 kit from this repository

The MESHSAT-830 generation of the carrier set (7 Sep 2026) has been designed and generated; no board of it has been fabricated and no kit has been built. This page is the path from an empty JLCPCB cart to a running kit, in order, pointing at the documents that carry the detail. Read it together with `docs/ASSEMBLY.md` (fasteners, build order, the pack, leads, coating, labels, removal, bench checks, bench-fit lists), `docs/PANEL.md` (the panel controller contract), `docs/V2-SPEC.md` (the device set and the budgets) and `docs/TEST-PLAN.md` (the qualification plan). The design record with every number and every ruling is `docs/MESHSAT-709-geometry-appendix.md`; sections 32.49 to 32.61 are this generation.

MeshSat is a prototype. Expect changes after the first build.

## 0. Before you start

- The rulings that are not open for redesign (appendix 32.49 to 32.53): three identical Compute Module 5 slots on one carrier, every radio and sensor a USB device shared by the HAL, k3s across the three modules; a built 4S smart pack in the east pocket (appendix 32.62; the BB-2590/U of 32.49 did not fit the case) with the charger, gauge reading and power control on PCB-A; no vent opening anywhere in the case, the plate or the connector plate (internal IP68 fans carry the heat to the skin); EMCON is a hardware line from the panel toggle to every transmitter rail gate and to the PA keying, plus a software hold; the panel is a USB device driven per `docs/PANEL.md`.
- Nothing on the bench gates the first order: every COTS site was checked against the manufacturer's own drawing, and the documents are in `vendor/` (rows marked "previous generation" belong to the released set of 5 and 6 September and stay for the record).
- The software of this generation is specified but not written: the panel controller firmware (MESHSAT-837), the sensor controller (838), the HAL device sharing and the per-device services (835 to 848). A first kit powers up and boots its modules; the panel, the radios and the sensors come alive with that software.

## 1. Order the boards

Seven boards, six assembled by the fabricator (standard assembly; the economic tier is not offered for these sizes), one bare. Everything you upload is in `release/revA/order/<board>/`: the Gerber zip, the JLC-format BOM and CPL (rotation offsets applied, bench-fitted parts removed, designator ranges expanded) and `ORDER-NOTES.txt` with the exact settings and the via-in-pad count of the board. `ORDER-LOG.md` in the same folder records the 3 September 2026 cart run line by line; the cart is not rebuilt until the owner declares this set final.

| Board | Folder | Size (mm) | Layers | Stackup |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PCB-A POWER + I/O A22 | `PCB-A-POWER-A22/` | 240 x 160 | 6 | JLC06161H-3313 (In1 and In4 solid ground) |
| PCB-B COMPUTE B16 | `PCB-B-COMPUTE-B16/` | 330 x 200 | 6 | JLC06161H-3313 (In1 solid ground, In4 the 5 V planes) |
| PCB-C PANEL BACKER C7 | `PCB-C-DISPLAY-C7/` | 344 x 228 ring, 240 x 176 void | 4 | JLC04161H-7628; the face itself is the aluminium plate of `release/revA/case/face-plate/` |
| PCB-D VHF APRS D8 | `PCB-D-APRS-D8/` | 100 x 80 | 4 | JLC04161H-7628 |
| PCB-E1 DOCK STRIP E6 | `PCB-E1-DOCK-E6/` | 267 x 68 | 4 | JLC04161H-7628 |
| PCB-E5 DOCK BLOCK E5 | `PCB-E5-BLOCK-E5/` | 43 x 26 | 2 | standard, 2 oz outer copper |
| PCB-P PACK BMS P1 | `PCB-P-PACK-P1/` | 70 x 44 | 2 | standard, 2 oz outer copper |

Common settings, from the ORDER-NOTES: 1.6 mm FR-4, 1 oz outer copper (0.5 oz inner), ENIG, matte black solder mask, white silkscreen, no castellations, order number removed, quantity 5 (the JLCPCB minimum). Ask for 90 ohm differential tuning on the USB 2.0 pairs (0.2 mm track, 0.15 mm gap on the outer layers) and on B16 for the PCIe, USB 3 and HDMI pairs (100 ohm, 0.127 mm track and gap on the six-layer stack). The fine-pitch parts (the six Compute Module receptacles, the PCIe switches, the USB 3 hubs, the RP2040 on C7) take 0.40/0.20 mm vias, 0.127 mm tracks and clearance and a 0.19 mm hole-to-copper clearance, the rules of Raspberry Pi's own CM5 IO board; JLCPCB's standard process covers them. Some ground pads carry a via in the pad (the fanout fallback where a via beside the pad had no room): the count is in each board's ORDER-NOTES, the vias are plain through vias and need no filling. Turn both free confirmation options on (production file review and the BOM/CPL confirmation) so the placement preview is checked before anything is cut. On the JLC preview, check the rotation of every polarised part against `release/revA/review/<board>/*-assembly-top.pdf`.

Parts JLCPCB does not fit are listed per board in the ORDER-NOTES ("fitted at the bench") and in `docs/ASSEMBLY.md` section 9: the Compute Modules and their receptacle-mounted cards, the bought modules (RockBLOCK, LimeSDR, the QMX unit, the sensor modules), the PA module, the connectors on flying leads and the switches. If JLCPCB has no stock for a line at order time, let it drop to DNP and fit it at the bench.

If you change a board first, regenerate it (`README.md`, "Regenerating a board"), then run `make_handoff.py`; it rewrites `order/` and `review/`. JLCPCB cannot swap Gerbers in an existing cart line, so batch changes before uploading.

## 3. Prepare the case

1. Fit the 1450PF frame per Peli's instructions; its ten inserts are the face plate's screw pattern (`release/revA/case/face-plate/` drawing).
2. End walls: drill the eleven 6.5 mm D-holes at Z 88 from the 1:1 template (sheets 3 and 4), fit the SMA couplers with their O-rings under the outside hex.
3. Back wall: cut the window for the connector plate between the ribs at X -95 and -18 (sheet 1), fit the plate with its gaskets, the two D38999 receptacles, the RJ45, the USB-C, the M8 pod receptacle and the ground stud; the sensor pod screws onto the plate over the M8.
4. Dock strip E6 on the floor along the front wall: degrease with IPA, four VHB pads at the corners, locate it by dropping two rods through its south rod holes before the pads touch.
5. The pack cradle at the west wall (after the position ruling of appendix 32.60 item 7) with the heater mat under it.
6. Bulkhead legend strips beside the SMA rows (west and east lists above), the "RF HAZARD DURING TX" label next to the VHF bulkhead, the asset label in Peli's recess (`docs/ASSEMBLY.md` section 6).

## 4. The pack

The pack is built for the kit (appendix 32.62): a 4S block of 18650 or 21700 cells welded on nickel strip, the BMS board P1 with its SMBus gauge, protection and cell thermistor, in the printed enclosure of `v2/cad/pack_4s.py` that lies in the east pocket. Its XT60 lead plugs into E6's `J_BATT` (pin 2, the pad nearer `F3`, is positive) and its SMBus pair into `J_SMB`. A22's BQ25731 charger (SMBus 0x6B) charges it from the 9 to 36 V front end and reads the pack's gauge, so the battery bar on the panel is the pack's state of charge. The pack is fused at 25 A on E6 (`F3`) and again at A22's node (`F1`). Charge current and the JEITA window are the charger's settings. The pack's own qualification (shock, vibration, temperature) is part of the kit's programme in `docs/TEST-PLAN.md`; the gauge takes its golden image from TI's bqStudio on the bench before the pack is closed.

## 5. Assemble

Follow `docs/ASSEMBLY.md` section 2 step by step; the short form:

1. Dock strip E6: solder the leads (`J_DCIN`, `J_SOLAR`, `J_BATT`, `J_SMB`, the sensor headers, the two mixer fans), fit the three fuse holders with their blades, and check the front end gives the 20 V bus at the block lands with 9 V and 36 V in. Then fit the raised block E5 on its four 6 mm standoffs, solder the twelve signal wires and the 12 AWG pair from the strip into the block's plated lands from below, and stick the strip down on its VHB pads.
2. Rods through the strip's holes with the spacers that set the dock gap (washer stack under the two north rods so all four sit level).
3. PCB-A A22: solder the Preci-Dip connector and the nine Mill-Max pins in from the underside (tails on the top face), fit the eleven blind-mate receptacles, the 25 A blade, and lower the board onto the rods so its pins land on the block; check continuity from the block's lands to the board with a meter before the first power-up.
4. PCB-D D8 on A22's four 22.6 mm standoffs at the mezzanine site: the 2x8 harness ribbon into `J_MEZZ1`/`J_HARN1`, the 5 V lead into `J_PWR1`, the antenna pigtail from `J_ANT` to A22's VHF jack `J_RF1`; the PA drive coax (`J_PAIN`), the PA output coax (`J_PAOUT`), the gate bias lead (`J_VGG`) and A22's 13.8 V lead (`J_PA`) are left coiled for the plate.
5. Bay spacers, PCB-B B16: the three Compute Modules pressed onto their receptacle pairs and screwed to the standoffs, the coolers clipped on with their fan leads in `J_FAN1..3`, the NVMe drives in `J_M2N1..3`, the WiFi link card in `J_M2C1` (slot 1) with its two pigtails to the east wall P2P couplers, the 5G module in `J_M2C2` (slot 2) with its SIMs in `J_SIM1`/`J_SIM2` and its pigtails to 5G MAIN and 5G DIV, the LimeSDR into `J_LIME` in its bay with its pigtail to SDR, the RockBLOCK on its bracket into `J_RB9704` with its pigtail to IRIDIUM, the GNSS pigtail from `J_GNSS1` to GNSS, the LoRa pigtail from `J_LORA1` to LORA, one module's antenna-kit lead to WIFI 2.4, the CR2032, the four rail leads from A22 (`J_5V_S1..3`, `J_5V_DEV`), the PoE lead (`J_54V`), the HDMI cable from `J_HDMI` and the camera and QMX leads (`J_CAM`, `J_QMX`) left for the face and the lid, the panel ribbon in `J_PANEL`; the 2x13 `J_AB1` header mates A22's at the same case XY as the board comes down. Nyloc on top.
6. Pack into its cradle, cable and SMBus into E6.
7. RF: the eleven right-angle plugs sit in their printed clamps on the dock strip and mate blind with the receptacles under A22 when the stack is lowered. The wall connectors' pigtails are torqued once at the wall side and finger tight at the device side, RG-316 with a 12.5 mm bend radius, tied at both ends.
8. The face (`docs/ASSEMBLY.md` section 2 step 8): press the eight standoffs, the two PA nuts and the sixteen light guides into the plate; the PA module on its thermal pad under the plate with its coax and bias leads to D8 and its 13.8 V lead to A22; the Xenarc on the VESA holes with its block through the cutout and its gasket; the switches, the sounder and the two headset jacks through the plate with their seals, the camera module behind its window, the e-paper taped under the lens; then the backer C7 onto the standoffs (its LEDs under the light guides, the switch leads to the lands beside each site, the e-paper flex into `J_EPD`, the headset leads to D8 `J_HS1`/`J_HS2`, the MAIN and PI leads, the ribbon), the PORON ring in the band, the plate into the frame on ten M3 with star washers. Flood and hose the face in its frame before the stack goes in (appendix 32.34, the same procedure as the previous face).
9. Lid: the QMX tray screwed to the lid's inner face over the right strip, the unit strapped in, its three leads in the lid harness (USB to B16 `J_QMX`, 12 V to A22 `J_HF`, the BNC-to-SMA jumper to the HF coupler's inside).

Torque table, threadlocker rules and every lead's wire gauge, length and connector are in `docs/ASSEMBLY.md` sections 1 and 4. Never put threadlocker on Nyloc nuts or on the switch nuts.

## 6. Coating and labels

After the bench fit, two thin coats of acrylic conformal coating on A22, B16, D8 and E6 with the masks of `docs/ASSEMBLY.md` section 5 (connector faces, the receptacles and sockets, the spring pins and their targets, SMA and U.FL bodies, the module sites, the test points, the water electrodes on E6). The backer's underside is coated after the silicone beads have cured, with `J_PANEL`, `J_EPD`, the lead lands and the standoff rings masked; the plate is bare anodised aluminium. Labels per section 6: the plate nameplate with P/N MSK-FK-1450, S/N, REV A and a data matrix; P/N, S/N, REV silk fields on every board.

## 7. Software

Three Compute Module 5: flash each eMMC with `rpiboot` through B16's `J_FLASH` with the `J_RPIBOOT` jumper on the slot being flashed (the B16 schematic names the strap per slot), Raspberry Pi OS with the carrier's overlays, then k3s across the three (one server, two agents, replicated storage on the NVMe drives) and the HAL (MESHSAT-801) that shares the USB devices cluster-wide. The kit has no radio on a module GPIO except the LoRa module on slot 3's SPI header (`meshtasticd` on `spidev0.1` with BUSY, DIO1 and reset on GPIO 23, 24 and 26): the LG290P, the two E72, the panel controller, the sensor controller, D8 (a USB audio and control set), the RockBLOCK, the LimeSDR, the QMX, the 5G module and the camera are USB devices on the per-slot hubs, and Ethernet ties the three modules through the KSZ9897 switch to the wall RJ45. The Xenarc is an HDMI monitor with USB touch behind the three-input display switch that the panel controller selects; the kiosk runs on whichever module owns the display (`docs/PANEL.md` section 5).

- Panel: `docs/PANEL.md` is the contract of the panel controller firmware (MESHSAT-837) and the bridge protocol over USB; until it lands, MAIN PWR, EMCON, ZEROIZE and the TX lamp work as hardware and everything else stays dark.
- Sensors: the sensor controller on E6 (MESHSAT-838) reports the climate, seal, water, gas, motion, lightning, Geiger and outside-pod readings over USB; the alarm rules are the bridge's.
- Power: the charger, the rail monitors and the rail enables are on the kit I2C bus mastered by the panel controller; the bridge reads them through it (SHORE, CHARGING, the battery bar, the per-slot currents) and asks it for the shore inhibit and the slot power cycles.
- The issue list of the software of this generation: MESHSAT-835 to 848 (`docs/V2-SPEC.md` section 7).

## 8. Bring-up checks

`docs/TEST-PLAN.md` is the qualification plan; the first-power checks from `docs/ASSEMBLY.md` section 8: the dock strip gives its 20 V bus with 9, 12, 24 and 36 V in, survives reverse polarity for 10 s and blows its fuse on a bolted short; each slot rail comes up to 5.1 V under 3 A; the lamp test lights all 17 LEDs, BLACKOUT kills the rail, the EMCON cover closed keeps the SA868 and the PA in receive while the bridge asserts PTT; the shore inhibit drops and restores the front end and the kit keeps running from the pack (record the current, it sets the survival time); at full load measure the current on the block's return pins against the pin rating; the PA flange temperature after a 10 minute key-down at 30 W into a load. Then the V1 checks (`../v1/BUILD.md` section 8) for the radios, the modem, the display and the demo.

Removal for maintenance (`docs/ASSEMBLY.md` section 7): lid, the ten plate screws, the ribbon, the headset and PA leads, the face out, then lift the A22 + D8 + B16 stack straight up. Nothing has to be unscrewed at the radio side: the eleven RF joints and the power contacts part as the stack rises, and the rods through the dock strip line them up again on refit.

## 9. Known gaps of this generation

- Nothing here has been built and no board has been fabricated. Report what does not fit on MESHSAT-830.
- The sealed face (the plate with C7 under it) is a construction, not a test result: the flood, hose, rain and dust procedure of appendix 32.34 has not been run on any face; the whole-kit MIL-STD-810 and 461 programme of `docs/TEST-PLAN.md` has not started.
- The pack is a built 4S smart pack in the east pocket by the ruling of 7 September (appendix 32.62); its board P1 and its enclosure are generated, the cell block welding and the gauge's golden image are bench work, and the second pack in the west pocket is a copy when wanted.
- Part picks owed: the toggle safety covers, the U-174/U jacks' drawing, the camera module, the sealed RJ45 and USB-C wall parts, the fans, the tablet, the 2 m antenna (the laptop research list of appendix 32.49).
- The QMX in the lid tray is a session decision under the owner's requirement for an HF unit inside the case (appendix 32.60 item 5, veto open); the tablet bracket is not drawn.
- The PA's thermal path (the flange on the plate) is an estimate until a module is keyed on a plate; the 30 W stage, the T/R relay and the LPF of D8 have not been measured.
- The three-module fabric (PCIe switches, USB 3 hubs, the HDMI and Ethernet switches) is generated from the makers' reference designs and has not been powered; the module's boot from its eMMC through this carrier, the display switch and the HAL sharing are bench items.
