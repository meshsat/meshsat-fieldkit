# Building a V2 kit from this repository

**Status (27 September 2026): the build guide of the 7 September generation, kept as history and as the one end-to-end
account of how a kit goes together. Do not order boards from it.** No board of the set is ready for layout
(`docs/CURRENT-EVIDENCE.md`: "Foundations incomplete; 0 boards ready for layout; 0 physically verified"); every
deliverable folder predates the circuit corrections of 26 September 2026 (`faf8c981`, `458b2873`, `d90f30e4`) and
every later one, which are in the schematics only; and the order folder `release/revA/order/` is rebuilt and quarantined, with nothing
ordered from it (owner decision 41 of 25 September 2026). The lines of this page that contradicted the current
baseline are corrected in place and marked "(corrected 27 September 2026)". Where this page and `docs/CASE-MARGINS.md`
section 4, `docs/PANEL.md`, `docs/CONOPS.md` or `docs/V2-SPEC.md` still differ, those documents govern. Keeping the
page as history with its stale lines corrected, rather than withdrawing it, is the session's choice SC-15 under the
owner's standing rule of 26 September 2026: it is the only assembly narrative in the tree, and the head above keeps a
reader from ordering from it. Reverse by withdrawing the page or its section 1.

The MESHSAT-830 generation of the carrier set (7 Sep 2026) has been designed and generated; no board of it has been fabricated and no kit has been built. This page was written as the path from an empty JLCPCB cart to an assembled kit, in order, pointing at the documents that carry the detail. Read it together with `docs/ASSEMBLY.md` (fasteners, build order, the pack, leads, coating, labels, removal, bench checks, bench-fit lists), `docs/PANEL.md` (the panel controller contract), `docs/V2-SPEC.md` (the device set and the budgets) and `docs/TEST-PLAN.md` (the qualification plan). The design record with every number and every ruling is `docs/MESHSAT-709-geometry-appendix.md`; sections 32.49 to 32.61 are this generation.

MeshSat is a prototype. Expect changes after the first build.

## 7. Software

Three Compute Module 5: flash each eMMC with `rpiboot` through B's `J_FLASH` with the `J_RPIBOOT` jumper on the slot being flashed (the B schematic names the strap per slot), Raspberry Pi OS with the carrier's overlays, then k3s across the three (one server, two agents, replicated storage on the NVMe drives) and the HAL (MESHSAT-801) that shares the USB devices cluster-wide. The kit has no radio on a module GPIO except the LoRa module on slot 3's SPI header (`meshtasticd` on `spidev0.1` with BUSY, DIO1 and reset on GPIO 23, 24 and 26): the LG290P, the two E72, the panel controller, the sensor controller, D (a USB audio and control set), the RockBLOCK, the LimeSDR, the QMX and the camera are USB devices on the per-slot hubs; the 5G module's data runs on slot 2's PCIe lane and its management link is USB, and the two WiFi link cards are PCIe cards on slots 1 and 3 (corrected 27 September 2026). Ethernet ties the three modules through the KSZ9897 switch to the wall RJ45. The Xenarc is an HDMI monitor with USB touch behind the three-input display switch that the panel controller selects; the kiosk runs on whichever module owns the display (`docs/PANEL.md` section 5).

- Panel: `docs/PANEL.md` is the contract of the panel controller firmware (MESHSAT-837) and the bridge protocol over USB. Corrected 27 September 2026: until it lands, MAIN PWR and the EMCON gates act in hardware, the indicator rail `LED_RAIL` stays dark, and the TX lamp with it, because C's high-side switch feeds that rail only while the firmware drives `PANEL_PWM` (`docs/PANEL.md` section 3, GPIO 8; `docs/feasibility/EMCON.md` section 0), no compute module runs (the slot enables stay low), and ZEROIZE does nothing, because the erase is the panel controller's firmware and provisioning behind a hardware toggle input (owner ruling D-03; `docs/PANEL.md` sections 6 and 7).
- Sensors: the sensor controller on E (MESHSAT-838) reports the climate, seal, water, gas, motion, lightning, Geiger and outside-pod readings over USB; the alarm rules are the bridge's.

## 8. Bring-up checks

`docs/TEST-PLAN.md` is the qualification plan; the first-power checks from `docs/ASSEMBLY.md` section 8: the dock strip gives its 20 V bus with 9, 12, 24 and 36 V in, survives reverse polarity for 10 s and blows its fuse on a bolted short; each slot rail comes up to 5.1 V under 3 A; the lamp test lights all 17 LEDs, BLACKOUT kills the rail, the EMCON cover closed keeps the SA868 and the PA in receive while the bridge asserts PTT; the shore inhibit drops and restores the front end and the kit keeps running from the pack (record the current, it sets the survival time); at full load measure the current on the block's return pins against the pin rating; the PA flange temperature after a key-down at 30 W into a load (corrected 27 September 2026: a PA key-down is bounded at 60 s, PROVISIONAL, session choice SC-10, and on the design record's own figure the flange passes the maker's 90 C reliability figure within that time from a +50 C plate, so the flange is watched with a thermocouple, `docs/feasibility/POWER-THERMAL.md` finding PWR-F15). Then the V1 checks (`../v1/BUILD.md` section 8) for the radios, the modem, the display and the demo.

Removal for maintenance (`docs/ASSEMBLY.md` section 7): lid, the ten plate screws, the ribbon, the headset and PA leads, the face out, then lift the A + D + B stack straight up.
