# Handover H1.1: the usability findings of H1, one by one

MESHSAT-1357, written 27 September 2026 by the H1.1 editor. Prototype design: no V2 board has been fabricated,
ordered, assembled, powered or measured. **H1.1 is a partial handover like H1: no layer is COMPLETE.**

**What was checked.** A fresh checker, an AI session given only the H1 ZIP, the public repository and a KiCad 9.0.9
host, tried to use H1 as an outside engineer would, and reported two BLOCKING and thirteen MINOR findings. It judged
usability, not engineering: it passed no judgement on any circuit, rating or decision. It called H1 a usable PARTIAL
handover. It reproduced the representative calculation, the six-board regeneration (PARITY on all six), the exports
and the test suite exactly as REGENERATE.md states them. Its findings are below in its own order. B1 and B2 are its
two BLOCKING findings and M1 to M13 its thirteen MINOR ones, each with the checker's location, how H1.1 answers it and
the commit that carries the answer.

**Commits.** `de59686e` (the H1.1 edits), `74c2df14` (the packer's `--zip-only` mode), `0098c0fd` (the candidates
README's export times and document origins) and the **build commit**: the commit that files this page, the one
`SOURCE.txt` in the H1.1 ZIP names on its `commit:` line. The build commit rewrites REGENERATE section 9 and adds the
remaining pointers named below. The H1 folder and `H1.zip` are unchanged.

**After H1.1 (added in H2, 27 September 2026).** B2's driver landed as `v2/ecad/tools/retake_schematic_phase.py`
(`e5fde2ed`) and ran as the consolidated re-take (`8ea7867e`): REGENERATE.md section 9 gives its commands, its run
and the per-board counts, and H2 carries both. It runs in a clean git clone, not in an extraction, because it refuses
a board whose inputs are not tracked and unmodified. M13's Q-B-ESC-1 part stays open (START-HERE section 7). The rows
below are H1.1's answers as written.

**Status words.** ANSWERED means the finding's problem no longer holds in H1.1. PARTLY means part of it still holds;
