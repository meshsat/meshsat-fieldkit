# Handover H2: the usability findings of H2, one by one

MESHSAT-1357, written 27 September 2026 by the H2 minor-findings editor, on branch `fnd/h2m` from `ef144760`.
Prototype design: no V2 board has been fabricated, ordered, assembled, powered or measured. **H2 is a partial handover:
layers 1 and 2 are COMPLETE, layers 3 to 9 are IN_PROGRESS; nothing here changes a layer's status.** Since H2,
layer 3 is COMPLETE (commit `24e7bf5a`, on its own records, `LAYER-STATUS.md`), and handover H3 is the first snapshot
that carries this page.

**What was checked.** A fresh checker, an AI session given only the H2 ZIP, the public repository and a rented KiCad
9.0.9 host, tried to use H2 as an outside engineer would. It judged usability, not engineering: it passed no judgement
on any circuit, rating or decision, and it is not a qualified review. It called H2 a usable partial handover, answered
the owner's six questions from the bundled pages, and reproduced the energy chain (FAIL from the ZIP alone, PASS of 98
once Mill-Max's page 28 is restored), the byte-identical power and case scripts, board P's regeneration, PARITY on all
six boards, the exports, the suite's 20 failures with their stated causes, section 1a's 29 fetches, and (on a
repository it built itself) board P's re-take. It found **no blocking defect and fourteen minor ones**. They are below
in its order, M1 to M14, each with the checker's location, how it is answered and the commit that carries the answer.
The H2 snapshot (`v2/release/handover/H2.zip`, its sha256 and manifest) is not touched: these answers are in the
editable pages, which the next snapshot carries.
