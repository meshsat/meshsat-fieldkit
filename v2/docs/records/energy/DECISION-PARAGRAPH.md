# Decision sheet: the 72-hour relay mission on battery and solar (M1)

Stream energy, MESHSAT-1357, 28 September 2026. A prototype design: nothing built or measured. AI analysis; the
figures are in `ENERGY-RECONCILIATION.md`.

The kit cannot run the 72-hour relay mission on its one battery and a solar panel as written. At its normal 43
watts a September night needs about 500 watt-hours; the aged battery holds about 108, so the kit stops within
eight hours, and no panel size fixes a night. Your choice: (1) accept that the prototype does not meet the mission
on battery and solar alone, with an external 12-volt source through the night; or (2) change three things: a
16-watt night mode keeping LoRa, Iridium, APRS and GNSS, a second battery (place to be checked), and a 200-watt
panel with the solar fuse and plug re-rated. Option 2 meets September and June, not December. I recommend 2.
