# L4-E9: the downstream register of the selected power architecture (MESHSAT-1357, 1 October 2026; round 2, 2 October 2026)

Prototype design: nothing here is applied, built or measured. Every implementation change, layout constraint, test and piece of
evidence that the selected architecture (A1 under D-06, with the Layer 4 decisions L4-E4 to L4-E9) hands downstream, each with
one named owner, its acceptance criterion, its state and its step in the release order below. `l4e9_power_path.py` reads this
table and refuses a row without a named owner or an acceptance (out 10); `test_l4e9.py` holds the same and checks that every
draft apply script of L4-E4 to L4-E8 is here.

**Two categories, kept apart (round 2).** (a) This register: downstream implementation and verification tasks. An owner and an
acceptance criterion close the ASSIGNMENT, not the item: each stays DRAFTED, MISSING DRAFT, PENDING or OWED until its acceptance
is met. (b) The unresolved choices that could overturn the architecture, U-01 (FEA-008's cell, L4-E10), U-02 (MESHSAT-1478, the
inside air against the +70 C parts), U-03 (O-1, the panel's supported open circuit) and U-04 (source-only and dead-pack
operation, the focused check's B4): they are NOT register items, no owner
closes them, and each keeps the closure gate's criteria 1 and 5 CONDITIONAL until its evidence lands
(`L4-POWER-ARCHITECTURE.md` section 7, `l4e9_power_path.out` section 9). Items here that exist only under a choice's outcome say
so in their Order column ("U-01").

**Owners** (by layer and role): Layer 4 coordinator (release records and assignment), Layer 5 interfaces, Layer 6 components,
Layer 7 mechanical, Layer 8 board A / board B / board E / board P generator owner, Layer 9 pre-layout analysis, prototype bench,
firmware owner, TEST-PLAN owner. **States:** DRAFTED (an apply script or a text draft exists, never applied), MISSING DRAFT (the
change is decided and no draft exists), PENDING (waits on another task's landing; none since L4-E7R was accepted), OWED (work
that is not a draft: evidence, analysis, layout, a test).

## The release order

1. **Release records.** L4-E4's `RELEASE.md` names accepted checks of L4-E4 and of the decisions it rests on (L4-E5, L4-E6 and
   L4-E8, all accepted); L4-E8's names `checks/check-l4e8-3.md`; L4-E7's names L4-E7R's accepted check 4; this record's waits on
   its own check. The missing drafts of step 2 are written and checked before any circuit round starts.
2. **Missing drafts written:** U3's ILIM_HIZ network (R-03), the 45 mOhm lcsc line (R-08), the board A declarations (R-10),
   R10 and C26/C27 with TRK_OUT's declaration (R-13 to R-15), the 0997's energy chain and derating entries (R-95, R-96).
3. **Board A, one circuit round and one regeneration**, in this order inside it: (a) R12 12 mOhm and C147 330 pF (L4-E6)
   **together with** the ILIM_HIZ network (L4-E5's H3): L4-E6 forbids R12 before the line; (b) R11 8 mOhm (L4-E4), never before
   (a); (c) the six ballasts and Cc2 3.3 nF (L4-E8), which refuse until R12 is in; (d) R138 5 mOhm (L4-E4, independent);
   (e) U17's sense (R-06); (f) the declarations and texts (R-09, R-10); (g) the regeneration on the box, its gates and evidence
   (R-11).
4. **Board E, one circuit round and one regeneration:** (a) Q1 to the CSD19532Q5B (this record) and E-F1's input capacitor, no
   later than (b); (b) R10 232 k with C26 and C27 re-rated (L4-E5), only in the same release as board A's H3 line;
   (c) U5's grade, the hold's parts and the input limit (L4-E7); (d) L4-E7R's backstop and corrected solar entry with its CS101
   correction (R-21, R-98, drafted; L4-E7R accepted); (e) F1 to the 0997010.WXN (R-18) with its configuration (R-95, R-96) and
   the hot-swap settings R22, R23 at 0.1 % and R24 22k (R-94, R-116); (f) the regeneration, its gates and evidence.
5. **Firmware:** FW-A16 restated (IIN_HOST a constant 4.70 A) only on a board A carrying the H3 line; on the drawn board the
   derated 4.00 A of r11dep stands. FW-A09 recalibrated to U17's new shunt R227 (R-27).
6. **The records re-issued** on the regenerated netlists (each of r11dep, L4-E4 to L4-E9 and the replay's section 11 refuses by
   design once its netlist moves).
7. **The bench rows** on the built prototype. V-A07 decides R11: 8 mOhm, or 7 mOhm, which changes R11's value only (R12, the
   bank and Cc2 serve both).
