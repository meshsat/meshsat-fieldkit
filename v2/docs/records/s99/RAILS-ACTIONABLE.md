Notes.

- Every row's "measurement" is the prototype obligation of the last column, kept separate from the desk closure so that
  a desk PASS is never read as the bench result (the owner's instruction, item 4).
- The per-rail loop minima quoted (7.06 A) are the fitted 6 mOhm shunt's with 1 percent and a assumed 50 K hot shunt; S2 and
  DEV share that stage design.
- The five PT rows are proposals for TEST-PLAN.md's owner; they name the measurand, the load state and what the
  reading decides, in the plan's own form (purpose, method, pass line, record).

The 60 s plateau used for arithmetic is a stated coincidence assumption. No datasheet onset bound or
closed-loop time constant is established; waveform and charge measurements decide transient behaviour.
