Subject: LT8705AIUHF: guaranteed limits for the IMON_IN input-current regulation (EA2 gain, VC dependence, line regulation,
A7 transfer error)

We use the LT8705AIUHF#PBF to hold a solar panel's input power under 100 W by its input-current limit (data sheet 8705af,
https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/8705af.pdf, "Current Limiting" on page 31 and
Figure 11). We size the limit from the Electrical Characteristics, and three quantities that set the regulated input
current are printed only as typical values, only at 25 C, or only at one test point. We ask for guaranteed limits, or a statement of what your
production test covers, for each:

1. The IMON_IN error amplifier EA2 (page 5: voltage gain 130 V/V and gm 185 umho, typical only). The IMON_IN regulation
   voltage (page 4: 1.187 / 1.208 / 1.229 V over the full temperature range) is printed at VC = 1.2 V. In input-current
   regulation VC settles where the power stage needs it. What is the guaranteed minimum EA2 voltage gain over -40 to
   125 C junction for the I grade, or, equivalently, the guaranteed range of the IMON_IN regulation voltage over the VC
   operating range? What is the VC operating range in regulation (minimum and the clamp), as opposed to the absolute
   maximum rating of -0.3 to 2.2 V on page 2?

2. The line regulation of the IMON_IN and IMON_OUT error amplifier reference (page 4: 0.002 / 0.005 %/V, VIN = 12 V to
   80 V, not switching, at 25 C). What is its limit while the converter is switching, and over -40 to 125 C junction,
   for VIN from 12 V to 25 V?

3. Do the full-range limits of the IMON_IN regulation voltage on page 4 already include the reference's temperature
   drift at VIN = 12 V, and do they hold with CIMON_IN filtering a discontinuous input current as page 31 describes?

4. The CSPIN/CSNIN to IMON_IN amplifier A7 (page 5: gm 0.95 / 1 / 1.05 mmho at 25 C and 0.94 / 1.06 mmho over the full
   range for the E and I grades, each at VCSPIN - VCSNIN = 50 mV and VCSPIN = 5.025 V). Our circuit runs CSPIN and CSNIN
   at the panel voltage, 16 V to 25 V common mode, with up to about 58 mV across the sense resistor, while the converter
   switches. What transfer error (gain and offset, IMON_IN current against the differential voltage) do you guarantee
   over 1.5 V to 80 V common mode (page 5's operating range), 0 to 100 mV differential, -40 to 125 C junction and while
   switching? If the page 5 limits apply only at the test point, what limits apply at 16 V to 25 V common mode?

5. The SWEN pin (added 1 October 2026, task L4-E7R; revised 2 October 2026). Under the input-current limit a fixed trip
   holds SWEN (pin 36 of the QFN) low through a supervisor's open-drain output; SWEN is fed from LDO33 through 8.06 kOhm
   with 6.04 kOhm to ground, so it is held low with no supply and cannot reach its threshold below about 2.66 V on LDO33.
   Page 12 describes SWEN as a logic input ("Tie high to enable switching. Ground to disable switching. Don't float this
   pin") and page 4 prints its rising threshold (1.156 / 1.206 / 1.256 V over the full range) and a typical 22 mV
   hysteresis; we find no row for its input current. What input current do you guarantee at SWEN over 0 V to 3.4 V and
   -40 to 125 C junction (our arrangement holds while it is under about 107 uA sourced and 31 uA sunk)? What is the
   guaranteed least falling threshold? With SWEN driven below its threshold, what delay to the last switching edge do you
   guarantee, and does every return through the initialize state of Figure 2 (page 15) restart with SS pulled low?

For each item we ask for a limit Analog Devices warrants, and the conditions under which it applies. Characterization
over production lots is welcome as supporting evidence, but on its own we cannot treat it as a production guarantee;
typical curves are not sufficient for our purpose. Thank you.
