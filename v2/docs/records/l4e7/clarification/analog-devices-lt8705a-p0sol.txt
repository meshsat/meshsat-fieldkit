Subject: LT8705AIUHF: the input-current regulation driven by an external current-output amplifier into IMON_IN, with CSPIN
and CSNIN tied to VIN

We use the LT8705AIUHF#PBF in a solar input stage (data sheet 8705af,
https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/8705af.pdf). We are considering not using the on-chip
input current sense: CSPIN and CSNIN tied directly to VIN at the pins, as pages 12, 29 and 31 ask when the input current
sensing is not in use, and the input-current regulation fed instead by an external current-output monitor (a TI INA169,
1 mA/V into the same RIMON_IN of about 34 kOhm and CIMON_IN of 100 nF) whose output is connected to IMON_IN. EA2 would then
regulate the IMON_IN voltage to 1.208 V (typical) exactly as page 31 and Figure 11 describe, with the current coming from
the external monitor rather than from A7. Page 31 also says to tie IMON_IN to ground when the input current sensing is not
in use; in our arrangement the input current regulation is in use, only A7's inputs are not.

8. (a) Is this use of IMON_IN supported, and do the IMON_IN regulation voltage (page 4: 1.187 / 1.208 / 1.229 V at
   VC = 1.2 V over the full temperature range) and the IMON_IN overvoltage threshold (page 5: 1.55 / 1.61 / 1.67 V) hold
   when the pin's current comes from an external source in parallel with A7's output?
   (b) With CSPIN and CSNIN on the same node (zero differential), what current does A7 source into IMON_IN at most over
   -40 to 125 C junction for the I grade, at 16 V to 25 V common mode? Page 31 states that no current flows for a negative
   differential; we find no figure for zero. Can A7's output ever sink current from the IMON_IN node?
   (c) Does a current source driving IMON_IN while the converter is disabled (SWEN low, or SHDN low, or in a fault cycle
   after IMON_IN exceeded its overvoltage threshold) affect the soft-start or the fault timing? Is there a limit on the
   capacitance or the source impedance at IMON_IN beyond the 0.1 uF to 1 uF page 31 gives for CIMON_IN?

For each item we ask for a limit Analog Devices warrants and the conditions under which it applies. Typical curves are
welcome as supporting evidence, but on their own we cannot treat them as a guarantee. Thank you.
