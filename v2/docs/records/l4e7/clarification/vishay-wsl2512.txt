Subject: WSL2512R0700FEA: pulse capability for a 10/1000 us surge current at up to 62 C

We use five WSL2512R0700FEA (data sheet Document Number 30100, Revision 23-Nov-2023) in parallel as a current-sense bank
on a solar converter's input, about 0.5 A each in normal operation, the air at -20 C to about +62 C. The data sheet's
performance table prints the short time overload test limit as +-(0.5 % + 0.0005 Ohm) and refers to the online
calculator for short time overload performance and pulse capability; its pulse capability chart is marked as illustrative.

1. Each part may carry a 10/1000 us surge (the double exponential surge protection parts are rated at, 10 us front,
   1000 us to half value) of up to 6.7 A peak, about 3.5 mJ in the part, once, at an ambient of up to 62 C. Do you
   warrant that the part stays inside the short time overload test limit after such a pulse, and inside its ratings
   during it?
2. If the online calculator is your statement of pulse capability, which of its results do you warrant, and under which
   mounting conditions (pad size, copper weight)?

We ask for a limit Vishay warrants and the conditions under which it applies. Characterization over production lots is
welcome as supporting evidence, but on its own we cannot treat it as a production guarantee. The answer bounds the
input's margin beyond the surge we derived for it, which this pulse exceeds; it is the clamp's own rating, not the
derived surge. Thank you.
