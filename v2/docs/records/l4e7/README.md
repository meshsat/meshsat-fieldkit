# l4e7: real component settings for board E's solar stage (layer 4 task L4-E7, MESHSAT-1357)

Prototype design, desk arithmetic. This folder takes implementable choice 3, REAL COMPONENT SETTINGS, of
`../l4e/L4-ENERGY-ARCHITECTURE.md` (finding O-2 and review L4-R01): the LT8705A's grade, RSENSE1 and RIMON_IN of its
input-current limit and R8 and R9 of its input-voltage hold, as catalogue parts with their makers' tolerance and TCR, and
section 11's 100 W corner check re-run on the values they achieve, at both temperature ends. Since L4-E7R (the owner's
instruction of 1 October 2026), `L4E7-CONTROL-DECISION.md` decides how REQ-016's 100 W is held: the LT8705A's limit
alone is not shown on warranted manufacturer limits, so a hardware trip (a WSL2512 sense bank, an INA169, a TPS3701 and a
TPS3808 holding SWEN low, SWEN off by default) sits under it, its bound on printed limits and two named assumptions carried
past their physical meaning, judged in three checks (normal operation; startup, shutdown and the fault response; the parts'
ratings during the disturbances derived from the approved test plan); third round, after `checks/astra-check-l4e7r-2.md`
and the owner's decision process of 2 October 2026, with the CS101 correction after `checks/check-l4e7r-3.md` (the bulk
ahead of the bank, a filter on the trip's input and the regulation one step lower, so the backstop holds through M2), and
the panel lead's surge and sustained over-voltage derived for REQ-016 (the coordinator's surge round of 2 October 2026, the
findings ledger's item 1), and the solar-fault remedies (the owner's amendment of 2 October 2026, item 3: an over-voltage
cut-off for a 36 V source on the port and a return switch for a reversed panel, drafted), with the guard already on bounded
after the consolidation review's B6 and, in round 2, the external review's L4-F01: the margin decided first (U5 within
+-0.240 V, the timestep's error added), Samsung parts whose DC-bias curves bound every ceramic bank on its own, the envelope
from a 0.30 uH source loop; round 2's 3.30 uH loop, WITHDRAWN as a passing floor in round 5 (the recheck: a connector fault
credits no lead resistance, and the pins' complete budget with RSENSE1's inductance is outside +-0.240 V in both polarities),
is kept only as the reference loop; no approach within the makers' printed rules removes the dependency, and the engineer's
row B6-ENG-1 carries the complete stage question. Round 3 (the one design-convergence attempt) worked
B6-ENG-1's route 3, the sense moved off the stage's input capacitance, to the circuit: no split of the input ceramics holds
both the guard-on transient and the sense's printed operating range in operation, result (ii); it found the as-drafted sense
outside that operating range at the 25 V corner (B6-ENG-2) and RSENSE1's unprinted inductance a condition of B6's floor.
Round 4 answered the Layer 6 author's two findings on the drafts: R97 at 0.1 % (L6P-F04) and PV_F's basis stated against the
TPS4811-Q1's recommended operating 80 V row, exceeded at the floor and OPEN (L6P-F10).
Round 5 answered the collaborator's recheck: the floor claim withdrawn, the sense model corrected (no monitor current for a
negative differential; the average reads high, not low), the "no damage" consequence withdrawn, R96 at 0.1 %.
Round 6 (4 October 2026) answered Layer 8's record l8p, finding L8P-F01: board E's drafts composed in L4-E9's change-list
order stopped the generator at its G14 table, because the backstop declared C66, C67 and C68 against U18's V+, U19's VDD and
U20's VDD with no decoupling class. The backstop draft now gives each class D (a capacitor a maker ties to a supply pin),
with its maker's 0.1 uF clause; the composed generator runs past the table (it stops next on L4-E11's aux draft, L8P-F03,
that record's). The results cache's KEY now holds the drafts compute() reads back, so a changed draft moves it.
