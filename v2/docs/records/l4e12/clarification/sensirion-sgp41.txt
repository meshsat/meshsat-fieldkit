Subject: SGP41: the duration the short-term storage range covers

The SGP41 data sheet version 1.0 (December 2021), Table 5, gives a short-term storage temperature range of -40 to +70 C
("Short-term storage refers to temporary conditions during, e.g., transport") and an operating range of -20 to +55 C.
Could you confirm:

1. The duration the short-term storage range covers.
2. Whether an unpowered SGP41 (its supply removed and its I2C pins not driven) may sit at up to +70 C for 4 hours, and at
   up to +70 C for about 6 hours a day on 10 consecutive days, at a non-condensing humidity, and then meet its gas
   specification after its conditioning once back inside its operating range.

Thank you.
