Subject: SGP41: storage and operating conditions outside Table 4

The SGP41 data sheet version 1.0 (December 2021), section 2.3, ties the gas sensing specifications of Table 1 to storage and
operation under the recommended conditions of Table 4 (operation -10 to +50 C, storage 5 to 30 C). Table 5 gives a
short-term storage temperature range of -40 to +70 C ("Short-term storage refers to temporary conditions during, e.g.,
transport") and an operating range of -20 to +55 C as absolute ratings. Could you confirm:

1. The duration the short-term storage range covers.
2. Whether an unpowered SGP41 (its supply removed and its I2C pins not driven) may sit at up to +70 C for 4 hours, and at
   up to +70 C for about 6 hours a day on 10 consecutive days, at a non-condensing humidity, and then meet its gas
   specification after its conditioning once back inside its recommended conditions.
3. Whether the gas specification holds, or by how much it changes, after an unpowered SGP41 is stored at -20 to +45 C for up
   to three months, or at -20 to +25 C for up to a year, and then conditioned and operated inside Table 4's conditions.
4. What gas specification applies while the SGP41 is operated between +50 and +55 C, and whether operation there leaves a
   lasting change once it is back under +50 C.

Thank you.
