# Draft decision sheet: the lid pack of Option A(i) (integrator's to send)

**Prototype design, AI review; desk results.**

**Question.** Option A(i) needs 48 cells in the lid. They fit only if one lid function you approved on 6 September 2026
leaves the lid. Which one?

**Why.** The closed lid leaves 44.39 mm over the face. The guarded toggles and the QMX HF set take ceiling; a tablet under the
pack stops a second cell layer over its band. With both kept the lid holds 39 cells.

**Options.**

1. **Keep HF; the tablet bracket leaves the lid.** 56 places (48 used). The tablet works from outside the case; REQ-011's
   bracket is not met.
2. **Keep an 8 inch tablet bracket; HF leaves the lid.** 61 places (48 used). HF inside the kit is lost: the base has no room.
3. **Keep both; 4S9P in the lid, 4S15P in all.** Not Option A(i). On the two-pack energy model M1 is not met with the lid at
   its 13.23 C basis (400, 650 or 1000 Wp), nor at 400 Wp with the lid at +20 C; met only at +20 C with 650 Wp or more.

**Common to 1 and 2.** The lid weighs about 4.9 kg. A lid stay at 100 degrees (about EUR 10) keeps the open case standing on
ground sloping under 6 degrees toward the hinge; a backward push of about 10 N on the open lid can tip it. The lid pack's lead
needs a sealed crossing of the face plate, not yet designed. D-01's deferral of HF, the bracket and a second pack is reopened.

**Session's engineering view.** Option 1: it keeps a radio bearer inside the kit.
