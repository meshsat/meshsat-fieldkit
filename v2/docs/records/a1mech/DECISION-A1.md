# Draft decision sheet for the owner: the lid pack of Option A(i) (at most 300 words; the integrator's to send)

**Prototype design, AI review. Nothing built or bought; these are desk results at the worst of Peli's figures.**

**Question.** Option A(i) needs 48 cells in the lid. The lid holds them only if one of the two lid functions you approved on
6 September 2026 leaves the lid. Which one?

**Why.** Between the face and the closed lid there are 44.39 mm at the worst. The guarded toggles (28 mm) and the QMX HF set
take their parts of the ceiling; a tablet under the pack stops a second cell layer over its band. With both kept the lid
holds 35 cells: 4S8P, and 4S14P in all, under the 4S15P that M1 needs on the energy model.

**Options.**

1. **Keep HF; the tablet bracket leaves the lid.** 56 cell places (48 used). The tablet works from outside the case on the
   kit's WiFi and USB-C outlet. REQ-011's bracket is not met.
2. **Keep an 8 inch tablet bracket; the QMX HF set leaves the lid.** 58 places (48 used). HF inside the kit is lost: the base
   has no room for it.
3. **Keep both; a 4S8P lid pack.** Not Option A(i); M1 not met on the model.

**Common to 1 and 2.** The lid grows from about 1.4 kg to 4.9 kg. A lid stay at 100 degrees (about EUR 10) keeps the open case
from tipping backwards; Peli states no stop angle. The lead to the lid pack needs a sealed crossing of the face plate, not yet
designed (the same open item as the HF leads). D-01, which defers HF, the tablet bracket and a second pack, is reopened.

**Session's engineering view.** Option 1: it keeps a radio bearer inside the kit.
