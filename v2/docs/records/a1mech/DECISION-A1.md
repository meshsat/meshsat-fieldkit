# Draft decision sheet: the lid pack of Option A(i)

**Prototype design, AI review; desk results.**

**Question.** Option A(i) needs 48 lid cells. They fit only if one lid function you approved on 6 September leaves. Which?

**Why.** The closed lid leaves 44.39 mm over the face; the toggles, the QMX set and a tablet take the room. With both kept the
lid holds 39 cells.

**Options.**

1. **Keep HF; the tablet bracket leaves the lid.** 56 places (48 used); REQ-011's bracket is not met. Open, the case stands
   on ground sloping under about 3 degrees toward the hinge (9.5 if Peli's feet stand at the bottom's edge); a 12 N push on the open lid can tip it.
2. **Keep an 8 inch tablet bracket; HF leaves the lid.** 61 places (48 used); HF inside the kit is lost. Open, level ground
   only (7.0 degrees if the feet stand at the edge); a 6 N push on the tablet's far edge can tip it.
3. **Keep both; 4S9P in the lid, 4S15P in all.** Not Option A(i). On the two-pack energy model M1 is not met with the lid at
   its 13.23 C basis (400, 650 or 1000 Wp), nor at 400 Wp with the lid at +20 C; met only at +20 C with 650 Wp or more.

**Common to 1 and 2.** The lid weighs about 5 kg and needs a stay at 100 degrees (about EUR 10); the limits above hold with it
until the feet are measured. The lead to the lid needs a sealed face-plate
crossing, not yet designed. D-01's deferral of HF, the bracket and a second pack is reopened.

**Session's view.** Option 1: it keeps a radio bearer inside.
