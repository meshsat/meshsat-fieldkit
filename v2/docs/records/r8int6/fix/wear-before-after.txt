== THE READING (by hand, each part that moved read against its netlist entry)

ACCEPTANCE ASKED: the wear set of every board unchanged, except that U116, U216 and U316 of board B leave it.
MEASURED: boards A, C, D, E and P are unchanged part for part (39, 9, 10, 8, 7). Board B goes from 44 to 38.
Six parts leave, no part enters. Three are the ones asked for; three more leave and are read below.

THE THREE THE TASK NAMES
  B U116, U216, U316   SN74LV1T08DBVR, a single two-input AND gate. Netlist footprint
                       Package_TO_SOT_SMD:SOT-23-5, five pins on nets. The wear word is "socket" in
                       "slot N's card-socket supply enable = EMCON AND PCIE_PWR_ENn": it names what the gate's
                       output switches. A soldered SOT-23-5 has no mating cycle and carries no mechanical load.
                       They were the three refusals of the box suite at a76a246e. No class is declared for them.

THE THREE THE TASK DOES NOT NAME
  B U101, U201, U301   Diodes PI7C9X2G404SL, a PCIe 2.0 packet switch. Netlist footprint
                       meshsat:LQFP-128_14x14mm_P0.4mm_EP6.0, 129 pins on nets (128 leads and the exposed pad);
                       v2/vendor/SOURCES.yaml gives the fitted part as PI7C9X2G404SLBFDEX, LQFP-128-EP 14x14.
                       Full value text, slot 1: "Diodes PI7C9X2G404SL PCIe 2.0 switch, slot S1: up = CM5 lane,
                       port 1 NVMe, port 2 card socket". The wear word is "socket" in the list of what the
                       switch's ports lead to. The part is a soldered integrated circuit.
                       WHY THEY MAY LEAVE: they were in the set only because of prose, the same defect as the
                       gates, met on 16 September and answered then by declaring a class for them. That class
                       says so itself (pcb_reliability.yaml, board b, "the PCIe switches' own sockets on the
                       carrier"): "these are soldered ICs and not connectors: they appear in this list because
                       the search finds their package names, and they carry no mating load at all", load "none".
                       The sockets those ports lead to are J_M2C1..3 and J_M2N1..3, which are in the wear set
                       before and after under the class "M.2 sockets" (count_expected 6, met).
                       WHAT IT LEAVES BEHIND: that class now covers no wear part. It raises no refusal (it has
                       no count_expected and its basis says why it has no cycle figure), but its basis sentence
                       "they appear in this list because the search finds their package names" is no longer
                       true of the tool. The list is not edited here; apply_drop_pcie_switch_class.py beside
                       this file makes the change for the integrator (tested on a copy outside the tree:
                       board B 6 to 5 classes, the set 26 to 25, a second run refused, the fixed tool on
                       the patched copy still 111 covered and 0 refusals). The integrator also owns the
                       pages that say "declares 25 classes" (pcb_rules_coverage.yaml REL-001 note,
                       PCB-GAP-REGISTER.md, PCB-GOLDEN-RULES.md), a figure of 16 September; the list holds
                       26 today. One correction to carry if the class is kept: its load says "a soldered
                       QFN" and the netlist and the fitted part say LQFP-128.

NO REAL CONNECTOR IS LOST. No part with a connector, fuse-holder, battery-holder or mounting-hole reference
(J*, F*, BT*, H*, W*) names its wear word only after the colon on any of the six boards, so the rule needed no
second form by prefix. The rule is one attempt, not two.

WHY THE SEPARATOR IS THE FIRST COLON OUTSIDE EVERY BRACKET, and not the first colon (decision, authority
SESSION): 343 of the 2630 values carry a colon followed by white space, and in 89 of them the first one stands
inside a bracket, for example board E D2 "SMCJ40A (bus clamp: 40 V standoff on the 9 to 36 V bus; ...)". Such a
colon belongs to a remark on the part. The two readings give the SAME wear set on all six boards today (column
"first" equals column "ident" on every row that is not excluded by its prefix), so the choice changes no
reading; it is taken because the identity it returns is never shorter than the plain split's, so where the two
ever differ the part is asked for rather than dropped, and a completeness gate must fail in that direction.
To reverse: replace the body of reliability.identity with a split at the first colon followed by white space;
t_a_colon_inside_a_bracket_does_not_end_the_identity is the test that then fails.

THE COVERED SUM the committed test asks to be at least 100: 114 before (with 3 refusals, so the test failed on
its first assertion and never reached the sum), 111 after with 0 refusals.
