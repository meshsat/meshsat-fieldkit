== Pull-up window (SDA; SCL identical)
   effective Rp: 1019 ohm at -5 % (sink check), 1080 nominal, 1138 at +5 % (rise check)
   Rp minimum for a 3 mA sink at VOL 0.4 V from 3.40 V: 1000 ohm (UM10204 7.1); the drawn 1019 ohm meets it
   pull-up current at 0.4 V: 2.94 mA worst (3.40 V over 1019 ohm), 2.69 mA nominal
   largest Cb for tr <= 300 ns: 311 pF (30-70 %, BQ25731, ATECC608B, ADS1115) and 269 pF (TPS23861's 0.8-2.3 V at 3.20 V) at the rise-check Rp; 328 and 303 pF at nominal
   Standard-mode alone (tr 1000 ns, 30-70 %) would allow 1037 pF; UM10204's Cb limit is 400 pF

== ONE SEGMENT, as drawn (trunk = the whole bus): fixed part and the copper it leaves
   SDA: fixed 300 pF bound / 176 pF nominal against 269 / 303 pF allowed: copper allowance -31 / 127 pF
       the committed layouts carry 1568 mm: 157 to 345 pF at 1.0 to 2.2 pF/cm; total 333 to 427 pF (nominal pins, low copper) up to 645 pF (bound pins, high copper)
   SCL: fixed 288 pF bound / 174 pF nominal against 269 / 303 pF allowed: copper allowance -19 / 129 pF
       the committed layouts carry 1452 mm: 145 to 319 pF at 1.0 to 2.2 pF/cm; total 319 to 407 pF (nominal pins, low copper) up to 607 pF (bound pins, high copper)
   RESULT: the fixed part alone uses 58 to 112 % of the rise-limited capacitance, and the laid copper exceeds what
   is left at every per-length figure in the range; the bus as one segment cannot meet the 300 ns rise its own
   targets require, and the drawn pull-ups cannot be made stronger (3 mA sinks). The bound includes failure.
