Subject: HL18650V wide-temperature 18650 cell: request for the signed product specification

Dear Topwell Power team,

We are evaluating your HL18650V (TWE0388) for a non-commercial prototype: a 4S3P pack of twelve cells inside a sealed field
case. Your product page lists discharge from -40 to 85 C, charge from -20 to 60 C and storage of -40 to 80 C within 30 days.
Before any purchase we need the maker's signed product specification. Could you send it, and confirm the following in it?

1. Storage: may a cell be held for 24 hours at +71 C, and separately for 24 hours at -33 C, and at what state of charge?
   What capacity recovery do you guarantee after each (the test method and the percentage)?
2. The discharge range of -40 to 85 C: is it the ambient air or the cell surface temperature? Does it include a cell at
   rest (neither charging nor discharging) at a high state of charge, for example ten days of cycles between +30 and +60 C
   at 95 % relative humidity?
3. Cycle life: the conditions behind "at least 500 cycles" (charge and discharge currents, end voltages, temperature).
4. The minimum capacity, the maximum dimensions with tolerances, and the maximum mass.
5. The UN 38.3 test summary and the IEC 62133 report for this model, and whether you are the cell's manufacturer.
6. The open-circuit voltage against state of charge at 25 C, so that a fuel gauge can be configured.
7. Charging by temperature: your page prints the coldest charge row as "-20 -10<T<=0 C: 0.1C to 4.1V". May a cell be charged
   between -20 and -10 C, and if so at what current and to what voltage? Is each charge row's temperature the cell surface or
   the ambient? What is the termination current at 4.2 V and at 4.1 V, and is there any limit on charging or holding a cell at
   4.2 V above 45 C?
8. Discharge current: the pulse current and its duration, and whether the 10 A continuous current is derated toward -40 C or
   toward +85 C.
9. Capacity in the cold: the discharge capacity at -20 C and at -40 C, at 0.2C and at 0.5C, to the end voltages your page
   states (2.5 V and 2.0 V), or the discharge curves by temperature.
10. Ageing: the capacity a cell keeps after the "at least 500 cycles" (the end-of-life criterion), and the self-discharge per
    month at 25 C and at 60 C.

Thank you. We will treat the document under the terms you state.
