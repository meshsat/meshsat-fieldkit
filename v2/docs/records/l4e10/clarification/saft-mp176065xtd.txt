Subject: MP 176065 xtd in a 4S1P pack: currents at temperature, storage dwell, and four further questions

Dear Saft team,

We are evaluating your MP 176065 xtd for a non-commercial prototype: a 4S1P pack of four cells inside a sealed field case
(prototype framing: nothing has been bought or built). Your datasheet (Doc. n 31109-2-0625, June 2025) states charge from
-30 to +85 C, discharge from -40 to +85 C and an allowable storage range of -40 to +85 C, and notes that the recommended
maximum discharge currents "Can vary depending on temperatures. Consult Saft". In our pack one cell carries the whole pack
current. Could you state the following?

1. Continuous discharge: may one cell deliver 10 A continuously at cell temperatures of -20, -10, +45, +60, +70 and +80 C,
   from full charge and from 30 % state of charge, down to 3.0 V under load? If not, the continuous current you recommend
   at each of those temperatures.
2. Pulse discharge: may one cell deliver 18 A for 60 seconds, and 20 A for 2 seconds (our pack protection's trip pulse), at
   the same temperatures and states of charge? The minimum rest you require between such pulses.
3. Charging below 0 C (your footnote 4): the current and voltage you permit between -30 and 0 C; and whether 3.0 A to 4.2 V
   at +45 C is within your recommendation.
4. Storage dwell and recovery: does the allowable range hold for 24 hours at +71 C and for 24 hours at -33 C at a 30 % state
   of charge, and what capacity recovery do you expect after each? The same for a rest at full charge through ten 24-hour
   cycles between +30 and +60 C at 95 % relative humidity.
5. Capacity: the minimum capacity at C/5 and +25 C to 2.5 V (the datasheet gives a typical 5.6 Ah), and the discharge
   curves by temperature (at about 3 A), down to -20 C.
6. Thickness: the maximum thickness of a sleeved cell over its life and at +85 C (the datasheet's 18.65 mm is at the
   beginning of life and full charge, "can increase with temperature and during battery life").
7. Charge termination: the end-of-charge current you recommend at 4.2 V.

Thank you. We will treat any document under the terms you state.
