Subject: MP 176065 xtd in a 4S1P pack: four clarifications

Dear Saft team,

We are evaluating your MP 176065 xtd for a non-commercial prototype: a 4S1P pack of four cells inside a sealed field case
(prototype framing: nothing has been bought or built). Your datasheet (Doc. n 31109-2-0625, June 2025) states charge from
-30 to +85 C, discharge from -40 to +85 C and an allowable storage range of -40 to +85 C. Could you confirm the following?

1. Pulse discharge: may one cell deliver 18 A for 60 seconds, at a cell temperature between -20 and +60 C? The datasheet
   gives 22 A pulses without a duration.
2. Storage: does the allowable range of -40 to +85 C hold for 24 hours at +71 C and for 24 hours at -33 C at a 30 % state
   of charge, and what capacity recovery do you expect after each?
3. Capacity: the minimum capacity at C/5 and +25 C to 2.5 V (the datasheet gives a typical 5.6 Ah), and the capacity at
   -20 C at about 3 A.
4. Thickness: the maximum thickness of a sleeved cell over its life and at +85 C (the datasheet's 18.65 mm is at the
   beginning of life and full charge, "can increase with temperature and during battery life").
