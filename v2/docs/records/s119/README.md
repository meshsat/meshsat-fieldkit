Stream s119, branch `fnd/s119` from set 12's tip `a1f8ec70` (`fnd/int13`), 29 September 2026. **Prototype design:
nothing is built, bought or measured. Every figure is the record's model on the September reference day, AI arithmetic
on the makers' figures; it is not a review by a person and not a measurement.** No requirement is changed: M1 and

owner decisions Q1 to Q4 of the EXECUTION-PLAN (the standing rule of 29 September 12:03) are not taken; every result is

**The core loss.** Coilcraft Document 804-1 (`v2/vendor/power/coilcraft-xal1010.pdf`, revised 02/25/26, page 1) prints
no core loss for the XAL1010 series: it says "Core and winding loss: See www.coilcraft.com/coreloss", an online
calculator this tree does not hold. So no number is put on it: it is EXCLUDED from both rows, and its sign is known: every
efficiency here is high by it, and every lowest store is high by what it would cost. What the record gives instead is the
room (`reconcile_s119.out` section 5): with U3 at 6.1 A and ratio C, U3 may fall to 0.931 (4S14P) or 0.916 (4S15P) at
every hour, and U3B to 0.845 or 0.810, before M1 is lost; a constant-watt loss weighs more in the day's weak hours, so the
watts printed there are an indication at the peak and not a bound on a constant core loss.

## Is the two-pack M1 result re-established for the circuit as now drawn?

**Partly, and not yet for the circuit as drafted.** Board A as now drawn (decision 57's FETs on U3, regenerated in set 12)
supports U3's 0.979, and with U3B carried at 0.961 both lid options that fit meet M1 on the model in the typical and the
adverse case (tablet out 93.7 and 80.6 Wh, QMX out 125.2 and 111.6 Wh); both functions kept still does not. But U3B is a
draft (TOPOLOGY.md 3b, not on any generator) and it still names CSD18510Q5B, which its 6 V REGN cannot drive and with
which it reads 0.802: with that U3B the adverse case is NOT MET for both lid options even with U3 as drawn. The result
holds for the circuit once U3B's draft takes the CSD17578Q5A four on its 800 kHz row (way (a), REGN 49.9 mA at the
makers' maxima against its 50 mA minimum limit: inside, with no margin) or the 400 kHz row (way (b), 0.974, REGN
32.6 mA). The registry script opens that as a SESSION item (S-121 if no other item takes the number first). The session's
recommendation to its owner: way (b), for REGN's margin and one inductor, one IADPT value, one compensation and one FET
pair shared by U3 and U3B; the energy chain keeps the lower 0.961 until it is drawn. Either way this is a model result;
nothing is measured, and REQ-072 stays FAIL.
