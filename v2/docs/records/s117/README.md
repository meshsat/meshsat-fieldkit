## 3. The choice: TI's 400 kHz row on the XAL1010 land

**L2 XAL1010-472ME (4.7 uH), R219 191 k 1 % 0603 on IADPT, C233 33 pF C0G, Table 9-5's 400 kHz compensation, PWM_FREQ at its
power-on 1b.** Ripple at G1 3.13 A nominal (27 % of IL), 4.60 A worst (39 %); at G4 2.28 A (33 %), inside TI's 20 to 40 %.

Why not the 800 kHz row on the same land (XAL1010-332ME, 169 k), which passes the inductor's ratings too (13.41 A worst,
47 % at G1):
- **Gate drive.** The drawn FETs (CSD18510Q5B, about 75 nC at 6 V from SLPS632 Figure 4, INFERRED; 58 nC at 4.5 V, table)
  need 2 x Qg x fS of REGN in buck mode: 93 to 120 mA at a typical 800 kHz against IREGN_LIM's 50 mA minimum, and even at
  400 kHz 60 mA with a 6 V drive (46 mA only with the 4.5 V table figure, a floor) and up to 77 mA (89 mA at 460 kHz) from
  the makers' maxima (`efficiency.out` section 5; corrected in the second issue, the check's m1). TI's own switching tests
  assume "MOSFET Qg = 4 nC" (8.5, pp.11 and 12), and TI's Figure 8-4 (p.19) loses about 0.96 W in all at 4 A, less than
  the drawn FETs' gate drive alone (1.24 W at 400 kHz): the curve describes much smaller FETs (the check's m2). Section 11
  replaces the FETs; with them the 800 kHz row still costs 0.6 to 1.4 W more at the model's peak, so this row stands.
- **Losses** (TI's Equations 6 to 22, pp.86 to 88; Qsw by TI's Equation 14, 49 nC, and by Qgd + Qgs - Qg(th), 34 nC; core
  loss not computed, Coilcraft publishes it only through its web calculator: INCONCLUSIVE): the 800 kHz row adds 6.6 to 8.6
  W at G1 and 4.4 to 5.6 W at G4. The stage (FETs and L2's copper) at 400 kHz: 7.5 to 9.5 W at G1 (118 W in), 4.7 to 5.8 W at
  G4 (100 W in); at 800 kHz 13.9 to 17.9 W and 9.0 to 11.2 W. REGN's own drop puts 0.88 W inside U3 at 400 kHz and 1.76 W at
  800 kHz (33 and 66 K on the JEDEC RthJA of 37.2 C/W, 8.4 p.9; ordering only, not U3's temperature).
- **Start-up.** 400 kHz is PWM_FREQ's power-on value, so with 191 k the resistor, the register and the compensation agree
  before any host write. The maker does not say whether the IADPT reading writes the register; with 191 k it does not
  matter, with 169 k it would until the host writes 0b.
- **Input limit.** Table 9-1 (p.26) has no 4.7 uH row, but with board A's 10 mOhm R16 and RSNS_RAC = 0b (FW-A01) the input
  limit is 6.35 A whatever the inductance (9.3.5 p.25, 9.6.22 p.80), so it does not bind here.
- C121's 10 nF is the CDIFF 10.2.2.2 asks for at 400 kHz, unchanged.

This is a SESSION decision (apply script `apply_decision_s117.py`, decision 56 on the tree it was checked against): no
reserved class names the files, no money, no claim, no residual risk a measurement here could remove, and one option stands
after the measurement. The way back (the 800 kHz row on the same land) is in its `reversed_by`.
