fix(v2): boards A and B draw their ten one-way clamps with a cathode pin, and all six boards are regenerated with parity [MESHSAT-1357]

The ten unidirectional suppressors of boards A and B were drawn on KiCad's Device:D_TVS. That is the bidirectional
symbol, and its pins A1/A2 name no cathode, so TRN-001 read FAIL on both boards (A: 4 symbol mismatches, B: 6). Each
part was already the right way round, with pad 1, the banded cathode, on its conductor.

They now go through kisch.tvs(), as boards D and P already do. The direction is read from the part number
(kisch.tvs_direction), the protected conductor and its return are named, and the call is recorded in the board's
intent under "clamps":
  A: D1 SMCJ18A VBAT, D2 SMCJ40A VIN_RAW, D3 SMBJ5.0A +3V3, D4 SMBJ18A PD_VBUS
  B: D1 SMBJ5.0A +5V_DEV, D2 SMBJ58A +54V_POE, D101/D201/D301 SMBJ6.0A +5V_S1..S3, D520 SMBJ5.0A +3V3_M2C2
Each call replaces its own line, so no line number moves in either generator.

kisch.py's comments now cite the Littelfuse SMBJ, Diodes DS19002, MDD SMBJ and Nexperia PESD12VL1BA sheets at their
filed v2/vendor paths, each sha256 read off the filed bytes and equal to v2/vendor/SOURCES.yaml. They cite DS18004
at v2/vendor/diodes/diodes-bzt52c-ds18004.pdf, and they correct the stale line saying board E's D3 had no code.

kisch.py is in every board's generator identity, so boards A, B, C, D, E and P were regenerated on the KiCad box
(9.0.9), next to main's own generators:
- main's generators against main's committed files: PARITY or PARITY_AFTER_NOISE on all 36 artefacts;
- an independent comparison (every part record, every net's nodes with pin, pinfunction and pintype, libparts,
  intents, schematic bodies) finds 0 unexpected differences. A and B change only the ten clamps: libsource
  D_TVS to D_Zener, pin names A1/A2 to K/A on the same nets, and the intent gains clamps. ERC changes only the
  symbol name in the ten lib_symbol_issues warnings. BOMs are at parity. C, D, E and P change only their sidecars.

Every schematic-phase gate reads the same before and after except TRN-001: A goes from FAIL to PASS of 26 and B from
FAIL to PASS of 17, both with 0 reversed and 0 symbol mismatches. PCB-BRING-UP.md and ROTATION-CHECKLIST.md were
re-rendered and are byte-identical.

Decision 31's holds on A, D and E stay: its resolution is in their netlists but in no committed layout.
