### Blocking

- **power_tree.py 'proof' of each stage, as claimed in v2/docs/diagrams/README.md ('power_tree.py refuses to draw a stage its netlist does not support'), power-tree.md ('Stages, each proved in the netlist'), the legend inside svg/power-tree.svg ('Every edge is proved in the netlist'), the added ARCHITECTURE.md 4.1 line ('every stage proved in the netlist by the tool that draws it') and the closer's report**
  *problem:* The claim is stronger than the check. prove() floods from the source net through every inductor and every power FET (any pin except 4), not only the named parts. It then accepts the stage if the target is reached and each named part was touched. Named parts are not checked to be in the path. For stages built on controllers with external FETs this accepts the wrong controller. I ran it at e3aedb25 on wrong entries and it accepted all of these: ('A','VBAT','+5V_DEV',('U4','R43')), ('A','VBAT','+13V8_PA',('U15','R55')), ('A','VBAT','+54V_POE',('U19','R71')), ('A','VBAT','PD_VPWR',('U16','R81')) and ('A','VBAT','+5V_S2',('U7','R35')). So the controller shown on each of the six LM5176 stages (and on the charger, front end and E's tracker, ideal-diode and hot-swap stages) is typed in TREE, not machine-verified. I checked each A controller's value text and VOUT pin 12 or shunt by hand: the drawn assignments are correct at e3aedb25. Only the guarantee is overstated, and a rebuild after round 8 would not catch a wrong assignment. Fix, either: (a) cross only FETs whose gate net touches a named part, and inductors next to those FETs or named parts, then re-run the five wrong entries above and require each to be refused; or (b) reword the four places to say the walk checks connectivity and that controller attribution is typed in TREE and checked against the part's value text.
- **svg/case-zstack.svg and pdf/case-zstack.pdf: the 1450PF frame at the end walls (tools/case_drawings.py:278-286)**

  *problem:* The wording written in pass 2 is contradicted by power_tree.py itself when run on the e3aedb25 netlists.   (1) A gate resistor passes as the controller, because drivers() counts any part with a pin on the FET's gate net:
  - E DC_F to DC_P through R1 alone (100k, Q1_G to DC_F), in place of U3 LM74700;
  - E TRK_OUT to VIN_RAW through R18 alone;
  - P SCP_OUT to PACK_P through R16 (5.1k), R17, R18 or R19, plus Q1 and Q2, in place of the BQ4050 U1.
  (2) A part that merely bridges the stage's two nets passes in place of the stage's own part:
  - A VBAT to +5V_S1 through U4 plus U8 (INA226 monitor), or U4 plus R28 (53.6k feedback resistor), in place of shunt R31;
  - the same with the INA226s U9, U10, U11, U14 and U17 for +5V_S2, +5V_S3, +5V_DEV, +13V8_PA and +54V_POE;
  - B +3V3_DEV to +2V5_KSZ through U1, the KSZ9897 switch itself (VDDIO on +3V3_DEV, AVDDH on +2V5_KSZ), in place of LDO U27;
  - D +5V_D8 to +3V3_D8 through U7, the TPA6132A2 headphone amplifier (VDD on +5V_D8, G0 strapped to +3V3_D8), in place of LDO U1.
  (3) is_inductor() accepts any two-pin ref starting with L, so LEDs are crossed as switching-node inductors: 32 on B, 6 on D, 2 on E. LED1 to LED4 appear in the walk of the KSZ entry.
  The diagram itself is correct.   This is the second attempt on this issue, so the smallest fix that closes it should be taken. Change the ARCHITECTURE.md 4.1 line, README.md, power-tree.md's limits and the closer report to say:
  - the listed negative-control classes are refused;
  - a part that bridges the two nets (a monitor, a feedback resistor, a load's supply pins) is accepted, and so is a passive on a FET gate named as the controller;
  - the attributions drawn at e3aedb25 were therefore also checked by hand.
  Drop 'wrong attributions are refused'.
  Also recommended:
  - count only non-passive parts (not R, C or D) as controllers in stage_graph();

  - add the single-part substitution class to negative_controls(), so the tool lists which substitutions it cannot refuse.
