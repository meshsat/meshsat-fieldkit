Subject: SPR-E-Flex-100: the warranted limit of the open-circuit voltage at STC, and the temperature coefficient

We are evaluating one SPR-E-Flex-100 as the solar input of a portable unit whose charger accepts a panel only if its
open-circuit voltage stays at or below 25.0 V with the cells at -20 C. Your datasheet (Document 523809 Rev D) gives, as
typical electrical data at STC, Voc 21.4 V and a voltage temperature coefficient of -58.9 mV/C. Your safety and
installation guide 524958 Rev F (Table 1 and its note, page 2) states that rated electrical characteristics are within 10%
of measured values at STC. Taken at 10 %, the open-circuit voltage at -20 C could reach about 26.2 to 26.7 V, above our
limit; the typical value gives 24.05 V. Because the open-circuit voltage also rises with irradiance, we evaluate it up to
2111 W/m2 (cloud enhancement on the extraterrestrial irradiance), where the typical value gives about 24.73 V.

1. For production SPR-E-Flex-100 modules, what upper limit on the open-circuit voltage at STC do you warrant (for example
   the flash-test acceptance limit, or a tolerance printed on the nameplate)? Is the 10 % of the guide's note that limit
   for Voc, and does it mean |rated - measured| <= 10 % of the measured value?
2. Is the -58.9 mV/C coefficient a typical value? What limits apply to it, and over which temperature range (down to
   -40 C)?
3. Which datasheet and guide revisions are current? Revision A of the guide gave Voc 21 V and Vmp 17.5 V where Revision F
   gives 21.4 V and 17.1 V: which applies to modules shipped today?
4. Can a flash-test report (Voc, Isc, Vmp, Imp at STC) be supplied for each module by serial number with the delivery?
5. If no limit is part of the specification, can you provide the distribution of Voc at STC over production lots (the
   sample size and the number of lots)? We would take it as supporting evidence only.

What we need is a limit you warrant on Voc at STC for the modules shipped, and the conditions under which it applies;
characterization data alone we cannot treat as a production guarantee. A warranted Voc at STC between 20.12 V and 21.63 V
would meet both of our limits: the upper one for the open circuit at -20 C and 2111 W/m2, the lower one because our
charger holds the panel at up to 18.8 V (the lower limit with the -58.9 mV/C coefficient as printed). Thank you.
