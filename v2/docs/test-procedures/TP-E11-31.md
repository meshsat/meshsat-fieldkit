## 1. Purpose and the decision it settles

The kit must run from a source alone (a dead or absent pack, a held pack) and must start from cold that way. The drafted charger
is TI's BQ25730, whose three modes the record bounds on the maker's printed rows, except where the sheet prints nothing:

<!-- q src="v2/docs/records/l4e11/L4E11-SOURCE-ONLY-AND-ENTRY.md" -->
> So on held evidence the start ends in VSYS_MIN or in a latch within 3.062 s, never undefined. Whether the first window succeeds
> is not bounded (no minimum delivered current is printed): **bench E11-31**, acceptance: from cold, at VBUS20 19.15 and 20.96 V,
> with VSYS's maximum capacitance and the declared always-on loads (board E on VSYS_E), at -20, 25 and 62.1 C, VSYS reaches 12.054
> V with Fault VSYS_UVP clear; the hiccup's 500 / 10 ms and the latch shown on a shorted VSYS and cleared by a re-plug.
<!-- /q -->

<!-- q src="v2/docs/records/l4e11/L4E11-SOURCE-ONLY-AND-ENTRY.md" -->
> **So each mode has the maker's printed bound on VSYS: D1 and D3 are removed.** The margin from the floor to A-14's assumed 10.0
> V converter floor is 2.054 V, which a load step in S2 or S4 must not use up: that is D2, and SLUSE65A prints no transient limit
> either (Figure 9-22, p.96, draws the peak power mode on VSYS undershoot, 1 to 6 A at VSYS_MIN 12.3 V with ICHG 0 A, at 4 ms a
> division, a scale that resolves no transient).
<!-- /q -->

This procedure measures the three modes, the held pack current, the start from cold and D2 (VSYS's step response) and settles
**U-04** (register row R-161, L4-E11 row E11-31): its move from a closure condition to a downstream qualification test once R-157,
R-177 and R-178 are applied (5d). It gates board A's final release; the charger's firmware rules (R-158) are written to the draft
meanwhile.

always-on loads are the draft's; the board E stub therefore carries U12, C31, U22's 12.0 V rail with C142 to C148 (C135 to C141
until set 28, renumbered by L4-E11 18a after they collided with the solar guard's port bank) and the fan headers, loaded to the declared currents (VSYS_E's 1.32 A at the floor: U12 0.8 A and U22 0.52 A, L4-E11 E11-33), and VSYS_E is
fed through U42 and its network as drafted (proposal: the same parts as TP-E11-38's coupon), so the start sees the eFuse's own
inrush ramp. Any difference from the draft is recorded, and the affected figures are then re-read on the first prototype.

## 3. Safety

- **The pack:** a battery simulator that sources and sinks (proposal) stands for the pack; a real 4S pack only under the laboratory's
  battery rules (README section 6) with a fuse in its lead.
- **Shorted VSYS (the hiccup and latch run):** the short is applied with VSYS discharged and the source off, then the source is
  plugged; the short is a rated low-resistance link, not a probe tip.
- **Load steps up to about 48 W at VSYS:** the electronic load and the leads rated for it; the load's own protection set.
- **Chamber** at -20 C and 62.1 C: burns; condensation when opening cold.

## 5. Setup and measurement points

1. The specimen of TP-E11-37 with the board E stub; the battery simulator on the pack terminals through R17; VBUS20 from the input
   supply; the electronic load on VSYS for the steps.
2. Measurement points: VSYS at the pair's source pins (DC and transient); SRN (the pack side of R17); the pack current in the pack
   lead; VSYS_E on the stub; VBUS20; BATDRV (information).
3. Firmware rules as drafted (E11-28, quoted in TP-E11-37's setup): EN_OOA 0 written at boot, with VSYS recorded before that write.

## 6. Steps

Each block runs at 25 C, then -20 C and 62.1 C, after the soak (proposal: 30 minutes with the board steady within 1 K).

1. **Pack absent (S4).** Pack terminals open. VBUS20 at 19.15 V, then 20.96 V. With the stub's always-on loads, read VSYS (DC) after
   EN_OOA 0 is written. Record VSYS before the write as well.
2. **Charge inhibited, pack present.** CHRG_INHIBIT 1. The simulator set in turn to SRN 10.0, 12.0, 12.3, 12.6, 14.4 and 16.8 V
   (proposal: each region of L4-E11 15d and its edges). Read VSYS at each.
3. **The held pack current.** At SRN 14.4 V and 16.8 V (proposal), CHRG_INHIBIT 1, board E on VSYS_E: read the pack current after it
   has settled (proposal: the mean over 5 minutes, 10 minutes after the setting). Record its sign: out of the pack (the drain the row
   bounds) or into it.
4. **Start from cold.** Pack absent; VSYS discharged to under 0.1 V (proposal); VBUS20 plugged at 19.15 V, then at 20.96 V. Capture
   VSYS from the plug to 5 s after (proposal), read Fault VSYS_UVP, and record the time to 12.054 V. Ten starts at each voltage and
   ambient (proposal), because the record cannot bound the first window.
5. **Hiccup and latch.** VSYS shorted, VBUS20 plugged: capture the converter's on and off times (500 ms off, 10 ms on, L4-E11 15b) and
   the latch after the 7th failure inside 90 s; remove the short; confirm the latch holds; re-plug VBUS20 and confirm the start.
6. **D2, the step response in S2 and S4.** S4: pack absent. S2: the simulator sink-only at 14.4 V (proposal). VBUS20 at 19.15 V (the
   low end, proposal). Apply each declared step of L4-E11 11a (the table below) from the stub's always-on load, ten times each
   (proposal); capture VSYS's minimum and the recovery time.

- **Pack absent:** PASS when VSYS less U is at least 12.054 V at every input voltage and ambient.
- **Inhibited:** PASS per region: SRN over 12.546 V, VSYS within VSRN + 150 mV plus or minus 2 % with U inside the band; SRN under
  12.054 V, VSYS less U at least 12.054 V; between, VSYS less U at least 11.96 V.
- **The held pack current:** PASS when the current out of the pack plus U is at most 1 mA at every point. A current into the held pack
  is recorded; TBD (owed by R-161): whether a current into the held pack counts against the 1 mA.
- **The start:** PASS when every start reaches 12.054 V with Fault VSYS_UVP clear at each input voltage and ambient; the time is
  RECORDED. One start that latches or reports the fault is a FAIL of the start at that point.
- **Hiccup and latch:** PASS when the hiccup and the latch are shown and the re-plug clears the latch.
- **VSYS before EN_OOA's write:** RECORDED.
- **D2:** each declared step whose VSYS minimum less U stays over 10.0 V is admitted in that state; a step that does not is assigned to
  R-c's step rule (not admitted while the pack cannot discharge), as the row says ("else R-c's step rule"). The result is the list of
  admitted steps per state, and the row passes when every step the kit's firmware will admit in S2 and S4 is shown over 10.0 V.
  The bench's step uses the load's fastest slew; TBD (owed by R-161): the declared steps' rise times, needed only if a step fails at
  the bench's slew and the real load is slower.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** the specimen's VSYS capacitance or always-on loads differ from the draft without the difference recorded; the
  ambient not held; EN_OOA not written before the reading the line applies to.

## 10. Consequence of a fail, and the re-test triggers

- **A mode line failed** (VSYS under its printed bound) contradicts the maker's printed row the record relies on (L4-E11 12c): the
  result goes to TI with Q-TI-15 and Q-TI-16, and U-04 returns to a closure condition (the record's fallback for a VSYS with no battery
  is the hold-up bank of L4-E11 11a, register row E11-24, never applied).
- **The held current over 1 mA:** the drains are not bounded on held evidence beyond the quantified 0.1408 mA (L4-E11 16e); the
  excess is traced (D3's hot leakage, the body diodes) and the hold's endurance re-derived.
- **A start that fails or latches:** the source-only start is not shown; the first window is the open item (L4-E11 15b).
- **A step under 10.0 V:** that step is not admitted in S2 and S4 (R-c), and the bridge's rule is written to the measured list.
- **Re-test triggers** (proposal, after TP-E11-37's block, whose triggers this specimen shares): the gate network, the FET part or lot,
  VSYS's capacitance, the charger's settings (EN_LDO, VSYS_MIN), the always-on loads or U42's network change.
