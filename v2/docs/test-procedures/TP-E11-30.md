## 1. Purpose and the decision it settles

When the pack's discharge FET switches onto an empty VSYS (a docking event), the inrush passes through one battery FET's body
diode. The record describes the waveform and why a junction-temperature calculation cannot accept it:

<!-- q src="v2/docs/records/l4e11/L4E11-SOURCE-ONLY-AND-ENTRY.md" -->
> A junction-temperature calculation does not extend ISM's printed rating (320 A, single pulse, tp at most 10 us, Tmb 25 C; Table
> 5, p.3). The waveform to accept: **242.9 A peak, time constant 33.8 us**, over IS's 80 A for 37.6 us, from a +70 C mounting
> base, once per docking event (the pack's discharge FET switching onto an empty VSYS), taken whole in one FET's body diode.
<!-- /q -->

The record selected a defined pulse qualification on parts (its option D2), with Nexperia's written acceptance (Q-NXP-1, drafted,
not sent) asked in parallel and board P's inrush limit (D3) as the fallback. This procedure is that qualification. It settles
**D-14's docking pulse** (register row R-160, L4-E11 row E11-30) and gates only board A's final release.

## 3. Safety

- **Stored energy.** The rig is a charged capacitor bank switched into the specimen. Its voltage and energy are the rig designer's;
  the bank has a bleed resistor, a visible charge indicator, an interlocked enclosure, and is discharged and shorted before any
  hand enters the enclosure.
- **Pulse current** to about 270 A: the leads and the switch are rated for it; magnetic forces on loose leads; eye protection
  against a failing part (a body diode can fail short or open violently under an over-stress).
- **Unattended pulsing** (about 5.6 h per part): an automatic abort on a missed or abnormal pulse (a peak or time constant outside
  the validity window of section 8), on the block's temperature leaving its window, or on a specimen short.
- **Hot block** at 75 C: burn protection when changing parts.

## 5. Setup and measurement points

1. **Six single-device coupons**, each with one BUK6Y10-30PX soldered as on board A (its mounting-base pad joined to a land that sits
   on the heated block), short low-inductance leads to the rig. Record the coupon's fabricator, copper weight and the solder process.
2. **The pulse path:** the rig's output into the part's drain-to-source direction that forward-biases its body diode (a P-channel
   part: drain positive; the supplier confirms against the sheet's symbol), gate tied to source.
3. **Measurement points:** the pulse current (the shunt or transformer in the return path); the voltage across the part at its pins;
   the block's sensor and the mounting-base thermocouple.

## 6. Steps

1. **Identity.** Record the reel and the date code of all six parts (one reel, one date code). Number them P1 to P6.
2. **First readings at 25 C**, each part, before any pulse: VSD at 80 A pulsed; IDSS at -30 V; RDS(on) at -10 V; IGSS. These are each
   part's own baseline ("its first reading").
3. **Set the rig** on a dummy (a sacrificial part of the same type, not one of the six): adjust until ten consecutive pulses each fall
   inside the validity window of section 8 and read 267.2 A and 37.2 us at the setting. Record the setting.
4. **Mount P1** on its coupon on the block at 75 C; wait until the mounting-base thermocouple is steady within 0.5 K over 5 minutes
   (proposal).
5. **Pulse:** 2000 pulses, one every 10 s. For every pulse record the peak, the time constant (an exponential fitted to the decay from
   the peak to 10 % of it; proposal), the forward voltage at the peak and the mounting-base temperature just before the pulse. A pulse
   outside the validity window is not counted and is repeated; more than 20 such pulses on one part (proposal) stop the run as
   INCONCLUSIVE for the rig to be corrected.
6. **Cool** the part to 25 C and repeat the four readings of step 2.
7. **Repeat** steps 4 to 6 for P2 to P6.
8. **Reduce:** per part, each post-test reading against its first reading and against the printed limit, with U.

- **Validity of each pulse** (from the block: "the envelope is set so that the waveform less its uncertainty still covers 242.9 A and
  33.8 us"): a pulse counts when its peak less U is at least 242.9 A and its time constant less U is at least 33.8 us, and the
  mounting base reads 75 C. With the block's 2 % and 5 % the setting of 267.2 A and 37.2 us leaves 261.9 A and 35.3 us at the least,
  inside that window. A pulse whose peak or time constant exceeds the setting by more than the rig's stated repeatability stresses the
  part beyond the plan: if a part fails after such pulses its run is INCONCLUSIVE, not a FAIL of the part (proposal).
- **Validity of the base temperature:** the mounting base stands for the +70 C mixed air at docking (the block: "the mounting base at
  **75 C** (over the +70 C mixed air at docking, when no current flows before the pulse)"); a pulse delivered with the base reading
  less its 2 K under +70 C is not counted (this folder's reading of that sentence).
- **PASS** for the row when all six parts, after 2000 counted pulses each, meet every line of the acceptance above with U taken
  against each line (README section 3): the VSD change plus its U at or under +5 %; IDSS plus U at or under 1 uA; the RDS(on) change
  plus U at or under +5 % and RDS(on) plus U at or under 10 mOhm; IGSS plus U at or under 100 nA.
- **FAIL** when any part fails any line on a valid run.
- **INCONCLUSIVE** when a run is stopped (section 6 step 5), the lot is mixed, or the pre-test reading of a part is itself outside the
  printed limits (that part is replaced from the same reel and the replacement recorded).
- A fall of VSD or RDS(on) is not bounded by the acceptance: TBD (owed by R-160): whether a fall beyond the measurement's uncertainty
  counts as a failure; until stated, any such fall is reported with the part's data and no verdict is drawn from it.
