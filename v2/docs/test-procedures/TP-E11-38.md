## 3. Safety

- **Hard shorts.** Case (c) applies a 10 mOhm short to a charged VSYS: the first microseconds come from VSYS's local capacitance, with a
  peak the record extrapolates to 566 A (17a). The voltage is low (at most 17.375 V) but the energy in the capacitors is released at
  once: the short is a rated switched link in an enclosure, never a hand-held lead; eye protection.
- **Heating.** Cases (e) and (h) run for an hour or more at +70 C into a short: the contact, the wire and the copper are expected near
  their 85 C lines. An automatic abort stops the source if the 813 body or the wire reaches 100 C (proposal: the run has failed by then,
  and the abort protects the parts and the chamber); the wire's insulation can smoke.
- **Fans** (cases (f) and (g)): finger guards on the fans; a stalled-fan fixture that cannot release the rotor suddenly.
- **Chamber** at -20 C and +70 C: burns; condensation when opening cold.

## 6. Steps

**Before any case:** the 813's four-wire resistance at 1 A (its first reading), D23's VF at 5 A (its first reading), photographs
of the contact, the wire and the copper. **After every case:** the same readings and photographs, the plunger checked free.

The matrix, as the row states it: at -20, 25 and 70 C and at VSYS 9.688 and 17.375 V, ten times each, with the cases that name a
temperature or a VSYS run only there. Read literally, that is about 20 h of chamber time for case (e) (one hour, ten times, at two
VSYS values) and 10 h for case (h); the supplier may propose fewer repetitions of (e) and (h) for the coordinator to accept as a
change of the row; none is taken here.

- **(a) The limit.** Slow ramp of the load in constant-voltage mode, VIN less VOUT held at 1 V: the current at which U42 limits.
  Then the reading with 17 V across U42 (VSYS 17.375 V, VOUT about 0.4 V), taken inside the first millisecond before thermal
  regulation (proposal), RECORDED for Q-TI-18.
- **(b) Operating overload.** U42 on and settled; switch in 2.88 Ohm, then the load just under 2 x I(OL): capture the current's
  settling (to 1.802 A within 1 ms), the time to turn-off (within 202 ms) and the off time before the retry (500 to 800 ms).
- **(c) Hard short while on.** U42 on with VSYS_E at its steady load; close the 10 mOhm short at VSYS_E. Capture the branch current
  (the peak and the time over 3.6 A), IN's peak and OUT's least.
- **(d) Start into the short.** Short closed first; enable U42 (or plug VSYS): capture the current from the first microsecond to after
  the turn-off: the current after 100 us, the time until thermal regulation begins (the current's step down), the regulated
  current, the time from regulation's start to turn-off, and the total on-time.
- **(e) One hour of retry** into the short at 70 C: the 813 body logged once a second (proposal), the wire and the copper likewise.
- **(h) Intermittent short** at 10 Hz, 1 Hz and 0.1 Hz for ten minutes each at 70 C; the duty is TBD (owed by R-184): the duty of the
  intermittent short at each frequency, with 50 % proposed until it is stated. Then one removal during limiting and one during the
  retry's off-time: the recovery of VSYS_E and U22's rail captured.
- **(f) The fans on the rail** at VSYS 9.688 V: U12, U22 and both fans started one at a time by the PWM-duty ramp, then both fans'
  duty stepped 0 to 100 % together. The ramp's duration and PWM frequency are the firmware's, TBD (owed by R-188): the ramp's
  duration and the PWM frequency the firmware writes. Capture FLT, VSYS_E, the 12 V rail and the branch current.
- **(g) Faults on the rail** at 70 C: one fan stalled (rotor held), then the 12 V rail shorted at a header: capture U22's response
  (limit or disable, then recovery), VSYS_E's least, the controller's supply (U12's output) and whether U42 retries.
