## 1. Purpose and the decision it settles

Board E's two mixer fans (Sanyo Denki 9WL0612P4H001, Layer 7's selection) run from a regulated 12.0 V rail (U22, an LTC3115-1)
fed from VSYS_E behind the eFuse U42. Their starting current and PWM input level are not read (the maker's manual is behind a
form), and the branch has little room for a start:

<!-- q src="v2/docs/records/l4e11/L4E11-SOURCE-ONLY-AND-ENTRY.md" -->
> **The fans' start** (NOT READ, Layer 7): with one fan running and U12 on, U42's 0.1504 A of room leaves 1.21 W at the rail for
> the other fan's start, 1.6 times its running power, before U42 limits; U22 itself delivers about 1.2 A at the floor. The fan,
> not the rail, is the unknown (E11-35, E11-38 f).
<!-- /q -->

<!-- q src="v2/docs/records/l4e11/L4E11-SOURCE-ONLY-AND-ENTRY.md" -->
> U22's 9 ms soft start covers the rail's own rise only; a fan's start surge comes when its PWM duty rises. **The stagger stays**:
> one fan at a time, each by a PWM-duty ramp into the fan's PWM input (Layer 7's F-L7-05), never while U12 or U22 starts.
<!-- /q -->

This procedure measures the fans' start and PWM input (register row R-179, L4-E11 row E11-35) and verifies the firmware's
one-at-a-time ramp on the branch (register row R-188, L4-E11 row E11-39). It settles the mixers' start current and PWM level on
U22's rail against U42's room, and gates the fans' acceptance for board E's final release. TP-E11-38 case (f) repeats the
stagger as part of the branch's fault envelope; this procedure is where the fan itself is characterised.

## 3. Safety

- **Fans:** finger guards; the fan clamped on its mounting before it is powered; nothing loose near the inlet.
- **Chamber** at -20 C and +70 C: burns; condensation when opening cold; the fan blows the chamber's air, so the chamber's own
  control may need a baffle (record it).
- Low voltage (12 V); the bench channel current-limited (proposal: 1 A, well over a 0.17 A fan, so the start is not clipped; record
  that the limit was never reached).

## 5. Setup and measurement points

1. **Part A.** Each fan on its mounting as under the plate (a plate section, the fan's screws as drawn), in the chamber; its 12 V and
   GND wires to the bench channel through the current probe; the PWM wire to the generator (open drain emulated by a transistor to
   ground, proposal, as Q9 and Q10 drive it); the tach wire to a counter (information).
2. **Part B.** The fans on the stub's headers J_FAN1 and J_FAN2 of TP-E11-38's coupon, U22 and U12 on VSYS_E, VSYS at 9.688 V (the
   floor), the branch current probed at J_DOCK pin 1, FLT pulled up for observation (TP-E11-38 section 5).

## 6. Steps

**Part A, each fan, at 25 C, then -20 C and 70 C after a soak (proposal: 30 minutes):**

1. **Identity:** the fans' date codes. **Hole pattern:** the centres and diameters, recorded against the drawing they must fit; that
   drawing and its tolerance are TBD (owed by E11-35): the mounting drawing and tolerance the fans' hole pattern is checked against.
2. **PWM input:** the open-circuit voltage at the PWM wire, the current at 0 V (pulled low), and the fan's speed against duty from 0
   to 100 % in 10 % steps (tach), including what the fan does at 0 % (stops, or runs at a minimum).
3. **Power-on surge:** the channel switched on at 12.0 V with PWM at 0 %, then with PWM at 100 %: the current waveform for 2 s
   (proposal), its peak, and its mean over the first 100 ms (proposal).
4. **PWM step start:** the fan at rest on 12.0 V; the duty stepped 0 to 100 %: the current waveform until the speed settles; its peak,
   the duration over the running current, and the running current. Repeat at 11.512 V and 12.431 V. Ten starts each (proposal).
5. **Ramp start:** as step 4 with the firmware's ramp in place of the step. The ramp's duration and the PWM frequency are the firmware
   owner's; until stated they are TBD (owed by R-188): the ramp's duration and the PWM frequency the firmware writes.

**Part B, on the coupon at 25 C, then 70 C (proposal):**

6. **The forced start (R-188):** with U12 and U22 settled and one fan running at full speed, start the other by the firmware's ramp;
   then the reverse. Capture the branch current at J_DOCK pin 1, FLT, VSYS_E and the 12 V rail. Ten forced starts (proposal).
7. **The firmware's order:** a cold start of the whole branch: confirm from the captures that the fans start one at a time, never both
   within 1 s, and never while U12 or U22 is starting (E11-39).

- **Part A (R-179, E11-35):** the start current, the PWM input level and the hole pattern are RECORDED and filed; they are compared with
  the record's room for one fan's start (1.21 W at the rail, with the other fan running and U12 on; L4-E11 18b, quoted in section 1).
  The record states that room as power at the rail but not which quantity of the start it bounds: TBD (owed by R-179): the start
  quantity (the peak power at the rail, total or above the running draw, and over which averaging window) that is compared with the
  1.21 W. A fan that does not start at 11.512 V or 12.431 V at any of the three ambients does not meet its maker's printed 10.8 to
  13.2 V operating range and is reported as a non-conforming fan (proposal).
- **Part B (R-188, E11-39):** PASS when at every forced start the branch current's maximum plus U is under 1.4713 A, one fan ramps at a
  time with the other, U12 and U22 settled, and the firmware never starts both fans within 1 s or a fan while U12 or U22 starts. FAIL
  otherwise.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** the bench channel reached its current limit during a start (the start was clipped); the fan not on its mounting as
  under the plate; the firmware's ramp not the one the firmware owner states.

## 10. Consequence of a fail, and the re-test triggers

- **A start over the room**, or a part B start reaching U42's limit: the stagger is lengthened or the ramp slowed (the firmware's), and
  if no ramp keeps the branch under 1.4713 A the branch's current is re-derived with U42's setting (L4-E11 18b: R228 stays 11.0 kOhm only
  while the declared current fits); the record names the reversal of the fans' feed as "a fan whose maker prints a range covering
  VSYS_E" (18a).
- **A PWM input the drafted open-drain drivers Q9 and Q10 cannot drive:** a finding for board E's generator owner (the drivers were kept
  with the fan's PWM level not read; L4-E11 18a).
- **Re-test triggers:** the block's "Re-test when" line (section 2): another fan part or lot; a temperature outside -20 to 70 C; the
  rail's window or the PWM ramp changes.
