## 1. Purpose and the decision it settles

Board A's battery switch is two Nexperia BUK6Y10-30P P-channel FETs, Q39 and Q40, driven by the TI BQ25730's BATDRV. Each is
sized to an RDS(on) **allowance** of 21.136 mOhm (TP-E11-36 confirms or reverses it). Whether the pair's junctions stay at or under
150 C through the pack's protection events depends on the installed thermal path of the two FETs together: each FET's own
impedance from junction to air (Zself) plus the mutual impedance through which the other FET heats it (Zmut). The record states
how the two combine:

<!-- q src="v2/docs/records/l4e11/L4E11-SOURCE-ONLY-AND-ENTRY.md" -->
> **The board and the coupling, stated as what a bench reads.** Each FET's self impedance Zself(t) (heat one, read its own
> junction by its body diode's VSD) and the mutual Zmut(t) (heat one, read the other). With Pb the even split's per-FET power at
> the allowance (it bounds each FET's power for any split, 15c), **TJ <= air + Pb x (Zself + Zmut)(t)** for either FET: the
> coupling is in the sum, not assumed away, and Zself includes the device's own Zth(j-mb).
<!-- /q -->

This procedure measures Zself and Zmut on a specimen and settles **D-14's installed path** (register row R-159, L4-E11 row
E11-29). Its result gates only board A's final release (5d below); layout and the evidence build proceed meanwhile.

> no worse by this rule: the pair's spacing is at least the coupon's; the pour inside the region is the coupon's or a superset

**Consequences for this procedure.** A coupon result is reported in two parts: the **steady** figures (steady state and 60 s),
which transfer to the final board only if its boundaries pass the block's comparison rule; and the **transient** figures (1 s,
20 ms, 244 us), which transfer only if the region is copied unchanged, otherwise they are re-read on the first prototype by this
same procedure. The coupon's design (copper, vias, the neighbours within 30 mm) is therefore drawn from board A's intended
region; the list of neighbouring sources with their heats and positions is TBD (owed by R-159): the list of parts within 30 mm of
Q39 and Q40 that dissipate in service, each with its declared heat and position, from the pre-layout analysis.

## 3. Safety

- **Hot surfaces:** the chamber air at +70 C, the FETs' junctions near 140 C at the steady point, the neighbours' resistive dummies,
  and the K-factor oven up to 150 C. Handle the coupon hot only with gloves; let the chamber cool before opening for changes.
- **Current:** the heating current is a few amperes through each body diode at about 2.1 W per FET; low voltage. The supplies are
  current-limited to the run's need and switched off before any wiring change.
- **The gate:** each FET's gate is tied to its source for the whole run (the channel must not conduct); a floating gate can turn a
  FET partly on and destroy the reading or the part.
- No lithium-ion cell is used; VBAT and CH_BATQ are bench nodes only.

## 5. Setup and measurement points

1. **The coupon:** board A's battery-switch region as drafted: Q39 and Q40 on their LFPAK56 lands, R17, the VBAT and CH_BATQ copper,
   the intended stack-up, copper weight and via field, and the neighbours' footprints within 30 mm carrying their dummies. Record the
   fabricator, the stack-up, the copper weights and the via counts to each inner plane.
2. **Electrical:** each FET's gate tied to its source. Heating current from CH_BATQ (drain) to VBAT (source) forward-biases the body
   diode of this P-channel part; the supplier confirms the polarity against the sheet's symbol. The VSD sense taps are four-wire, at
   each FET's own pins.
3. **Mounting:** the coupon fixed to a plate section as board A is fixed to the case (its standoffs, their material and fastening as
   Layer 7 draws them), in its installed orientation, inside the still-air box in the chamber.
4. **Thermocouples:** the air in the box (two, mid-height, shaded from the coupon), the plate section (one), the coupon's copper
   next to each FET (one each, for information only: the junction is read by VSD).

## 6. Steps

1. **Identity.** Record both FETs' reel and date code, the coupon's fabricator, stack-up and copper weight (the block's lot line).
2. **Each unit's own Rth(j-mb)**, before mounting, on the cold plate by the transient dual-interface method; record it per unit.
3. **Mount** both FETs on the coupon (reflow profile as the maker recommends; record it). Inspect the solder joints (X-ray of the
   LFPAK56 mounting-base joint, proposal: voids change the path).
4. **K-factor**, each FET in place on the coupon, in the oven: at each calibration temperature soak until the coupon's thermocouple
   is steady within 0.2 K over 10 minutes (proposal), then read VSD at the sense current. Fit VSD against temperature; record the
   slope, its standard error and the residuals. The sense current stays the same for the whole run.
5. **Chamber.** Fit the coupon to its plate section, wire the dummies and the thermocouples, close the still-air box, and bring the
   air to +70 C. Energise the neighbours' dummies at their declared heats (the list owed in section 2). Wait until the air and the plate
   are steady within 0.2 K over 30 minutes (proposal).
6. **Case A, Q39 heated alone.** Heat Q39 at 2.1136 W (power within 1 %), Q40 unheated with its sense current only. Hold until Q39's
   junction rise changes by at most 1 % of itself over 10 minutes (proposal; the steady endpoint). Record both junctions (VSD
   converted by each K-factor) and the air. Switch Q39 to its sense current and record both junctions' cooling curves from the
   switch to at least 60 s past steady recovery (proposal: until both are within 1 % of their pre-heating values).
7. **Case B, Q40 heated alone.** As step 6 with the roles exchanged.
8. **Case C, both heated** at 2.1136 W each, as step 6: the direct reading of each FET's (Zself + Zmut) at steady state and, by the
   cooling curves, at every time.
9. **Repeat** cases A, B and C three times each (proposal) to obtain the repeatability term.
10. **After the runs:** one K-factor point re-read at the chamber temperature (drift within the K-factor's 2 %, else the run is
    INCONCLUSIVE); visual inspection of the coupon.
11. **Reduction** (record the script or worksheet): Z(t) from each cooling curve as (rise at steady less rise at t) / P (the cooling
    curve's superposition, valid while the network is linear, as the block's linearisation states); Zself,i and Zmut,j->i from cases
    A and B; for each FET i the sum Si(t) = Zself,i(t) + Zmut,j->i(t); the direct sum from case C. Apply the block's correction for
    other units: Si(t) plus (1.4 K/W less unit i's Rth(j-mb)), which is conservative at the short times.

- **PASS** at a time when the larger of the two FETs' corrected sums plus its U is at or under that time's limit; the row passes when
  every time passes, from case C and from the A plus B decomposition alike.
- **FAIL** otherwise, on a valid run (README section 3, including "within uncertainty").
- **INCONCLUSIVE** when: the power is not within 1 % of 2.1136 W; the air drifts by more than its 1 K; the K-factor's re-check moves
  by more than 2 %; the steady endpoint is not reached; case C's direct sum and the A plus B sum differ by more than their combined
  U (the linearity the reduction rests on is then not shown; proposal); the coupon differs from the drafted region in a way the
  record of the run does not explain.

## 9. The uncertainty budget

| Term | Source | Value |
|---|---|---|
| K-factor | the block | within 2 % (relative, on every rise) |
| Heating power | the block | within 1 % (relative) |
| Air temperature | the block | within 1 K (on the steady and 60 s readings only: a cooling-curve difference cancels it) |
| VSD noise at short times | this procedure (proposal) | at most 5 % of the 244 us rise at the limit, standard |
| Switching delay and early-time correction | this procedure (proposal) | settled within 25 us; any correction applied stated with its U |
| Repeatability | step 9 | the standard deviation of the three repeats |
| Unit Rth(j-mb) reading | step 2 | its own U, carried into the corrected sum |
| Coupon boundaries | not a measurement term | handled by the transfer rule, never folded into U |

U = 2 x the root-sum-square of the standard terms (README section 3). Example at the steady limit, with the block's three terms
taken as expanded figures: 2 %, 1 % and 1.4 % give about 2.7 %, so a steady reading passes at or under about 32.3 K/W (32.3 x 1.027 = 33.1).

## 10. Consequence of a fail, and the re-test triggers

- **If TP-E11-36 reverses the allowance** (RDS(on) over 21.136 mOhm), this row's target is re-sized before layout (L4-E11 16a) and the
  limits above change with it; the record's plan states the steady figure at the sheet's 25 mOhm:
