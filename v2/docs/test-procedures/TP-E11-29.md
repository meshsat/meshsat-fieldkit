## 3. Safety

- **Hot surfaces:** the chamber air at the block's temperature, the junctions near the junction limit in the combined runs (step
  11), R17 at its power, the neighbours' dummies, the heavy leads and their guard heaters, SW_S's clamp, and the calibration oven up to
  the junction limit. Handle the
  coupon hot only with gloves; let the chamber cool before opening for changes.
- **Current:** the heating supply carries up to about 23 A through one channel at a fraction of a volt (section 4); the band's
  supply and R17's supply carry the block's band current, tens of amperes. Leads, lugs and supplies rated for it, joints torqued
  and checked, because a loose high-current joint heats and can scorch the coupon. Every supply is current-limited to the run's
  need and switched off before any wiring change.
- **The gates:** each gate is driven by the fixture at every instant: at VBAT (off), 10 V under VBAT (conducting), or tied to its
  own drain tap Dk (the threshold connection). A floating gate can turn a FET partly on and destroy the reading or the part. The
  part's limits are VGS +-20 V and VDS -30 V (Nexperia BUK6Y10-30P, 17 April 2020, Table 5 p.3): the gate drivers are clamped inside
  +-15 V (proposal) and the heating supply's open-circuit voltage is limited under 20 V (proposal).
- **The heating supply is never connected to a FET in the threshold connection.** A gate tied to its drain with the supply on the
  pours would put the whole heating current through that diode-connected FET: at least 20.7 W at 13.82 A on the threshold's least
  printed row (L4-E11 24a). So a gate is tied to its drain tap only while the series switch SW_S is open, enforced by a hardware
  interlock that reads SW_S's OWN state continuously (its drive enable), with the shunt's current under 10 mA as a second barrier for a
  failed switch; SW_S closes only while no gate is tied and a gate sits 10 V under VBAT. The six rules R1 to R6 are in section 5; a search
  of every reachable state finds none hazardous under them (L4-E11 25c).
- **The heating supply never meets an open circuit:** it always has a path, through a conducting channel or through the bypass
  SW_B onto its dummy leg; SW_B closes before SW_S opens, and SW_S closes before SW_B opens; in time division the gates switch
  make-before-break.
- No lithium-ion cell is used; VBAT and CH_BATQ are bench nodes only.

## 4. The method, the equipment, and the accuracy each measurement needs

**The method in one paragraph.** Current always enters at the common source pour (VBAT) and leaves at the common drain pour
(CH_BATQ). In that direction every body diode is reverse-biased, so a FET whose gate sits on VBAT carries only its leakage. One
gate at a time is driven: 10 V under VBAT to heat that FET through its channel, or tied to its own drain tap Dk (gate k on CH_BATQ
at its own drain tap Dk) to read that FET's threshold voltage at a small sense current. For every reading the heating supply is
disconnected from the pours by a series switch, so only the sense current flows. So the heating power and the temperature reading
each belong to one named FET, although the three share both pours. The record's statement of the three connections (round 13) and
of the fixture that makes them (round 14):

<!-- q src="v2/docs/records/l4e11/L4E11-SOURCE-ONLY-AND-ENTRY.md" -->
> (B) the pours as board A's; only the gates apart: gate k to a driver, the other gates on VBAT (off)
>     calibration (oven)   I_M 1 mA (+) -> VBAT pour (the common source) -> channel k at threshold, GATE k ON CH_BATQ -> CH_BATQ pour -> (-)
>                          (VDS = VGS, the connection of the sheet's own threshold row); the other two off, their body diodes
>                          reverse-biased (the source positive); V(source tap k, drain tap k) is device k's threshold
>     heating              I_H (+) -> VBAT pour -> channel k, gate k driven 10 V under VBAT -> CH_BATQ pour -> shunt -> (-);
>                          P_k = I_H x V(source tap k, drain tap k)
>     sensing              I_H off; gate k switched to CH_BATQ; I_M as in calibration; then gate j, then gate l in turn
>     several heated       time division: the channels conduct in turn in slots of at most 244 us, so each device's power is
>                          still I_H x its own VDS over its slots

## 6. Steps

1. **Identity.** Record the three FETs' reel and date code, the coupon's fabricator, stack-up and copper weight (the block's lot
   line), and the fixture's switches with their makers' printed off-state leakage, capacitance and on-resistance.
2. **Each unit's own Rth(j-mb)**, before mounting, on the cold plate by the transient dual-interface method; record it per unit.
3. **Mount** the three FETs, R17 and the dummies on the coupon (reflow profile as the maker recommends; record it). Inspect the
   solder joints (X-ray of the LFPAK56 mounting-base joints, proposal: voids change the path).
4. **K-factor, first calibration,** the coupon in the oven with the fixture connected in its reading state (SW_S open, SW_B closed),
   each FET in turn tied to its own drain tap: at each calibration temperature soak until the coupon's thermocouple is steady within
   0.2 K over 10 minutes (proposal), then read FET k's threshold at the sense current. Fit the threshold against temperature for each
   FET; record the slope, its standard error and the residuals (validity check V6). The sense current stays the same for the whole run.
   At each calibration temperature also read each guard's pair isothermally: its offset dT_0 (at most 0.05 K, proposal).
5. **The off devices' and the fixture's leakage** (validity check V3), at the highest calibration temperature, the fixture connected
   in its reading state: all three gates on VBAT, 3 V applied from the VBAT pour to the CH_BATQ pour (the threshold's largest printed
   value), the current read. It must be at most 1 % of the sense current; if it is more, raise the sense current until it is not,
   restate the sense current's own heating (its current times the threshold, times the bar) and repeat step 4 at the new current.
   Read the fixture's part again at the laboratory's temperature before and after the runs (the coupon disconnected at the taps).
   **Then the control run C0** (L4-E11 24f; V2R-m4), still in the oven at the top calibration temperature, the coupon isothermal: for
   each FET, its gate driven exactly as in a run (10 V under VBAT for a case's heating time, then the reading sequence of step 7), with
   SW_S open so only the sense current flows, and the threshold read in the same 100 us to 10 ms window. Its difference from that
   temperature's static calibration is a bias-induced threshold shift with the settling: it is APPLIED AS A CORRECTION to that FET's
   readings taken after its own gate was driven, and only its own uncertainty (0.25 K, proposal) stays in U (validity check V9).
6. **Chamber and baseline.** Fit the coupon to its plate section, wire the fixture, the dummies and the thermocouples, close the
   still-air box, and bring the air to the block's temperature. Energise the neighbours' dummies at their declared heats (the list
   owed in section 2) and the band at the block's current; R17 stays unheated in the baseline and in cases A to D and W (its heat
   enters in cases E and F only). **Pickup check:** with no FET heated, watch each FET's threshold reading while the band's current is
   switched on: a step at the switching instant, before any heat can arrive, is pickup in a sense loop; it is recorded and subtracted
   from every later reading (the band's current is held within 1 %), its uncertainty in the budget; a step over 1 K at the K-factor
   (proposal) is removed by moving the taps before any run. Wait until the air and the plate are steady within 0.2 K over 30 minutes
   (proposal). **Check V3 again in the chamber**, every supply, dummy, thermocouple, guard and instrument connected as in a run, all
   gates on VBAT, SW_S open. Switch the guards on and wait until each is in control. **Record each junction's baseline rise over the
   air, B_k, and RT1's land:** the band's and the neighbours' share, judged in the limit's line (section 8).
7. **Cases A, B and C: Q39, Q40 and Q42 each heated alone,** at the even split's power and again at the worst split's power (the
   block's two figures), the band and the dummies on. Hold until the heated FET's junction rise changes by at most 1 % of itself
   over 10 minutes (proposal; the steady endpoint). **Reading a junction** (L4-E11 24b): SW_B closes; 10 us later SW_S opens (t = 0,
   read on the shunt as the coupon's current falls to the sense current); 2 us later the gate of the FET to be read leaves its
   driver's -10 V output and is tied to its own drain tap Dk, the other gates on VBAT (the interlock allows it only with SW_S open by
   its own state and the shunt under 10 mA, rule R1); SW_S's clamp ends the current left in the leads within about 0.7 us; sample the threshold from 100 us to 10 ms and extrapolate to t = 0 on the square root of time (the early-time
   correction, stated with its uncertainty: it carries the 10 us in which the current divides between the dummy leg and the coupon,
   SW_S's on-resistance among them, and the switch's own opening); then the gate back to -10 V, SW_S closes, SW_B opens, and heating
   resumes. Through every step the device under test's channel is the only one conducting between the pours.
   **One FET is read per interruption,** the interruptions at least 1 s apart (proposal), so each reading is the first after its own
   interruption;
   the heating is off about 1 % of the time and each power is averaged over the whole cycle. Read the heated FET at 60 s and at steady;
   read the other two junctions, RT1's land, each joint's thermocouple and each guard's logged difference and heater power at steady
   (validity check V8).
8. **Case D, the three evenly,** by time division: the three gates driven in turn, the slots' widths and the current set so that
   each FET's own power is the block's even figure within the power term (the three RDS(on) differ, so equal slots do not give
   equal powers). Hold to the steady endpoint; read each junction (one per interruption), RT1's land and the leads.
9. **Cases W39, W40 and W42, the fixed split** (m = 0): time division with the slots set so that one FET takes 1.513 W and each other
    0.756 W, each FET in turn as the hot one. Hold to the steady endpoint; read each junction and RT1's land. Read the hot FET once at
    the end of its own slot and once at the end of its off interval (validity check V5). These cases check linearity (V4); they no
    longer judge the limit.
10. **Case E, R17 heated alone** at the block's R17 power, no FET heated, the band and the dummies on. Hold until steady. Record
    each junction's rise over the air before R17 is switched on and at R17's steady state; the air's drift between the two
    readings stays within 0.1 K (proposal), else the case is repeated.
11. **Cases F39, F40 and F42, the limit read directly,** after the matrix of cases A to C is reduced (step 14): for each FET k hot in
    turn, the split worst for junction k's own row of the matrix (step 14: x_k = 2 - 4 m_k while m_k is under 1/4, the even split
    from 1/4; P_hot = I^2 R x_k / (x_k + 2)^2 and each other I^2 R / (x_k + 2)^2 with I^2 R = 12.103 W), R17 at its power, the band
    and the dummies on, to steady. Record each junction, RT1's land and the leads.
12. **Repeat** cases A to E and W three times each (proposal) to obtain the repeatability term, and run the second coupon by
    steps 1 to 14 (section 2).
13. **Check V3 in the chamber at once after the last case**, the coupon hot, every instrument connected; then the **K-factor's second
    calibration,** as step 4 (validity check V1); visual inspection of the coupon.
14. **Reduction** (record the script or worksheet).
    - **The matrix:** from cases A to C at each power, Z[k][j] = junction k's rise **over step 6's baseline B_k** (not over the air)
      divided by FET j's power, P_j: Zself,k = Z[k][k], and the six mutual terms; each heated junction's readings corrected first by
      its control run's measured difference (step 5). Apply the block's correction for other units (its lot line) to each Zself: plus
      the printed maximum Rth(j-mb) less unit k's reading.
    - **Each junction's own worst split** (L4-E11 24d): in junction k's row, C_k the largest term (Zself,k in any physical reading)
      and D_k the sum of the other two; m_k = D_k / (2 C_k); Zw_k = 9 C_k^2 / (4 (2 C_k - D_k)) while m_k is under 1/4, else C_k + D_k.
      Zw_k at most 45.88 K/W is the same as S_k = C_k + D_k at most section 1's bar at m_k. The former bar at m = the largest Zmut over
      the largest Zself, applied to every S_k, is **withdrawn** (it gave a junction the bar of another junction's coupling).
    - **R17's coupling** into each junction from case E, as the rise difference over R17's power, z17_k.
    - **The pours:** from case j alone, the VBAT pour's path resistance V(joint on VBAT, tap Sj) and the CH_BATQ pour's V(tap Dj, joint
      on CH_BATQ), each over the heating current; R_pour,max is the VBAT pour's largest of the three plus the CH_BATQ pour's largest of
      the three. The pours' service loss is at most (23.93 A)^2 x R_pour,max.
    - **The guarded leads:** each guard's residual flux q_res,J = k A (|its logged difference| + its offset dT_0) / dx (copper's k
      390 W/(m K), assumed; A and dx as built), at most 5.2 mW; the joint-to-junction impedance Z_kJ = Z_Jk, joint J's rise over the
      baseline per watt of FET k in cases A to C (its first thermocouple), by reciprocity. With the guards in control the coupon is the
      coupon without its leads plus these residuals, so no factor and no assumption on the leads' share enter.
    - **The limit by superposition:** for each junction, T_k = B_k + z17_k x 2.86 W + 1.3448 W x Zw_k + Zself,k x (23.93 A)^2 x
      R_pour,max + the sum over the two joints of Z_Jk x q_res,J: the baseline, R17, the FETs at junction k's own worst split, the
      bound for the pours' service loss (no source raises junction k more per watt than FET k itself) and the guards' residual.
    - **The direct cases F:** each case's hottest junction rise over the air plus the same two terms.
    - **Each line's U, propagated** through this reduction from the budget's sources (section 9).
    - The gradient from the hottest junction to RT1's land in every case, RECORDED.

## 10. Consequence of a fail, and the re-test triggers

- **A fail** (R-159): "over the limit, more copper or a heat path, never a lowered service". The service's current and its duration

  source-island coupon judged on the 40.78 K/W bar alone, with an allowance for the VBAT copper that a second specimen would have

  2), and a reading over the allowance re-sizes this row before layout (R-182 and E11-36, quoted in TP-E11-36). The re-sized limit
  is the record's to state before this procedure is run against it.
- **If TP-E11-37's answer is negative:** the pair at its fallback (R-183, quoted in TP-E11-37). For the pair this procedure is run
  with two gates against the fallback line (section 2).
- **Re-test triggers:** the block's "Re-test when" line (quoted in section 2): any boundary failing the comparison rule; another FET
  part or lot without a unit reading; a change of the FET count or of R17's place; a change of the plate attachment, the fan or its
  position.
