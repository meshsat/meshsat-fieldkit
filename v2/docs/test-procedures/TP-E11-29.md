## 3. Safety

- **Hot surfaces:** the chamber air at the block's temperature, the junctions near the junction limit in the combined run (step 9),
  R17 at its power, the neighbours' dummies, and the K-factor oven up to the junction limit. Handle the coupon hot only with gloves;
  let the chamber cool before opening for changes.
- **Current:** each FET's heating current is a few amperes through its body diode at low voltage. The band's supply and R17's
  supply carry the block's band current, tens of amperes at a fraction of a volt: leads, lugs and supplies rated for it, joints
  torqued and checked, because a loose high-current joint heats and can scorch the coupon. Every supply is current-limited to the
  run's need and switched off before any wiring change.
- **The gate:** each FET's gate is tied to its source for the whole run (the channel must not conduct); a floating gate can turn a
  FET partly on and destroy the reading or the part.
- No lithium-ion cell is used; VBAT and CH_BATQ are bench nodes only.

## 5. Setup and measurement points

2. **Electrical:** each FET's gate tied to its source. Heating current from CH_BATQ (drain) to VBAT (source) forward-biases the
   body diode of this P-channel part; the supplier confirms the polarity against the sheet's symbol. The VSD sense taps are
   four-wire, at each FET's own pins. The band's supply drives the band's copper only, not through any FET or R17, and R17's supply
   drives R17's own terminals (proposal: so that each heat source can be switched alone). With no FET heated, the three VSD readings
   are watched while the band's current is switched on: a step at the switching instant, before any heat can arrive, is pickup in a
   sense loop and is removed by moving the taps before any run (proposal).
3. **Mounting:** the coupon fixed to a plate section as board A is fixed to the case (its standoffs, their material and fastening as
   Layer 7 draws them), in its installed orientation, inside the still-air box in the chamber.
4. **Thermocouples:** the air in the box (two, mid-height, shaded from the coupon), the plate section (one), R17's body (one), the
   coupon's copper next to each FET (one each, for information only: the junction is read by VSD).

## 6. Steps

1. **Identity.** Record the three FETs' reel and date code, the coupon's fabricator, stack-up and copper weight (the block's lot
   line).
2. **Each unit's own Rth(j-mb)**, before mounting, on the cold plate by the transient dual-interface method; record it per unit.
3. **Mount** the three FETs, R17, RT1 and the dummies on the coupon (reflow profile as the maker recommends; record it). Inspect
   the solder joints (X-ray of the LFPAK56 mounting-base joints, proposal: voids change the path).
4. **K-factor**, each FET in place on the coupon, in the oven: at each calibration temperature soak until the coupon's thermocouple
   is steady within 0.2 K over 10 minutes (proposal), then read VSD at the sense current. Fit VSD against temperature; record the
   slope, its standard error and the residuals. The sense current stays the same for the whole run.
5. **Chamber and baseline.** Fit the coupon to its plate section, wire the dummies and the thermocouples, close the still-air box,
   and bring the air to the block's temperature. Energise the neighbours' dummies at their declared heats (the list owed in section
   2) and the band at the block's current. Wait until the air and the plate are steady within 0.2 K over 30 minutes (proposal).
   Record each junction's rise over the air: the band's and the neighbours' share, RECORDED (the record budgets the band apart from
   the FETs' sum, 19b quoted in section 1).
6. **Cases A, B and C: Q39, Q40 and Q42 each heated alone** at the block's per-FET power (within its power term), the other two at
   their sense current only, the band and the dummies on. Hold until the heated FET's junction rise changes by at most 1 % of itself
   over 10 minutes (proposal; the steady endpoint). Record the three junctions (VSD converted by each K-factor) and the air. Switch
   the heated FET to its sense current and record the three junctions' cooling curves from the switch until each is within 1 % of
   its baseline rise (proposal).
7. **Case D, all three heated** at the block's per-FET power each, as step 6: the direct reading of each FET's sum.
8. **Case E, R17 heated alone** at the block's R17 power, no FET heated, the band and the dummies on. Hold until steady (as step 6).
   Record each junction's rise over the air before R17 is switched on and at R17's steady state; the air's drift between the two
   readings stays within 0.1 K (proposal), else the case is repeated.
9. **Case F, combined (proposal, information):** the three heated at the block's per-FET power, R17 at its power, the band and the
   dummies on, to steady. Record each junction at the switch instant (with the early-time correction) and the air. The hottest
   junction, referred to the block's air (its rise added to the block's air when the chamber's differs), is RECORDED as the direct
   reading of what the two pass lines of section 8 stand for.
10. **Repeat** cases A to E three times each (proposal) to obtain the repeatability term, and run the second coupon by steps 1 to 10
    (section 2).
11. **After the runs:** one K-factor point re-read at the chamber temperature (drift within the block's K-factor term, else the run is
    INCONCLUSIVE); visual inspection of the coupon.
12. **Reduction** (record the script or worksheet): Z(t) from each cooling curve as (rise at steady less rise at t) / P (the cooling
    curve's superposition, valid while the network is linear, as the block's linearisation states); Zself,i and Zmut,j->i from
    cases A to C; for each FET i the sum Si(t) = Zself,i(t) + Zmut,j->i(t) + Zmut,k->i(t) over the two other FETs j and k, at 60 s and
    steady (the record's "Zself + 2 Zmut" with each actual mutual term in place of an assumed equal one; proposal); the direct sum
    from case D; R17's coupling into each junction from case E, as the rise difference over R17's power. Apply the block's correction
    for other units (its lot line) to each Si: plus the printed maximum Rth(j-mb) less unit i's reading.

**ACCEPTANCE AWAITING CORRECTION AND SUPPLIER AGREEMENT.** The limit quoted from L4-E11 round 9 assumes the current splits evenly
between the three FETs; with unequal on-resistance one FET can dissipate up to 9/8 of the even-split power (the maximum of
r/(R+2r)^2 is at r = R/2). The corrected limit is owed by L4-E11's next round. This procedure is not to be run until the corrected
limit is quoted here and the supplier has agreed the procedure.

The pass lines below are therefore the proposed form only (the owner's review of 4 October 2026): each is written against the
figure the two quotes state (not restated here), and each is re-read against the corrected limit before any run. The per-FET
heating power of the steps (the block's operating point) is re-read with it.

## 10. Consequence of a fail, and the re-test triggers

- **A fail** (R-159): "over the limit, more copper or a heat path, never a lowered service". The service's current and its duration
  are kept, and no protection is lowered (R-159, quoted in section 8).
- **If TP-E11-36 reverses the allowance:** the block's per-FET power is computed at the allowance (its operating point, section 2),
  and a reading over the allowance re-sizes this row before layout (R-182 and E11-36, quoted in TP-E11-36). The record states no
  three-FET limit at another RDS(on); the re-sized limit is the record's to state before this procedure is run against it.
- **If TP-E11-37's answer is negative:** the engineer chooses between the pair at its fallback and one FET with a heat path through
  the case (R-183, quoted in TP-E11-37). For the pair this procedure is run with two FETs against the fallback line (section 2); one
  FET with a case path is a different specimen and is not written here.
- **Re-test triggers:** the block's "Re-test when" line (quoted in section 2): any boundary failing the comparison rule; another FET
  part or lot without a unit reading; a change of the FET count or of R17's place; a change of the plate attachment, the fan or its
  position.
