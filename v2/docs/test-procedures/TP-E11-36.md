## 1. Purpose and the decision it settles

The BQ25730's BATDRV drives the battery FETs' gates at least 8.5 V below VSYS, and the maker's sheet prints no RDS(on) maximum at
that drive when hot. The record's statement of the gap and of the allowance:

<!-- q src="v2/docs/records/l4e11/L4E11-SOURCE-ONLY-AND-ENTRY.md" -->
> BATDRV's gate drive is 8.5 / 10 / 11.5 V (SLUSE65A p.17) with the FETs' sources on VSYS, so VGS is -8.5 V at the least. The
> BUK6Y10-30P's sheet (17 April 2020, Table 7, p.6) prints RDS(on) at most 10 mOhm at -10 V and 25 C, 16 mOhm at -10 V and 175 C
> and 25 mOhm at -4.5 V and 25 C. **No printed point bounds the operating point** (-8.5 V, hot): the -10 V rows need more drive
> than BATDRV promises and the -4.5 V row is at 25 C only. The search for a part whose sheet prints one found none: the held
> SQJ403EP and AONS21357, and two sheets fetched for this round and filed held (Vishay SQJ407EP, 62806 Rev. B; Nexperia
> PXP9R1-30QL, 5 January 2021), all print their hot maxima at -10 V only. **SESSION:** section 15c's 21.136 mOhm is withdrawn as a
> bound and kept as the **allowance** each FET is sized to (why this value: every downstream figure of 15c and 15d, the 9.688 V
> supplement floor among them, was computed at it, and at 25 mOhm the sensitivity is printed); **E11-36** confirms it (Nexperia's
> maximum at -8.5 V and 150 C filed, or a pulsed Kelvin reading on parts from the lot in an oven at 150 C) or reverses it. At the
> sheet's largest printed maximum, 25 mOhm, every power scales by 1.1828; parts at 25 mOhm on a board built to the allowance would
> take the held 20 A to 152.8 C and the service to 137.1 C, which is why E11-36 gates E11-29. *Reverse:* a reading over the
> allowance re-sizes E11-29 before layout.
<!-- /q -->

This procedure is the "pulsed Kelvin reading on parts from the lot in an oven at 150 C". It settles **D-14's RDS(on) allowance**
(register row R-182, L4-E11 row E11-36) for the lot read; only Nexperia's printed maximum would settle it for every lot. It gates
the sizing's confirmation and board A's final release, and it comes before TP-E11-29's verdict, whose limits are computed at this
allowance.

## 3. Safety

- **Oven at 150 C:** burns; the coupon, its wiring and connectors rated for 150 C (a high-glass-transition laminate and
  high-temperature wire insulation; proposal); the oven vented if the laminate or flux outgasses.
- **Current:** 9 A pulses at a few hundred millivolts; low voltage; the pulse source current-limited.
- **The gate:** the -8.5 V gate supply is applied before the drain pulse and removed after it; a gate left floating can turn the
  part partly on.

## 4. Equipment, and the accuracy each measurement needs

| Item | Class | Accuracy and capability needed |
|---|---|---|
| Pulsed current source | a high-current pulse source or a curve tracer's pulsed module | ID -9 A in pulses short enough that self-heating stays under 1 K (the block); current within 1 % (the block) |
| Kelvin voltage measurement | a digitiser or a pulse-capable voltmeter on separate sense leads at the part's drain and source pins | within 0.5 % (the block): at 9 A through 21.136 mOhm the drop is 190.2 mV, so about 1 mV |
| Gate supply | a low-noise bench supply or a source-measure unit | VGS -8.5 V measured at the gate pin (the block); its own uncertainty recorded |
| Oven | a laboratory oven with a reference thermocouple at the coupon | the junction at 150 C within 1 K after the soak (the block) |
| Reference temperature sensor | a calibrated thermocouple or a platinum resistance sensor fixed to the coupon beside the part | within 1 K (the block's oven term is this reading) |

**The pulse width that keeps self-heating under 1 K** follows from the record's own figures: at 9 A through 21.136 mOhm a part
dissipates 1.712 W, and the device's own transient impedance (L4-E11 16b, from Nexperia's Fig. 4 scaled to Table 6) is 0.2589 K/W
at 244 us, so a pulse of 244 us or shorter heats the junction by at most 0.44 K, under the block's 1 K. The source text:

<!-- q src="v2/docs/records/l4e11/L4E11-SOURCE-ONLY-AND-ENTRY.md" -->
> **0.2589 K/W at 244 us, 1.3571 at 20 ms, 1.4 at 1 s and at 60 s**
<!-- /q -->

The reading is taken late in the pulse, after the current has settled (proposal: the mean over the last fifth of the pulse), and
the pulses are spaced at least one second apart (proposal) so each starts from the oven's temperature.

## 5. Setup and measurement points

1. **The Kelvin coupon:** ten positions (or ten coupons), each part on an LFPAK56 land with separate force and sense connections at
   its drain and source pins and a gate connection; one reference sensor per coupon beside the parts.
2. **Wiring** out of the oven in high-temperature leads; the force leads sized for 9 A; the sense leads twisted pairs.
3. **Polarity:** for this P-channel part VGS is -8.5 V (gate below source) and ID is -9 A (current from source to drain through the
   channel), as the block states; the supplier confirms the polarities against the sheet.

## 6. Steps

1. **Identity.** Record the reel and date code of the ten parts (the build's lot).
2. **Room reading** at 25 C as a sanity check (information): RDS(on) at VGS -10 V and -8.5 V; a part reading over the printed 10 mOhm
   at -10 V and 25 C is set aside and recorded (it would also fail TP-E11-30's limits).
3. **Soak** the coupon in the oven at 150 C until the reference sensor is within 1 K of 150 C and steady within 0.2 K over 10 minutes
   (proposal).
4. **Read each part:** VGS -8.5 V at the gate; one 9 A pulse of at most 244 us; the Kelvin voltage and the current over the reading
   window; RDS(on) = V / I. Three pulses per part (proposal), at least one second apart; the mean and the spread recorded.
5. **Gate sensitivity (information, proposal):** the same reading at VGS -8.3 V and -8.7 V on two parts, to state how much the gate
   supply's own uncertainty moves RDS(on).
6. **Re-read** the reference sensor after the last part; a change over 1 K makes the round INCONCLUSIVE.

- **PASS** for the lot when every part read gives RDS(on) plus U at or under 21.136 mOhm.
- **FAIL** when any part's RDS(on) plus U exceeds 21.136 mOhm on a valid reading: the allowance is reversed for that lot and R-159
  (TP-E11-29's limits) is re-sized before layout, as quoted above. A part over the limit is never discarded from the result.
- **INCONCLUSIVE** when the oven is not within 1 K of 150 C, VGS at the gate is not -8.5 V within its stated uncertainty, a pulse's
  self-heating could exceed 1 K (pulse width over 244 us or current over 9 A), or the reference sensor moves by more than 1 K.

## 9. The uncertainty budget

| Term | Source | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Kelvin voltage | the block | within 0.5 % |
| Current | the block | within 1 % |
| Oven (junction temperature) | the block | within 1 K; its effect on RDS(on) is the part's temperature coefficient times 1 K. On the line through the sheet's two printed maxima at -10 V (10 mOhm at 25 C, 16 mOhm at 175 C; L4-E11 16a) that is 0.04 mOhm/K, about 0.27 % at 15 mOhm (an inference from maxima, not a printed coefficient) |
| Self-heating | the pulse width (section 4) | at most 0.44 K, a one-sided bias, added to the oven term |
| Gate voltage | step 5 | the slope times the gate supply's U |
| Repeatability | step 4 | the spread of the three pulses |
| The block's total | the block | "RDS(on) within about 2 %": U is the larger of 2 % and the supplier's own root-sum-square of the terms above |

## 10. Consequence of a fail, and the re-test triggers

- **A fail** reverses the allowance: TP-E11-29's limits are re-derived for the measured value before layout; the record states the
  consequence at the sheet's largest printed maximum, 25 mOhm (L4-E11 16a, quoted in section 1).
- **A maker's maximum** at -8.5 V and 150 C from Nexperia closes the row for every lot and makes this test unnecessary (5d: a draft to
  Nexperia is owed to L4-E11).
- **Re-test triggers:** the block's "Re-test when" line (section 2): another lot or part; BATDRV's printed least drive falling; the
  junction limit rising over 150 C.
