**Consequences for this procedure.**

- **The specimen carries three FETs.** If TI's evaluation hardware is used, it is modified to the draft: BQ25730 pin 21 (BATDRV) to
  the gates of Q39, Q40 and Q42 from one node by equal branches (their length and any series element as drafted), the three between
  VSYS and CH_BATQ, R17 (5 mOhm) between CH_BATQ and the pack, and VSYS's capacitance as drafted (the parts as
  `v2/docs/records/l4e11/apply_gen_sch_a_charger.py` writes them for board A; at most 489 uF on VSYS, L4-E11 15b and 12e). The
  modification is recorded with photographs; any difference from the draft limits the transfer as the block states. A run with two
  FETs fitted says nothing about the three, and the reverse (the block's extrapolation line).
- **The shared pour.** The block records each junction "on the shared pour", so the three sit on one CH_BATQ drain pour and one VBAT
  source pour as board A's layout requirement draws them (L4-E11 19b, quoted in TP-E11-29 section 2). On evaluation hardware that
  is a small carrier for the three FETs, wired in by the shortest leads the bench allows (proposal); its copper is recorded.
- **Each drain's current.** The block reads each FET's drain current. With the drain tabs on one pour, the current is read in each
  FET's source connection (the same current), through a calibrated link of equal resistance per FET or a current probe on an equal
  loop per FET (proposal). The links are a difference from the draft and are kept small against the FETs' on-resistance; their
  resistance and inductance are recorded with the run, because source resistance evens the sharing and the result is read with that
  in mind.
- **Each junction.** The channel conducts during these runs, so the body diode's VSD method of TP-E11-29 does not apply. Each FET's
  junction is recorded as its drain tab's temperature (a fine thermocouple at the tab) plus its own dissipation (its current times
  the drop across it) times the sheet's printed maximum Rth(j-mb), which TP-E11-29's block quotes (proposal).
- **The gate network is the draft's.** The block's re-test line names the gate network. Round 9's draft
  `v2/docs/records/l4e11/apply_gen_sch_a_dd7.py` (register row R-217, the hardware charge inhibit) puts the drain of a small
  P-channel FET, Q49, on the same gate node. If the build is to carry that draft, the specimen carries Q49 and its gate parts too,
  with the inhibit not asserted, so Q49 is off as in normal service (proposal); the run's record states which gate network was
  tested. Q49's own function is register row R-219's bench, not this procedure's.
- The same specimen then serves TP-E11-31.

## 3. Safety

- **The pack:** a lithium-ion pack is not needed; a bidirectional DC source (a battery simulator that sources and sinks) stands for
  the pack (proposal), set to the record's voltages. If a real 4S pack is used: the laboratory's battery rules (README section 6),
  a fuse in the pack lead rated for the 10 A run, and the pack outside the chamber unless its own temperature rating covers the run.
- **Current:** up to 10 A on the battery path; leads, links and the simulator rated for it. If one FET takes most of the current
  (which this procedure exists to find out), its tab is watched: a run is stopped when a recorded junction reaches the junction
  limit the acceptance of TP-E11-29 quotes (proposal).
- **Chamber:** -20 C and 70 C runs; burns at 70 C; at -20 C, let the specimen warm in dry air before opening (condensation on a
  powered board).

## 5. Setup and measurement points

1. Specimen in the chamber; the battery simulator on the pack terminals (through R17 as drafted); VBUS20 from the input supply; the
   electronic load on VSYS; the charger's converter running as in service (the block's neighbouring sources).
2. Probe points: BATDRV at pin 21; the gate node, and each FET's gate at its pin; VSYS at the three's source pins; CH_BATQ at the
   drain tabs; the battery current (a current probe on the pack lead); each FET's current (its link or probe); each FET's tab
   thermocouple. The sum of the three FET currents is compared with the pack-lead current at one steady point before the runs; a
   difference beyond the two readings' combined uncertainty is resolved first (proposal).
3. The charger configured as the firmware rules draft it (E11-28): EN_OOA 0 at boot, VSYS_MIN 12.3 V (its POR value for 4S),
   ChargeCurrent as each step needs.
4. Record what the block lists as boundaries: the gate loop (its length, the branches, any series element, Q49 if fitted), the
   three's spacing and copper, the links, the neighbouring sources running, the airflow and the chamber.

## 6. Steps

Run the whole sequence at 25 C first, then at -20 C and 70 C, each after the specimen has soaked to the chamber's temperature
(proposal: 30 minutes after the board's thermocouple is steady within 1 K).

1. **Supplement entry.** Battery simulator at 14.4 V (proposal: a mid-charge 4S stack), VBUS20 at 20 V, the load at 0, 2, 5 and 10 A
   in turn (the block's 0 to 10 A; the points are a proposal). Remove VBUS20. Capture BATDRV, the gate node, VSYS, the battery
   current and each FET's current from 1 ms before to 50 ms after (proposal). Record: VSYS's least value; the time from removal
   until the gate is at least 8.5 V below VSYS (TI's least BATDRV drive, L4-E11 16a); any ringing or periodic component on BATDRV
   or the gates after the entry has settled; each FET's current during the entry and once it has settled. The block's BATDRV time
   constants for the three (section 2) are expectations on typical Ciss, not limits: the measured gate edges are RECORDED beside
   them.
2. **Ideal-diode regulation and sharing.** VBUS20 present with the input limit set below the load (proposal: the load 0.5 A above
   the input's limit) so the pack supplements a small current; step the supplement current in small steps (proposal: 0.1, 0.25,
   0.5, 1, 2 A), then to 5 and 10 A (proposal), where the FETs are fully on and share by their on-resistance. At each step record
   the drop across the three (the 30 mV regulation, which holds until the FETs are fully on), each FET's current and its own drop,
   and BATDRV and the gates for oscillation over at least 1 s (proposal). At 2, 5 and 10 A hold until the tab thermocouples change
   by at most 0.5 K over 5 minutes (proposal), then record each tab and each FET's current again.
3. **LDO mode and sharing.** VBUS20 present; battery simulator at 10.0 V (the pack's CUV stack) and at 8.0 V (the Shutdown Voltage);
   the charger in precharge at the record's 0.33616 A (the block). Record VSYS (DC, and its ripple), BATDRV and the gates over at
   least 10 s (proposal), each FET's current, and each tab once steady (as step 2). The FETs are linear here and dissipate; the
   record sizes this mode with the whole current in one FET (quoted in section 8), and L4-E11 12e's rule R-b' bounds when this mode
   may run.
4. **Supporting reading:** after the runs, the three FETs' Ciss (desoldered, or on spare parts of the same lot) at VDS -15 V and
   near 0 V, against the typical figures the records state for one part and for the three (section 1). Information only.
