## 1. Purpose and the decision it settles

TI asks for a battery FET whose Ciss is under 5 nF; the drafted pair of BUK6Y10-30P is at or over that on typical figures, and TI
does not say what the 5 nF bounds. The record:

<!-- q src="v2/docs/records/l4e11/L4E11-SOURCE-ONLY-AND-ENTRY.md" -->
> TI's rule (SLUSE65A p.92) is "the Ciss of P-channel MOSFET should be chosen less than 5 nF"; TI prints the drive, RBATDRV_ON at
> most 6 kOhm and RBATDRV_OFF at most 2.1 kOhm, not the requirement behind 5 nF. The BUK6Y10-30P prints Ciss as a typical only:
> **2.36 nF at -15 V** (Table 7), no maximum and no spread; its Fig. 12 (typical) reads about **2.87 nF at -0.1 V**, near the
> battery FET's working point (on, or an ideal diode at 30 mV). The pair is **4.72 nF at the maker's test point and about 5.74 nF
> near 0 V**: it meets TI's figure only at that test point and as a typical. Into the pair's typical Ciss BATDRV's time constants
> are 28.32 us on (-15 V), 34.44 us on near 0 V and 12.05 us off; no printed limit exists to compare them with, so the actual
> requirement cannot be derived from TI's sheet. **Not shown on printed data.**
<!-- /q -->

<!-- q src="v2/docs/records/l4e11/L4E11-SOURCE-ONLY-AND-ENTRY.md" -->
> **SESSION:** the pair stays selected (it is the only arrangement whose heat a board pour can carry). **OPEN**, with E11-37: TI's
> statement of what 5 nF bounds (Q-TI-17, drafted in `clarification/TI-QUESTIONS.md`, not sent) or the bench's BATDRV behaviour
> with the pair, and on a negative answer the engineer's choice between (S1) and (S2).
<!-- /q -->

This procedure is the bench half of that choice: it watches BATDRV drive the pair through the three behaviours TI's BATFET must
show and settles **D-14's Ciss against TI's 5 nF** (register row R-183, L4-E11 row E11-37). It decides only the choice between (S1)
the pair and (S2) one FET with a heat path through the case. TI's statement (Q-TI-17, drafted, not sent) would replace it.

## 3. Safety

- **The pack:** a lithium-ion pack is not needed; a bidirectional DC source (a battery simulator that sources and sinks) stands for
  the pack (proposal), set to the record's voltages. If a real 4S pack is used: the laboratory's battery rules (README section 6),
  a fuse in the pack lead rated for the 10 A run, and the pack outside the chamber unless its own temperature rating covers the run.
- **Current:** up to 10 A on the battery path; leads and the simulator rated for it.
- **Chamber:** -20 C and 70 C runs; burns at 70 C; at -20 C, let the specimen warm in dry air before opening (condensation on a
  powered board).

## 5. Setup and measurement points

1. Specimen in the chamber; the battery simulator on the pack terminals (through R17 as drafted); VBUS20 from the input supply; the
   electronic load on VSYS.
2. Probe points: BATDRV at pin 21; each FET's gate at its pin; VSYS at the pair's source pins; CH_BATQ at the pair's drain pins;
   the battery current (a current probe on the pack lead).
3. The charger configured as the firmware rules draft it (E11-28): EN_OOA 0 at boot, VSYS_MIN 12.3 V (its POR value for 4S),
   ChargeCurrent as each step needs.

## 6. Steps

Run the whole sequence at 25 C first, then at -20 C and 70 C, each after the specimen has soaked to the chamber's temperature
(proposal: 30 minutes after the board's thermocouple is steady within 1 K).

1. **Supplement entry.** Battery simulator at 14.4 V (proposal: a mid-charge 4S stack), VBUS20 at 20 V, the load at 0, 2, 5 and 10 A
   in turn (the block's 0 to 10 A; the points are a proposal). Remove VBUS20. Capture BATDRV, the gates, VSYS and the battery
   current from 1 ms before to 50 ms after (proposal). Record: VSYS's least value; the time from removal until the gate is at least
   8.5 V below VSYS (TI's least BATDRV drive, L4-E11 16a); any ringing or periodic component on BATDRV or the gates after the entry
   has settled.
2. **Ideal-diode regulation.** VBUS20 present with the input limit set below the load (proposal: the load 0.5 A above the input's
   limit) so the pack supplements a small current; step the supplement current in small steps (proposal: 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2 A).
   Record the drop across the pair at each step (the 30 mV regulation, which holds until the FET is fully on), and BATDRV and the
   gates for oscillation over at least 1 s at each step (proposal).
3. **LDO mode.** VBUS20 present; battery simulator at 10.0 V (the pack's CUV stack) and at 8.0 V (the Shutdown Voltage); the charger
   in precharge at the record's 0.33616 A (the block). Record VSYS (DC, and its ripple), BATDRV and the gates over at least 10 s
   (proposal), and the pair's case temperature (the linear FET dissipates; L4-E11 12e's rule R-b' bounds when this mode may run).
4. **Supporting reading:** after the runs, the two FETs' Ciss (desoldered, or on spare parts of the same lot) at VDS -15 V and near
   0 V, against the typical 2.36 nF and about 2.87 nF a part (L4-E11 16c). Information only.

## 8. Pass, fail and inconclusive

The register's acceptance and L4-E11's row, quoted:

<!-- q src="v2/docs/records/l4e9/DOWNSTREAM-REGISTER.md" row="R-183" col="Acceptance" -->
> TI's statement of what the 5 nF bounds filed, or the bench's BATDRV behaviour with the pair at -20, 25 and 70 C: supplement
> entry, the ideal diode's 30 mV regulation without oscillation and LDO mode at VSYS_MIN within its printed band; on a negative
> answer the engineer chooses between the pair and one BUK6Y10-30P with a heat path through the case (L4-E11 16c's (S2))
<!-- /q -->

<!-- q src="v2/docs/records/l4e11/L4E11-SOURCE-ONLY-AND-ENTRY.md" row="E11-37" col="Acceptance" -->
> TI's statement of what the BATFET's 5 nF bounds (Ciss at which VDS, or a gate charge; Q-TI-17, drafted in
> `clarification/TI-QUESTIONS.md`, not sent), or the bench's BATDRV behaviour with the pair at -20, 25 and 70 C: supplement entry,
> the ideal diode's 30 mV regulation without oscillation and LDO mode at VSYS_MIN within its printed band; on a negative answer
> the engineer chooses between the pair and one FET with a heat path through the case (section 16c)
<!-- /q -->

- **Supplement entry:** PASS when, at each ambient and load, the pair reaches full drive and no oscillation is seen after the entry
  has settled. VSYS's least value is RECORDED and handed to TP-E11-31's D2 line (this row states no floor for it).
- **The ideal diode's 30 mV regulation:** PASS when the regulation holds without oscillation at every step. The band the 30 mV is judged
  against is the one SLUSE65A prints for the ideal-diode regulation if it prints one; otherwise TBD (owed by R-183): the band within
  which the regulated drop counts as "regulation", and until it is stated the drop is RECORDED.
- **LDO mode at VSYS_MIN within its printed band:** PASS when VSYS less U is at least 12.054 V at each ambient and pack voltage, and no
  oscillation is seen. The printed band's lower side is 12.054 V (VSYS_MIN_REG_ACC, 12.3 V at -2 %, SLUSE65A p.10); its upper side
  is not printed (L4-E11 12c and A11-18 take 12.546 V where a figure needs it), so VSYS's upper value is RECORDED against 12.546 V
  without a verdict:

<!-- q src="v2/docs/records/l4e11/L4E11-SOURCE-ONLY-AND-ENTRY.md" -->
> VSYS_MIN_REG_ACC prints -2 % in its maximum column as well (p.10), so its upper side is not printed; no claim rests on it (the
> floor decides each), and where a figure needs it 12.546 V is taken (A11-18)
<!-- /q -->

- **"Without oscillation"** has no stated amplitude or duration in the records: TBD (owed by R-183): the criterion that makes a
  waveform an oscillation (a periodic component's amplitude at BATDRV or VSYS, and how long it must persist); until it is stated,
  every periodic component after settling is recorded with its frequency and amplitude, and the row's verdict waits for it.
- **FAIL** (the "negative answer"): any oscillation by the stated criterion, a failed supplement entry, or VSYS less U under 12.054 V in
  LDO mode.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** the specimen's gate loop, pair or VSYS capacitance differ from the draft without the difference being recorded and
  accepted; the ambient not held; the probes' ground loops too long to exclude probe-induced ringing (the supplier repeats with a
  shorter connection).

## 10. Consequence of a fail, and the re-test triggers

- **A negative answer** sends the choice to the engineer: (S2), one BUK6Y10-30P with a heat path through the case, instead of the pair
  (S1); the thermal path of TP-E11-29 then changes with it (L4-E11 16c, quoted in section 1).
- **TI's statement** of what the 5 nF bounds (Q-TI-17) closes the row without this test.
- **Re-test triggers:** the block's "Re-test when" line (section 2): the gate network, the FET part or lot, VSYS's capacitance or the
  charger's settings (EN_LDO, VSYS_MIN) change.
