## 0. The order the boards come up, and the rules for every step

**The order** (proposal; each step only after the previous one has passed its stop conditions):

1. **E5**, the dock block: no power; continuity, isolation and contact resistance (section E5).
2. **P**, the pack board, on a cell simulator, never on cells first (section P).
3. **C**, the panel board, alone on a bench 5 V supply, with its controller's firmware loaded (section C).
4. **A**, the power board, alone on bench supplies (section A).
5. **D** on A over the mezzanine harness and the 5 V lead (section D).
6. **B** on A over the rail leads and the A to B ribbon, then **C** on B over the panel ribbon and to A over the MAIN lead (sections B
   and C).
7. **E** alone on bench supplies, then E with A through E5 (section E).
8. The pack (P with its cells) on E's J_BATT, the stack seated on E5, only after the docking pulse has been qualified (section P).

**Rules for every step.**

- **Current-limited supplies, in two stages.** Stage 1: the limit set just above the expected no-load current with every switched
  load at its power-up state (`v2/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` 4.3's line-state table, quoted in section A). The records do not itemize the
  power path's quiescent draws, so the expected stage-1 current of each board is owed by the budgets row, R-51 (the record's own
  statement is quoted below); stage 1 records it as the board's baseline. Stage 2: the limit raised to the rail's declared typical
  current (Appendix A's "current limit" column) only for a step that enables a load. A supply that reaches its limit is a short
  until proved otherwise.
- **A derived rail is never applied from the bench**: it is measured where it is made, against its expected value and tolerance
  (Appendix A: plus or minus 5 % of the declared voltage, or the record's tighter band where this page names one).
- **Thermal watch:** every board is scanned with a thermal camera at each stage; a part that heats with no load to explain it is a
  stop. Temperatures are recorded, not judged here (the thermal acceptance belongs to T-H1 and the TEST-PLAN rows).
- **Every stop** leaves the board unpowered and is recorded with the readings that caused it; the step is not repeated until the
  cause is found.

### A2. First power-up

1. **No pack, no other board.** Every board-to-board connector open; J_MAINSW to a bench pushbutton (IF-AC-MAINSW's MAIN lead); a
   bench I2C host on the kit bus at J_AB1 (the pin map is IF-AB-RIBBON's).
2. **VIN_RAW_IN** at J_VR1 to J_VR4 from a bench supply at 12.0 V (Appendix A's VIN_RAW row), stage 1. Expected before MAIN: the
   front end U2 runs once U34 releases, VBUS20 rises, the charger U3 starts, VSYS (the net VBAT) rises; the LTC2954 holds RAIL_EN low,
   so board A's +3V3 stays off. The stage-1 current is TBD (owed by R-51): board A's quiescent draw with every switched load off,
   which no record itemizes.
3. **The charger's first writes** (section A5), then VSYS read against its no-pack line.
4. **MAIN pressed**: +3V3 rises, the expanders U27 and U28 leave reset as inputs, DEV_EN is on by its pull-up, so +5V_DEV starts by
   design; every other enable stays off. Record the input current again.
5. **The pack node from a bench supply, never from a pack:** CELL+ at J_CP1 (and the precharge pin J_PRE1) from a current-limited
   supply at 14.4 V (Appendix A), stage 1. A pack, or any stiff source, on CELL+ produces the docking pulse through one battery FET's
   body diode (242.9 A peak, 33.8 us), which is qualified only by TP-E11-30.
6. **Stage 2, one switched load at a time** through the expanders (section A5's order): each output's enable, its rail measured, its
   current recorded, then off before the next.

### A7. Stop conditions

- A supply at its limit in stage 1, or any input current over the stage's expected value by more than its stated tolerance.
- VBUS20 outside 19.08 to 20.96 V; VSYS under 12.054 V with no pack after EN_OOA 0; any rail outside Appendix A's tolerance or this
  page's band; a rail that is up with its enable off.
- A slot rail, PoE, the PA rail or an outlet rising before its enable is written; any SLOT_EN line above 0.266 V before the panel
  drives it.
- U42's limit acting (VSYS_DOCK collapsing) with nothing on pin 1; U44's or U45's limit acting at no load.
- **On a board without the VBUS20 cut: VIN_RAW over 24 V, ever.**
- A pack, or a stiff source, on CELL+ before TP-E11-30 has passed or Nexperia's acceptance is filed (R-160).
- A part heating with no load to explain it.
