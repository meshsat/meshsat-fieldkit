# MeshSat control panel (PCB-C backer C7 under the face plate): software contract for the panel controller and the bridge

> Generation MESHSAT-830 (7 Sep 2026, `V2-SPEC.md`, appendix 32.52, 32.60). Nothing has been built: this contract describes the C7 backer as generated by `v2/ecad/tools/gen_sch_c.py` and is the specification for the panel controller firmware (MESHSAT-837) and for the bridge on the compute modules. The previous contract (the released C6 with the PCA9555 pair on the compute module's bus and the previous e-paper module on SPI0) is in the git history of this file.

Hardware record: `MESHSAT-709-geometry-appendix.md` sections 32.52 (fabric), 32.56 (face), 32.60 (C7 design). The panel is a **USB device**: an RP2040 panel controller (`U3`) on **bank 1's** USB hub over the `USB_PNL` pair of the 2x13 ribbon `J_PANEL`. Since the I/O high-availability work of 9 September 2026 a bank is not a module: bank 1's home host is the slot 1 module and its failover host is slot 2, selected in hardware (`ARCH-PCB-B-IOHA.md`), so the panel keeps a host when one module is lost. The bridge sees the panel move hosts, never disappear, unless two modules are gone. The compute modules never touch the panel's pins directly; every indicator, switch, the e-paper, the sounder and the light sensor are behind the controller, which presents one USB composite device (a CDC console for the bridge protocol and a vendor HID report for the state). The lines that act without any software are MAIN PWR, EMCON and the TX lamp (section 6). The ZEROIZE toggle is a hardware line too, but it reaches only the controller: the erase itself is the controller's firmware (decision 30, ruled by the owner on 26 September 2026, section 6).

## 4. Expanders (PCA9555, on the controller's I2C bus, INT wire-ORed on `EXP_INT`)

| Bit | Port 0 (output, LED sink) | Port 1 |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | SHORE (green, `D9`) | TX_LAMPTEST (output): low lights the TX lamp through the BAT54 tie `D17` |
| 1 | MSG (white, `D11`) | SPARE6 |
| 2 | PI ring (amber, in the PI button) | SPARE7 |
| 3 | BAT1 (bar 20 %, amber, `D12`) | SPARE8 |
| 4 | BAT2 (40 %, green, `D13`) | SPARE9 |

| 6 | BAT4 (80 %, `D15`) | SPARE11 |
| 7 | BAT5 (100 %, `D16`) | SPARE12 |

LED drive rule: an LED is ON when its port bit is configured as an OUTPUT driving 0; OFF when the bit is configured as an INPUT (high impedance). Never drive a 1: the anode rail is the 5 V LED rail and a 3.3 V push-pull high ghosts the LED. On boot the firmware first writes the configuration registers so every LED bit is an input, then the output registers to 0, then enables outputs one by one.

Hardware LEDs, no software: MAIN ring (alive whenever the LED rail is present), TX (red, `D3`, follows D8's real KEY line `TR_APRS` through `Q3`; the lamp-test tie lights it too).

## 5. Compute slots seen from the panel

The controller owns three lines per slot: `HBn` in, `SLOT_ENn` out, and the display switch selects. Rules:

- Boot, in this order: `PI_KILL` driven low (push-pull, and never released while any slot is powered: on the netlist a powered slot's level stage can lift it and switch the kit off, a mechanism not yet measured); `PI_SHDN_REQ` released; `ZEROIZE_HW` read, and if it is closed, or the flash holds a wipe-pending record, the erase of section 6 is completed before any slot powers; every expander's output register written before its configuration register (the default output register is all ones, so configuration first would switch on the PoE and USB-C stages), keeping `DEV_EN` at 1, which is written 0 only as the last act of a shutdown; the charger's registers written; then the three `SLOT_EN` high, one at a time. A slot whose heartbeat stays flat for 60 s after its rail came up is shown as a slot fault (MASTER CAUT, the e-paper names the slot); the controller power-cycles it once (rail off 5 s) and then leaves it off until the operator acts.
- `HDMI_SEL` follows the slot the bridge names over USB; with no bridge instruction the controller selects the lowest slot with a live heartbeat.
- The PI button: short press = `PI_SHDN_REQ` (clean shutdown of every module), hold 8 s = `PI_KILL`. The MAIN PWR button is hardware to A22's power controller and the controller only sees its effect.

| 0x68 | DS3231M holdover clock (CR2032 backed) | B16 |

The sensor board inside E6 (BME688 at 0x76 and the IMU at 0x68 on its own bus) belongs to the sensor controller, a second RP2040 that is another USB device; its addresses never appear on the kit bus. Rules: the panel's 3.3 V is never switched while the ribbon is attached (an unpowered PCA9555 clamps the bus). With the ribbon unplugged the kit fails safe on transmit: A22 pulls `EMCON_HW` low (`R102`, as B16 does with `R58`) and A22, B16 and D8 pull `TX_INHIBIT_n` low (`R145`, `R59`, `R2`), so every EMCON gate closes and the PA cannot key; A22 pulls `ZEROIZE_HW` high (`R117`, no wipe) and `SHORE_INHIBIT` low (`R118`, the inputs run). The slot enables are pulled low on A22, so no compute module runs. A kit without its panel neither computes nor transmits, and it does not usefully charge either (section 10).

| LIGHTING position | Inputs | Controller action |
|---|---|---|
| DAY | LIGHT_DAY_n low | duty 100 %, the bridge sets the monitor's backlight full |
| NIGHT | LIGHT_NIGHT_n low | duty 15 %, backlight 20 %, sounder allowed |
| NVG | NIGHT plus the bridge's NVG mode | duty 2 % (the lowest PWM step the firmware allows), backlight 5 %, red and amber indicators only (16c) |

One dimmer for the whole panel (MIL-STD-1472). The TX lamp is never dimmed below 10 % duty; in BLACKOUT it is dark like everything else (EMCON sits next to it for that case). The VEML7700 reading is reported to the bridge for automatic backlight control in DAY and NIGHT.

## 9. Indicator semantics, controls and the e-paper

Indicators as in the previous generation: MASTER WARN flashes (3 to 5 Hz) for any unacknowledged red condition (SOS active, ZEROIZE armed, thermal trip, pack under-voltage, slot fault), MASTER CAUT for an unacknowledged amber one (a bearer lost that was up, shore lost while charging was expected, disk nearly full, a slot fault), SAT / MESH / LTE / GPS steady for a bearer up (GPS: fix), SHORE and CHARGING from the charger over the bus, MSG for an unread inbound message, the PI ring as a 0.5 Hz heartbeat while any slot's bridge runs, BAT1..5 for 5 s after a lamp test or a short TEST press with the lowest LED flashing below 10 %. A flasher that fails must fail to steady-on (the controller holds the last written state when the USB link drops).

Controls: TEST / ACK short press = acknowledge (flashers to steady, sounder muted, MSG cleared, battery bar 5 s); hold 2 s = lamp test (all 17 LEDs 3 s, chirp, bar after); hold 5 s within 60 s of a "show QR" request from the touch UI = the e-paper shows the enrolment QR while held. SOS closed 2 s = SOS mode (flipping back cancels; the e-paper confirms both). **What SOS does (D-10, owner ruling of 26 September 2026; firmware only, no board change):** the bridge sends a distress message with the kit's position over the bearers that are up, Iridium first when nothing else is, to a configured recipient list. It never transmits through EMCON: with EMCON asserted the message is queued until EMCON is released and the operator is told on the panel (how, exactly, is the session's to define with the rest of the indications). SOS is part of prototype 1's core acceptance, taken by the session under the owner's standing rule of 26 September 2026, because D-10 defines its action and the foundation decision table recommended it. EMCON = section 6 plus the software hold (queue every send, show EMCON on the e-paper, MASTER CAUT steady). ZEROIZE closed 5 s = the erase of section 6, and it is the ONLY trigger (owner ruling of 26 September 2026): the tamper or lid switch is logged and never wipes, and there is no remote wipe in this revision. MASTER WARN flashes while armed; flipping back inside 5 s aborts. The line is LEVEL-SENSITIVE across a power loss: at boot the controller reads it before any slot powers, a toggle still closed or a wipe-pending record in the controller's flash completes the erase, and the kit re-arms only when the switch is returned.

E-paper (PDi E2370KS0C1, 416 x 240, iTC controller, PDi's driving note rev 02): idle page with callsign, battery and shore state, GPS fix, UTC time, last inbound message, bearer chips and the slot health; refresh at most once a minute, full refresh once an hour and after ZEROIZE; the QR carries an enrolment URL plus a one-time code, never key material; the controller powers the display down through `EPD_PWR_n` between refreshes (the boost stage runs only while `BUSY` is high) and polls `BUSY` before every command.

Sounder patterns: chirp 50 ms (acknowledge), double chirp (lamp test), 1 s on 1 s off (SOS armed), continuous 3 s (ZEROIZE complete), muted in BLACKOUT.

## 10. Shore charge inhibit and the pack

`SHORE_INHIBIT` (controller GPIO 20, ribbon pin 26) reaches A22 through B16 and the dock strip E6 through A22's block: high = the shore and vehicle inputs are held off at the front end, so nothing charges. Low, floating or the controller dead = the inputs run. As generated nothing charges the 4S pack in any case: the charger's cell-count strap (`R26` 60.4k from VDDA over `R27` 40.2k, 40 %) sits in the BQ25731's 2S window of 35 to 48.5 % (TI SLUSE66A electrical characteristics), which sets 8.4 V charge and 12 V system over-voltage, so a generator fix to the 4S setting (75 %) is owed. Once it is fixed, the charger still needs a host (the kit-bus master writes its registers and services its 175 s watchdog), so with the controller dead it falls back to a small charge current: when the watchdog expires without a host write, `ChargeCurrent()` resets to 256 mA (TI SLUSE66A, 9.3.21.1, and the `WDTMR_ADJ` field, 175 s by default), and the register's power-on value is itself ambiguous in the same datasheet (0080h in the register heading, 0000h in its figure), so the exact figure is TBD until measured. That current feeds the kit's loads and the pack together: a kit with a crashed panel does not usefully charge. The bridge asks the controller to assert it when the pack temperature (the pack gauge's own thermistor, read by the sensor controller on E6 and reported over USB) is below 0 C, when the operator sets "no charge", and clears it with hysteresis (charge again above 3 C). Boot state: low. SHORE shows the charger's input-present bit, CHARGING its charge-in-progress bit; the pack is a built 4S smart pack with its own SMBus gauge, read by the sensor controller on E6 (not by the charger), so the battery bar comes from the pack's state of charge as the sensor controller reports it. As generated since `faf8c981` that path exists: both ends of the pack's SMBus lead are the same JST-XH 1x4 in the pack board's order (1 SMBC, 2 SMBD, 3 the pack's own negative lead, 4 PRES), so a straight four-way lead mates them, and `check_contracts.py` checks the lead as a cross-board contract (ASSEMBLY.md section 4). **Corrected 26 September 2026:** this paragraph said E6's end was a 2.54 mm 1x6 header that no lead mated, which was true before `faf8c981`.

## 11. Bridge protocol over USB (MESHSAT-837 defines the wire format)

The controller exposes a CDC serial port and a HID report. The bridge on the slot that owns the display sends: indicator states (one byte per LED), the lighting duty override, the sounder pattern, the e-paper page (a 1-bit bitmap or the idle page fields), the display-switch selection, the shutdown and kill commands, the charge inhibit. The controller reports: every switch state and edge with a timestamp, the heartbeat presence of the three slots, the light reading, the LED rail presence, the TX lamp state, the EMCON and ZEROIZE lines, its own firmware version. Any slot may read; only the display owner may write, and the owner is re-elected by the k3s cluster when its heartbeat stops (32.52).
