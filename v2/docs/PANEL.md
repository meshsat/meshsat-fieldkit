# MeshSat control panel (PCB-C backer C7 under the face plate): software contract for the panel controller and the bridge

> Generation MESHSAT-830 (7 Sep 2026, `V2-SPEC.md`, appendix 32.52, 32.60). Nothing has been built: this contract describes the C7 backer as generated by `v2/ecad/tools/gen_sch_c.py` and is the specification for the panel controller firmware (MESHSAT-837) and for the bridge on the compute modules. The previous contract (the released C6 with the PCA9555 pair on the compute module's bus and the previous e-paper module on SPI0) is in the git history of this file.

Hardware record: `MESHSAT-709-geometry-appendix.md` sections 32.52 (fabric), 32.56 (face), 32.60 (C7 design). The panel is a **USB device**: an RP2040 panel controller (`U3`) on **bank 1's** USB hub over the `USB_PNL` pair of the 2x13 ribbon `J_PANEL`. Since the I/O high-availability work of 9 September 2026 a bank is not a module: bank 1's home host is the slot 1 module and its failover host is slot 2, selected in hardware (`ARCH-PCB-B-IOHA.md`), so the panel keeps a host when one module is lost. The bridge sees the panel move hosts, never disappear, unless two modules are gone. The compute modules never touch the panel's pins directly; every indicator, switch, the e-paper, the sounder and the light sensor are behind the controller, which presents one USB composite device (a CDC console for the bridge protocol and a vendor HID report for the state). The lines that act without any software are MAIN PWR and EMCON (section 6). The TX lamp's sink follows D8's real KEY line in hardware, but its feed, `LED_RAIL`, exists only while the controller drives `PANEL_PWM` (section 3, GPIO 8), so as drawn the lamp needs the controller; moving its feed to `LED_RAIL_SW` is the board C author's open choice (`feasibility/EMCON.md` section 8). The ZEROIZE toggle is a hardware line too, but it reaches only the controller: the erase itself is the controller's firmware (decision 30, ruled by the owner on 26 September 2026, section 6).

LED drive rule: an LED is ON when its port bit is configured as an OUTPUT driving 0; OFF when the bit is configured as an INPUT (high impedance). Never drive a 1: the anode rail is the 5 V LED rail and a 3.3 V push-pull high ghosts the LED. On boot the firmware first writes the configuration registers so every LED bit is an input, then the output registers to 0, then enables outputs one by one.

Hardware LEDs, no software: MAIN ring (alive whenever the LED rail is present) and, since round 8 of MESHSAT-1357, the EMCON lamp (amber, `D22`: lit while `TX_INHIBIT_n` and `EMCON_HW` both read LOW, through `U14` and `Q7`, fed from `LED_RAIL_SW` like the MAIN ring, so it works with the controller dead, in reset or unflashed). TX (red, `D3`) follows D8's real KEY line `TR_APRS` through `Q3` in hardware and the lamp-test tie lights it too, but its anode is `LED_RAIL`, which exists only while the controller drives `PANEL_PWM` (`feasibility/EMCON.md` section 8; REQ-012 stays open on it). The lamp test cannot light `D22`: no expander reaches it, and setting EMCON is its test.

## 5. Compute slots seen from the panel

The controller owns three lines per slot: `HBn` in, `SLOT_ENn` out, and the display switch selects. Rules:

- Boot, in this order: `PI_KILL` driven low (push-pull, and never released while any slot is powered: on the netlist a powered slot's level stage can lift it and switch the kit off, a mechanism not yet measured); `PI_SHDN_REQ` released; the ZEROIZE toggle read (`ZEROIZE_SW`, GPIO 22), and if it is closed, or the flash holds a wipe-pending record, the erase of section 6 is completed before any slot powers; every expander's output register written before its configuration register (the default output register is all ones, so configuration first would switch on the PoE and USB-C stages), keeping `DEV_EN` at 1, which is written 0 only as the last act of a shutdown; the charger's registers written; then the three `SLOT_EN` high, one at a time. A slot whose heartbeat stays flat for 60 s after its rail came up is shown as a slot fault (MASTER CAUT, the e-paper names the slot); the controller power-cycles it once (rail off 5 s) and then leaves it off until the operator acts.
- `HDMI_SEL` follows the slot the bridge names over USB; with no bridge instruction the controller selects the lowest slot with a live heartbeat. Since board B's round 8 the display switches pass a slot only while that module's own 3.3 V is up (`U519`, `U520` qualify each switch's enable with the slot's power-good, FAB-02), so selecting a dark slot shows no picture rather than feeding the dark module's HDMI pins; with the panel absent B's `R15`/`R16` (10 k) hold both selects low, slot 1.
- The PI button: short press = `PI_SHDN_REQ` (clean shutdown of every module), hold 8 s = `PI_KILL`. The MAIN PWR button is hardware to A22's power controller and the controller only sees its effect.

## 9. Indicator semantics, controls and the e-paper

Indicators as in the previous generation: MASTER WARN flashes (3 to 5 Hz) for any unacknowledged red condition (SOS active, ZEROIZE armed, thermal trip, pack under-voltage, slot fault), MASTER CAUT for an unacknowledged amber one (a bearer lost that was up, shore lost while charging was expected, disk nearly full, a slot fault), SAT / MESH / LTE / GPS steady for a bearer up (GPS: fix), SHORE and CHARGING from the charger over the bus, MSG for an unread inbound message, the PI ring as a 0.5 Hz heartbeat while any slot's bridge runs, BAT1..5 for 5 s after a lamp test or a short TEST press with the lowest LED flashing below 10 %. A flasher that fails must fail to steady-on (the controller holds the last written state when the USB link drops).

Controls: TEST / ACK short press = acknowledge (flashers to steady, sounder muted, MSG cleared, battery bar 5 s); hold 2 s = lamp test (all seventeen controller-lit indicators for 3 s, a chirp, the battery bar after: the sixteen panel LEDs `D1` to `D16`, the TX lamp `D3` among them through its tie `D17`, and the PI button's ring; the MAIN and TEST rings are lit whenever the LED rail is present and are not the controller's to test, and the controller's status LED `D18` sits on the board, not in the face; reconciled 27 September 2026, S-39: the "17" was right; section 1 counts the face's LEDs, `D1` to `D16` and, since round 8, the hardware EMCON lamp `D22`, which is not among these seventeen: as drawn no expander and no test tie reaches it, so the lamp test cannot light it and setting EMCON is its test, section 4); hold 5 s within 60 s of a "show QR" request from the touch UI = the e-paper shows the enrolment QR while held. SOS closed 2 s = SOS mode (flipping back cancels; the e-paper confirms both). **What SOS does (D-10, owner ruling of 26 September 2026; firmware only, no board change):** the bridge sends a distress message with the kit's position over the bearers that are up, Iridium first when nothing else is, to a configured recipient list. It never transmits through EMCON: with EMCON asserted the message is queued until EMCON is released and the operator is told on the panel, as the SOS indications below set out. SOS is part of prototype 1's core acceptance, taken by the session under the owner's standing rule of 26 September 2026, because D-10 defines its action and the foundation decision table recommended it. EMCON = section 6 plus the software hold (queue every send, show EMCON on the e-paper, MASTER CAUT steady). ZEROIZE closed 5 s = the erase of section 6, and it is the ONLY trigger (owner ruling of 26 September 2026): the tamper or lid switch is logged and never wipes, and there is no remote wipe in this revision. MASTER WARN flashes while armed; flipping back inside 5 s aborts. The line is LEVEL-SENSITIVE across a power loss: at boot the controller reads it before any slot powers, a toggle still closed or a wipe-pending record in the controller's flash completes the erase, and the kit re-arms only when the switch is returned.

E-paper (PDi E2370KS0C1, 416 x 240, iTC controller, PDi's driving note rev 02): idle page with callsign, battery and shore state, GPS fix, UTC time, last inbound message, bearer chips and the slot health; refresh at most once a minute, full refresh once an hour and after ZEROIZE; the QR carries an enrolment URL plus a one-time code, never key material; the controller powers the display down through `EPD_PWR_n` between refreshes (the boost stage runs only while `BUSY` is high) and polls `BUSY` before every command.

**SOS indications (S-32's operator half; taken by the session on 27 September 2026 under the owner's standing rule of 26 September 2026, `CONOPS.md` section 7a).** Armed (the toggle closed 2 s): SOS ACTIVE (`D4`) flashes at 1 Hz, MASTER WARN flashes, the sounder runs its SOS pattern, and the e-paper shows "SOS ARMED" with the UTC time. Sent: `D4` goes steady once the message has been handed to a bearer, the sounder pattern ends, and the e-paper lists each bearer and recipient as the message goes, with the position sent (the LG290P's fix, or the last fix with its age when none is current); MASTER WARN stays until acknowledged. Queued under EMCON: `D4` keeps flashing, nothing transmits (D-10), and the e-paper shows "SOS QUEUED: EMCON ON, OPEN EMCON TO SEND". Queued for want of a bearer: the e-paper shows "SOS QUEUED: NO BEARER" and the time of the next attempt. No host for the panel (the panel controller's bank without a module, which is bank 1 as board B is generated and bank 3 once board B carries BANK-R1, `CONOPS.md` section 4c; or no module running): the controller shows it by itself, with no bridge: `D4` flashes at 4 Hz, MASTER WARN flashes, and it writes "SOS NOT SENT: NO HOST" on the e-paper. Cancelled (the toggle returned): `D4` goes dark and the e-paper shows "SOS CANCELLED" with the time; a message already sent is followed by a cancellation notice to the same recipients over the same bearers, under the same EMCON rule. In BLACKOUT the sounder and the LEDs are dark and the e-paper, which emits nothing, carries every indication.

**ZEROIZE indications (S-19; taken by the session on 27 September 2026 under the owner's standing rule, the wipe itself being `feasibility/ZEROIZE.md` section 3.4).** They need only this controller: no module and no bridge, because the slots are cut during the wipe. Arming (the toggle closed, before the 5 s commit): MASTER WARN flashes; flipping back aborts, and the e-paper shows "ZEROIZE ABORTED". Complete (both key-encryption keys destroyed and verified, the journal's DONE record): the sounder sounds continuously for 3 s, MASTER WARN goes steady, and the e-paper is fully refreshed to "ZEROIZED" with the UTC time and "KEYS DESTROYED AND VERIFIED", a page it keeps with the power off; the slots stay off while the toggle stays closed. Incomplete (a key not verified destroyed after the timed phase's retries): MASTER WARN keeps flashing, the sounder gives three 200 ms pulses every 5 s, and the e-paper shows "ZEROIZE INCOMPLETE: SLOTS HELD OFF, RETRYING"; the controller keeps retrying with the slots off (section 3.4 step 7) and at every boot (section 3.5), fail secure. In BLACKOUT only the e-paper shows the result, so an operator in BLACKOUT reads it there. `feasibility/ZEROIZE.md` section 3.4 step 8 ("sounds 3 s and refreshes the e-paper") is the complete case; the incomplete pattern above is handed to that page's owner to carry in the same step.

Sounder patterns: chirp 50 ms (acknowledge), double chirp (lamp test), 1 s on 1 s off (SOS armed, until the first send), continuous 3 s (ZEROIZE complete), three 200 ms pulses every 5 s (ZEROIZE incomplete), all muted in BLACKOUT.

## 10. Shore charge inhibit and the pack

`SHORE_INHIBIT` (controller GPIO 20, ribbon pin 26) reaches A22 through B16 and the dock strip E6 through A22's block: high = the shore and vehicle inputs are held off at the front end, so nothing charges. Low, floating or the controller dead = the inputs run. As generated since `458b2873` the charger's cell-count strap is `R26` 13.3k over `R27` 40.2k, 75.1 % of VDDA, inside the BQ25731's 4S window of 68.4 to 81.5 % (ChargeVoltage 16.8 V, SYSOVP and VSYS_MIN at their 4S defaults; TI SLUSE66A 8.5 and Table 9-2; `gen_sch_a.py:764-771` at `45bde541`), and the kit's loads sit on the charger's system node VSYS (the net `VBAT`), with the pack beyond the charge shunt `R17` and the 25 A blade `F1`, TI's own topology (SLUSE66A Figure 10-1; `gen_sch_a.py:18-48`). So shore carries the loads up to the charger's input limit without any software, about 1.6 to 1.7 A at the 20 V bus (about 31 W) until the host writes IIN_HOST, and the pack supplies above it. The charger still needs a host (the kit-bus master writes its registers and services its 175 s watchdog; it never terminates a charge by itself, SLUSE66A 9.4.1): ChargeCurrent() is 256 mA at power-on by TI's answer (E2E thread 1316778, 23 January 2024) against 0 A in the datasheet's prose, it returns to 256 mA whenever the watchdog expires without a host write (SLUSE66A 9.3.21.1, the `WDTMR_ADJ` field), and whether the charger charges before any host write is a bench question (TBD). That trickle goes to the pack alone, less board E's always-on draw, which sits on the pack side: a kit with a crashed panel is carried by shore at idle and its pack is held rather than charged (`review-packets/battery/CHARGER-STATE-SEQUENCE.md` sections 2 and 4 to 6). **Corrected 26 September 2026 (S-07):** this paragraph said the strap selected 2S (`R26` 60.4k) and that the 256 mA fed the loads and the pack together, which was the circuit before `458b2873`. Every charge hold (cold, hot, the margin hold, or the operator's 'no charge') is a flag the firmware keeps until its own condition clears it (the cold hold, read from the pack gauge's own thermistor by the sensor controller on E6 and reported over USB, below 0 C and cleared with hysteresis above 3 C), whatever the pack's state. While the pack can take charge or discharge, the flag sets the charger's CHRG_INHIBIT bit; with both pack FETs open the gauge holds and the bit is left clear, and it is written from the flag the moment a FET closes. No hold uses the CHG_INHIBIT line or SHORE_INHIBIT. The bridge asks the controller to assert SHORE_INHIBIT only for 'inputs off' and the water-on-floor isolation, with a warning first while the pack cannot discharge (L4-E11 7a, rule R-a; HW-FW-CONTRACT.md FW-A19, FW-C08 and FW-A14; restated 3 October 2026, Layer 5's power pass). Boot state: low. SHORE shows the charger's input-present bit, CHARGING its charge-in-progress bit; the pack is a built 4S smart pack with its own SMBus gauge, read by the sensor controller on E6 (not by the charger), so the battery bar comes from the pack's state of charge as the sensor controller reports it. As generated since `faf8c981` that path exists: both ends of the pack's SMBus lead are the same JST-XH 1x4 in the pack board's order (1 SMBC, 2 SMBD, 3 the pack's own negative lead, 4 PRES), so a straight four-way lead mates them, and `check_contracts.py` checks the lead as a cross-board contract (ASSEMBLY.md section 4). **Corrected 26 September 2026:** this paragraph said E6's end was a 2.54 mm 1x6 header that no lead mated, which was true before `faf8c981`.

## 11. Bridge protocol over USB (MESHSAT-837 defines the wire format)

The controller exposes a CDC serial port and a HID report. The bridge on the slot that owns the display sends: indicator states (one byte per LED), the lighting duty override, the sounder pattern, the e-paper page (a 1-bit bitmap or the idle page fields), the display-switch selection, the shutdown and kill commands, the charge inhibit. The controller reports: every switch state and edge with a timestamp, the heartbeat presence of the three slots, the light reading, the LED rail presence, the TX lamp state, the EMCON line (as `U13` copies it to GPIO 21) and the ZEROIZE toggle, its own firmware version. Any slot may read; only the display owner may write, and the owner is re-elected by the k3s cluster when its heartbeat stops (32.52).
