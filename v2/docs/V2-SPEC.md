# MeshSat field kit V2, approved specification (state of 7 Sep 2026, design record sections 32.40 to 32.61)

Prototype design. Nothing in this document has been built, ordered or field deployed. The 7 September text records the owner's rulings in `MESHSAT-709-geometry-appendix.md` together with proposals the session made under them (the 200 Wh pack of 32.62 was one), and the board generation that implements it is MESHSAT-830 (A22, B16, C7, D8, E6, face plate and case template). The generators of that generation ran on 7 September 2026 (the boards table below carries their numbers); items marked *owed* still need a document, a quote or a pick. Lines corrected on 26 September 2026 carry a numbered mark and are listed under "Corrections, 26 September 2026" at the end: each correction either names the owner ruling it follows (the identifiers D-nn of `CONOPS.md` section 7) or is a session reading of the generators against the makers' documents, and says which.

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|

| Compute | three identical CM5 slots (8 GB, 64 GB eMMC, wireless) joined by a five-port Gigabit switch chip with the sealed wall port and a spare header |
| Per slot | a PCIe switch (Diodes PI7C9X2G404SL, one upstream and three downstream lanes; 32.54) feeding one NVMe M.2 M-key 2242 (storage replicated across slots by k3s) and one card slot (slot 1 the WiFi link card, slot 2 the 5G module, slot 3 the second WiFi link card, correction 5); a four-port USB 3 hub with device headers; HDMI into the three-input display switch; time-pulse and heartbeat lines |
| Devices | most radios, the sensor controller and the panel are USB devices on a slot's hub (USB-serial bridges for the UART radios, two RP2040-class controllers for the panel and the sensors, a USB audio and control set for the APRS board); the HAL shares them to the other modules over the network. Not everything is on a hub: the LoRa module is on slot 3's SPI, the 5G module's data on slot 2's PCIe lane (its management link is USB), the two WiFi link cards on the PCIe switches of slots 1 and 3, the compute modules' own WiFi and Bluetooth on each module, and the secure element, the holdover clock and the TMP117 on the kit I2C bus (correction 6). **The physical owner is a hardware fabric choice, not a cabling one:** each of the three hub banks is switched between a home module and one neighbour by a 2-of-3 voted selection driven by three I/O supervisors (`ARCH-PCB-B-IOHA.md`), so the loss of one module is meant to move its bank rather than remove it. The WiFi link cards do not move; the antennas move to the second card through the voted changeover. The LoRa module and the 5G data path have no second path and go with their module (`ARCH-PCB-B-IOHA.md` section 15), the named exceptions to line 29's requirement for prototype 1 (`ARCH-PCB-B-IOHA.md` section 15a; correction 6). Compute redundancy through k3s is not peripheral redundancy and was never the same thing; the WiFi link cards and the NVMe drives are the only duplicated devices |
| Panel controller | LEDs, switches, sounder, e-paper, ambient light, blackout and NVG lighting, HDMI input select, heartbeats, the hardware EMCON line and the ZEROIZE toggle input independent of any module (the wipe itself is firmware and provisioning, D-03; correction 7); the secure element on its bus |
| Security | ZEROIZE as the owner ruled it on 26 September 2026 (D-03, decision 30): a crypto-erase through the secure element. Drive and eMMC keys are wrapped by a key the secure element holds; the covered toggle held 5 s, the only trigger, destroys that key, then the running modules drop their keys from RAM, then the slots are cut. Level-sensitive after a power loss: the toggle is read at boot before any slot powers, a wipe-pending record resumes the wipe, and the kit re-arms only when the toggle returns. Firmware and provisioning, no board change; precondition: an erasable ATECC608B slot map (NDA datasheet), a TPM 2.0 the fallback. Accepted residual risk: a drive unlocks at boot only through the secure element, the panel controller and the kit I2C bus. Firmware integrity: software-verified boot on the STM32H743 I/O supervisors, and on the compute modules if Raspberry Pi documents it, is the prototype's floor, and a hardware root of trust is required at a production trigger (owner ruling D-13, 26 Sep 2026; correction 14). | Time | the LG290P's time pulse fanned out to every slot; the DCF77 pulse reaches only the sensor controller on E (correction 8); a holdover RTC on the panel controller, chrony on each module |

## Qualification

The method-by-method plan with pass criteria is `TEST-PLAN.md` (approved item 16e).
A whole-kit test plan to MIL-STD-810 (transit drop, vibration, temperature operation and storage, humidity, immersion) and MIL-STD-461 (conducted emissions and susceptibility on the power leads, radiated emissions) is part of the design: written before the build, run on the built prototype in-house where possible and at a lab where not, pass criteria per test, fixes fed back into the record (approved 6 Sep 2026). **Owner rulings on this plan (`CONOPS.md` sections 2a and 7; correction 10).** On 25 September 2026 (D-02a) the owner ruled that the +55 C operation, +71 C storage (E3) and -33 C storage (E4) of `TEST-PLAN.md` are qualification margins over the envelope's -20 to +40 C in use and -20 to +45 C in storage, with two pass lines: operate to specification inside the envelope, survive and recover at the margin. On 26 September 2026 he ruled the severities E1 (26 drops from 1.22 m) and E2 (composite wheeled vehicle profile, 1 hour per axis) and the altitude, 0 to 3000 m in use and 0 to 4500 m in transport, with the service life TBD for prototype 1 (D-02c); no CE or RED marking and no EMC claim for the prototype, so every MIL-STD-461 run is characterisation (D-04); no vehicle surge claim (D-16); and the outside review route, prepared by the session with nothing spent beyond the SIDN voucher without his approval: a SIDN-voucher security review of ZEROIZE and key fill, a paid battery-and-protection review before the pack is built, and an EMC pre-compliance session once the prototype exists (D-09). Prototype 1 is accepted on a named core (D-01, 25 September 2026: messaging over Iridium, 5G, LoRa and APRS, the three-slot failover fabric, pack, vehicle and solar charging, hardware EMCON, ZEROIZE of the secure element, pack safety, service and programming access; SOS joins it as the session's choice under the owner's standing rule of 26 September 2026); every other function is built where possible and reported NOT_YET_TESTED, never as a pass.

## Thermal and environment
no vent opening anywhere in the case skin or the plate (owner ruling 7 Sep 2026, 32.53): the heat leaves through the aluminium face plate and the case walls; five IP68-rated internal fans (one per CM5 cooler, two mixer fans under the plate) driven by the sensor controller couple the inside air to the skin; the 30 W PA module bolts flat to the plate's underside on two PEM nuts with a thermal pad (32.56); estimated inside-air rise about 10 K with one module and 16 K with three loaded modules with the lid open (32.53), to be measured by `TEST-PLAN.md` E3; the kit is to be operated shaded, a stated operating condition, with a shade accessory (a lid sun shield or a tarp) and full-sun design a later qualification item (owner ruling D-02e, 26 Sep 2026); with the lid closed the kit runs the reduced mode (owner ruling D-02b of 25 Sep 2026: closed-lid operation in a defined reduced mode, for example GNSS, LoRa mesh, Iridium and APRS beacons, monitor off and one module, with a closed-lid state and thermal test to join `TEST-PLAN.md` and a lid sensor owed as an engineering item; accepted consequences: above +35 C ambient the kit runs one module, and with three loaded modules charging holds off above about +25 C ambient); the pack's heater mat and the inside climate sensor set the cold-weather behaviour, and a start from a pack cold-soaked below about -10 C at the cells is out of scope for the prototype: it needs shore or vehicle power, or warming, first (owner ruling D-02d, 26 Sep 2026); the case keeps Peli's own pressure equalisation valve (correction 10).
