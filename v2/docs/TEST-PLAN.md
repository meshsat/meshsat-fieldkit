# MeshSat field kit V2, qualification test plan (MIL-STD-810 and MIL-STD-461 methods; approved 6 Sep 2026, appendix 32.50 item 16e)

Prototype design. This plan is written before the build and run on the built prototype; nothing below has been run. Every test names the method it follows, the way it is run here (in-house on the bench or at a laboratory), the pass criterion, and where the result goes (a dated section of the design record with the measured numbers and the fix if it failed). Tests that need a laboratory are marked; their cost is the owner's decision when the prototype exists. **Revised 27 September 2026 (MESHSAT-1357, the layer-2 and layer-3 closing actions of the handover audit):** every row now states its **purpose** and the **requirement records it verifies** (identifiers of `v2/ecad/tools/pcb_requirements.yaml`); the stored state keeps the pack fitted (section 1); E5 is written for the sealed case and E8 names Peli's pressure valve; the closed-lid state and its test are defined against the ConOps's reduced mode; D-02a's two pass lines are on every margin; the MIL-STD-461 rows are characterisation (D-04); M7 is at decision 34's ruled level; the thermal characterisation tests the hot end depends on are section 8 and the mechanical fit checks section 9. This revision carries the battery stream's round-8 rewrite (sections 5 to 7, option C of `review-packets/battery/THERMAL-COORDINATION.md` section 9), checked against the review's rule that a qualification condition is never chosen because the circuit can pass it: its storage clause (pack out) did not meet that test and is replaced (section 1); the rest is kept. **Pass 2, later on 27 September 2026, answering the first pass of Review A (`reviews/REVIEW-A-LAYER-2-2026-09-27-pass1.md`):** E3-L carries E3-A's whole pass line with the lid closed, requires the owner's D-02b example set with the SOS path at every level (the generated board's shortfall is not waived), and fails on an SGP41 above +55 C (findings B4 and B5); E4-O restores round 8's +1.0 C charge floor and -9.0 C discharge stop and requires the kit-to-kit link after the cold warm-up (B7, m10); T-H3 replaces `ASSEMBLY.md`'s ten-minute key-down (B6); the transport and stored states have every input unplugged (m5). **The second release attempt of layers 1 to 3, later on 27 September 2026 (the release checks' layer 2 B2 and layer 3 R4):** P15 (section 7) forces the hot stop at room temperature on the pack and on shore, so REQ-077 is verifiable whatever a chamber reaches; E3-H gains a stepped run beyond the envelope and the rule that a step that acted in no run reads NOT_VERIFIED; E3-L's pass line is unchanged.

**Purposes, and the pass line each carries** (owner ruling D-02a; SC-03; D-04):

- **acceptance**: inside the adopted envelope (`OPERATING-ENVELOPE.md` section 4) or at a ruled severity; pass line: operate to specification.
- **margin**: a qualification margin beyond the envelope (D-02a, SC-03); pass line: survive and recover (no damage, no lost data or keys, full function once back inside the envelope). A margin result is never reported as an envelope claim.
- **deviation**: run in a configuration other than the state it represents, because a part of the product cannot take the level; it is stated in the row, its pass says nothing about the product in that state, and the product-level result it cannot give is recorded as a finding.
- **characterisation**: measured and recorded, with no pass line and no claim (D-04 makes every MIL-STD-461 run one; the thermal tests of section 8 measure what the design's bounds rest on).
- **protection**: a bench test of one protective function (sections 5 and 7); pass line: the function acts at its threshold and delay.

## 1. Test articles and conditions

- **Article:** one complete kit as specified in `V2-SPEC.md`: Peli 1450 of the current moulding (D-08a) with the 1450PF frame and the face plate, the board set A, B, C, D, E1, E5 and P as generated at the commit the build is ordered from (each board's phase is on `v2/docs/CURRENT-EVIDENCE.md`; corrected 27 September 2026: this line named the A22 to E6 set of 7 September), the built 4S3P pack (fitted or out as each test's state says), the Xenarc monitor, all radios and antennas fitted as for deployment, the lid tablet bracket loaded with a dummy mass.
- **States:** transport (closed, latched, antennas off, cables out and every input unplugged, the kit off, the pack fitted and put in its gauge's shutdown), deployed (open, antennas on, cables in, pack fitted, shaded, D-02e), deployed closed-lid (the reduced mode of D-02b as `CONOPS.md` section 4c defines it: slots 2 and 3, monitor off, and its one-module heat stage; lid closed, antennas on, cables in, pack fitted), stored (closed, latched, every input unplugged, the pack fitted at the cells' ex-factory state, 3.49 to 3.69 V per cell, and in its gauge's shutdown). These are `CONOPS.md` section 4's Transport, Normal, Reduced and Storage rows. **The states are session text, not an owner ruling:** the owner approved item 16e on 6 September 2026 (appendix 32.50: that this plan be written, with pass criteria per test); the session wrote the first three states on 7 September 2026 (`2e33773b`), when the pack was a bought BB-2590/U, and its stored state had the pack out; round 8 added "pack fitted" to the deployed states, the closed-lid state and "the kit off". **On 27 September 2026 the stored state changed to pack fitted** (`CONOPS.md` sections 4 and 7a), from the product need rather than from what a test can pass: the pack is bonded under the stack (`ASSEMBLY.md` section 1) and comes out only by the service lift of D-14, a field kit is kept ready to deploy, and the envelope's storage limits are the pack's own. Each test runs the kit in the state its row names, and a row that cannot says so as a **deviation**: E3-S, E3-O, E4-S and E5 keep the cells out of the exposure, because the cell maker rates them to +60 C at most in storage and in discharge and to -20 C at the lowest in storage (Samsung INR18650-35E Ver. 1.1 3.12 and 3.13), and the product-level result they cannot give is finding BAT-F19 (section 6). The pack's own exposures, fitted in the stored, transport and deployed states or alone, are section 6.
- **Instrumentation:** the kit's own sensor board (inside temperature, humidity, pressure, shock and tilt log, water sensor, gas) logs every test; the bridge's logs and the panel controller's event log are the record; external references are a calibrated thermometer, a pressure gauge for the seal check, and the laboratory's equipment where a laboratory runs the test.
- **Order:** the seal check first and after every environmental test; the functional check (section 4) before and after every test; a failure stops the sequence until its cause is recorded.

## 4. Functional check (run before and after every test)

Power on from the pack; the panel controller reports; every bearer comes up and passes traffic (Iridium message, 5G data, WiFi link to a second kit or a laptop, LoRa mesh packet, Zigbee and Thread join, APRS beacon heard by a receiver, HF CAT and audio, SDR capture, GNSS fix with time pulse); the display, touch, e-paper, LEDs, sounder, headset audio and PTT, camera; the sensors report; the seal check (inside against outside pressure after the valve settles) passes; EMCON silences every transmitter, measured with a receiver or spectrum analyser outside the kit and never with the kit's own SDR, whose supply EMCON removes (the per-transmitter bench tests of `feasibility/EMCON.md` section 6; corrected 26 September 2026, S-07); blackout darkens the kit; ZEROIZE wipes the secure element (verified by a failed key operation afterwards); the tamper switch logs the lid; shore, solar and vehicle inputs charge the pack; the PoE and USB-C outlets deliver; the log holds every step.

**Purpose and trace.** Acceptance, run before and after every test and at commissioning (`CONOPS.md` section 4d). It verifies: the core bearers passing traffic, REQ-001; the other bearers, REQ-002; a message each way over each long-range bearer in turn, with the kit's own hand-off within 10 s and the end-to-end time recorded as characterisation, REQ-003 (`CONOPS.md` section 4c); with one long-range bearer disabled, its queue delivered over the next bearer within 10 s of the bearer being declared down (NEED-02, REQ-076); the face, the e-paper and the indicators, REQ-007, REQ-008, REQ-013; the TX lamp and its lamp-test tie, REQ-012; the headsets, REQ-009; the camera, REQ-010; the sensors, REQ-041; the seal check, REQ-021 and CON-008; EMCON with its latency per row and the hardware EMCON lamp `D22`, REQ-030, REQ-031, REQ-032, REQ-071 (the bench tests E-01 to E-12 of `feasibility/EMCON.md` section 6 carry the fault conditions); blackout, REQ-033; ZEROIZE and its indications, REQ-035 and REQ-038 (the drives re-enrolled afterwards); the tamper switch and the lid-driven reduced mode, entered also after a start with the lid closed once the lid is read, REQ-036 and REQ-052; SOS, its queue under EMCON and its indications, REQ-060; the slot supervision, REQ-062; charging from each input, REQ-015, REQ-016, REQ-046; the outlets and their interlock, REQ-017 and CON-019. The failover fabric is accepted by `ARCH-PCB-B-IOHA.md` section 13's tests A1 to A14, each device of a moved bank back in service (`ARCH-PCB-B-IOHA.md` section 7 step 12, application access resumed) within 30 s plus its maker's start-up, and the bridge's own service back on a surviving module within 60 s of the loss (REQ-004, `CONOPS.md` section 4c).

## 5. Pack protection (rule BAT-001), function by function

Prototype design. **Nothing below has been run**, and none of it can be until a pack is built. These are
the tests the protection thresholds are derived FOR: `v2/ecad/tools/pcb_pack_protection.yaml` carries the
intended BQ4050RSMR configuration, every threshold inside the Samsung INR18650-35E's own limit at the pack's worst
parallel count (4S3P, the Pack Design Guideline's Portable IT column), and `pack_protection.py` judges that table
against the cell maker's own specification on every run. A test here is what turns a configured number
into a measured one.

Run them on a block that can be replaced, behind a current-limited supply and a fire blanket, with the
cell taps on a datalogger. Record every trip level AND its delay: a threshold that trips at the right
level and the wrong time is not the protection this rule asks for.
