# V2: the Peli 1520 carrier set (MESHSAT-709)

Seven KiCad 9 boards for the kit V2 go-box: a Peli 1520EU case with the 1520PF panel frame. The power and compute boards stack on four M3 rods and lift out of the case in one piece; a dock strip on the case floor carries the shore-power spring pins and stays wired to the case together with the RF junction strip on the wall. The control panel is the panel of the frame.

Three rulings bind the design (details in `docs/MESHSAT-709-geometry-appendix.md`, section 25): the Geekworm X1202 is the only charger and UPS, with the welded 1S8P pack in parallel with its own cells and nothing between them; EMCON is a hardware line from the panel to the APRS PTT driver plus a software hold; the panel is driven per `docs/PANEL.md`.

## Regenerating a board

The boards are generated by scripts, not drawn by hand. The pipeline runs on the design laptop (KiCad 9.0.9 with its Python bindings, headless):

1. `ecad/tools/full_<board>.sh` writes the schematic and the placed board from `gen_sch_<x>.py`, `gen_pcb_<x>.py` and `gen_pcb_<x>3.py` (placement, planes, keep-outs), then runs the numeric gate `check_pcb_<x>.py` against the appendix numbers.
2. `ecad/tools/route_parallel.sh <projectdir> <name> '<attempt list>'` runs Freerouting attempts in parallel; `finish_after_parallel.sh` picks the best session. The panel (PCB-C) gets one attempt: its optimiser pass takes 30 to 50 minutes and cannot be interrupted without losing the route.
3. `cleanup_dangling.py`, then `finish_board.sh <projectdir> <name> <postfix or -> <deliverable dirname>` runs the DRC, exports the fabrication files and writes the deliverable folder into `release/<rev>/boards/` (`MESHSAT_FK_REV`, default `revA`).
4. `make_handoff.py` rebuilds `release/<rev>/order/` and `release/<rev>/review/` from the deliverable folders, applying the JLCPCB rotation offsets and removing bench-fitted parts from the JLC BOM and CPL.

Prerequisites on the laptop: KiCad 9.0.9 (`kicad-cli` and `pcbnew` importable from `python3`), Java with `~/bin/freerouting-1.9.0.jar` (and `2.1.0`), Xvfb for the headless router, and `~/.venv-cad` with `build123d` for the STEP probes in `vendor/probes/` and `tools/peli_probe.py`. Route sessions, logs and per-user KiCad state are ignored by git (`out/`, `logs/`, `*.kicad_prl`).

## Case

Peli 1520EU without foam plus the 1520PF special application panel frame (CAD in `vendor/peli/1520/`). The panel is 442 x 311 mm on the frame's 16-hole pattern; the stack closes at 104.8 mm of the 114.1 mm available under the panel. RF blind-mate (SMP) is deferred to Rev B (MESHSAT-775); Rev A uses SMA couplers on the wall strip.
