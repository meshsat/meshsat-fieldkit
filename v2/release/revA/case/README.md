# Case machining, Rev A

`wall-receptacles-1to1.pdf` (two sheets, A4 landscape, print at 100 percent): the window and screw holes in the Peli 1520 base for the two MIL-DTL-38999 wall receptacles, and the 82 x 54 x 3 mm aluminium connector plate that carries them. Generated by `v2/ecad/tools/case_wall_cutouts.py`; the design record is appendix section 32.29, the leads are in `docs/ASSEMBLY.md` section 4.

The plate goes on the **back long wall, the hinge side**. The front wall carries the handle, the pressure valve, two rib clusters and both latch straps (Peli customer drawing 1521-931) and has no flat free area; the end walls are too narrow between their inner ribs. Both sheets are drawn as seen from outside the back wall, so case +X is on the viewer's left.

Why a plate: the 1520's inner wall drafts about 2 degrees and its outer skin is ribbed, so a flanged receptacle cannot seal on the case itself. The plate is flat, the receptacles seal on it with their own gaskets, and a 2 mm closed-cell gasket between plate and wall takes up the wall.
