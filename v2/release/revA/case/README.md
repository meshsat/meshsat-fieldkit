# Case machining, Rev A

`wall-receptacles-1to1.pdf` (two sheets, A4 landscape, print at 100 percent): the window and screw holes in the Peli 1520 base for the two MIL-DTL-38999 wall receptacles, and the 82 x 54 x 3 mm aluminium connector plate that carries them. Generated by `v2/ecad/tools/case_wall_cutouts.py`; the design record is appendix section 32.29, the leads are in `docs/ASSEMBLY.md` section 4.

Why a plate: the 1520's inner wall drafts about 2 degrees and its outer skin is ribbed (the Peli STEP is an envelope model, `vendor/peli/wall2.py`), so a flanged receptacle cannot seal on the case itself. The plate is flat, the receptacles seal on it with their own gaskets, and a 2 mm closed-cell gasket between plate and wall takes up the wall.

Nothing has been cut yet. Confirm the plate outline against the case in hand before machining, in particular that no latch or handle boss falls inside it on the outer skin.
