# Heating pad under the cell block: part search, 4 September 2026

Scope: one self-adhesive 12 V silicone rubber heating pad under a 75 x 198 mm, one-cell-thick lithium block in a printed enclosure, to warm the cells before charging in the cold. Hard constraints from the brief: 12 V DC nominal, at most 2.0 A (24 W) at 12 V because the feed is a 2.0 A eFuse, 10 to 20 W preferred; footprint no larger than 70 x 180 mm (one pad, or two in parallel that together stay under 2.0 A); thickness 2 mm or less; adhesive backed (3M or equivalent); leads at least 150 mm; watt density under 0.3 W/cm2 for continuous use against a cell face; a built-in thermostat or thermal fuse opening around 50 to 70 C preferred but not required.

Every number below is quoted from a saved document or page in this folder, or is arithmetic on those numbers (current = W / 12 V, watt density = W / area). Anything not from a document is marked unverified.

## 2. Recommendation

**Recommended: RS PRO 245-556, 50 x 150 mm, 7.5 W, 12 V dc (row 1).** It is the only candidate that meets every hard constraint on documents alone: the product sheet gives part number, size, voltage, wattage, peak and maximum operating temperature and the adhesive maker; the range leaflet gives the mat material, thickness, adhesive, lead specification (500 mm PTFE), bend radius and the operating rules; the current sheet of range sibling 731-366 gives thickness 1.4 mm and lead length 500 mm for the same construction. At 0.625 A it uses under a third of the 2.0 A eFuse, so wire, connector and eFuse tolerances are irrelevant, and its 0.10 W/cm2 is a third of the density limit, which matters because no stock candidate has a thermostat and this pad sits against cell cans: at that density the pad surface cannot run away far from the cell temperature even if the software hold fails, and the leaflet states the 12 V mats tolerate about 30 V before reaching their dissipation limit, so at 12 V the pad is far inside its rating. It is stocked by RS in the Netherlands under the same stock number (RS NL page, 403 to the runner, archived copy saved), and the identical size and wattage table is sold by Thermosense (LM50X150-7.5-12, sheet saved) and ESSKA (5034HM006000, EUR 32.16 incl. VAT, 4 weeks), so it is second-sourced in the EU. The one thing it does not give is the preferred 10 to 20 W: 7.5 W is 63 percent of the low edge of that band. Estimate only, not a sheet value: twelve 18650 cells of about 50 g each (Samsung 35E maximum weight) and a cell heat capacity near 1 kJ/kg/K give the block about 0.6 kJ/K, so 7.5 W raises it by roughly 10 K in 13 to 14 minutes before losses, against 8 to 9 minutes for 12 W; either is a pre-charge warm-up, not a heater that must keep up with the outdoors, and the printed enclosure inside the Peli case is the insulation. If the owner wants the band met with this family, the only in-footprint pairing is 245-528 plus 245-512 end to end (50 x 175 mm, 8.75 W, 0.73 A), which gains little; the 15 W member of the range (75 x 200 mm, Hawco SRM007, Thermosense LM75X200-15-12, ESSKA 5034HM007000) is the natural match for a 75 x 198 mm block but fails the 70 x 180 footprint on both axes, so it can only come back if the footprint ruling changes. Control: the leaflet requires an external thermostat and fuse for every mat of the range; the 2.0 A eFuse is the fuse, and the pack thermistor plus a series bimetal cut-out in the 50 to 70 C class would be the thermostat (a separate part, outside this search).

**Runner-up: O.E.M. Heaters SC774, 2 x 4 in (50.8 x 101.6 mm), 12 W, 12 V, PSA, 305 mm PTFE leads (row 6).** It is the only documented single stock silicone part found that delivers the preferred band inside the footprint at 12 V: 1.00 A leaves half the eFuse, 0.23 W/cm2 is under the density limit, the leads are twice the minimum, the adhesive is factory PSA, and it ships from stock. It is the runner-up rather than the pick because its documentation is thinner where the brief needs it: no thickness is stated anywhere on the seller's pages (a 2 mm limit cannot be ticked from documents), the maximum temperature (450 F) comes from the seller's generic article rather than the part page, there is no PDF sheet, resistance tolerance is not stated, and it is a US shop with no stated EU shipping at USD 70.99 before freight and import. Two SC774 in parallel would be 24 W and 2.0 A nominal with no tolerance margin on a 2.0 A eFuse and is not acceptable; SC774 plus RS 245-512 end to end (50.8 x 176.6 mm, 15.75 W, 1.31 A) fits and meets the band but mixes vendors and adhesive systems.

## 3. What could not be verified

- The exact thickness of 245-556: the two RS documents disagree (0.7 +-0.1 mm in the 2001 range leaflet against 1.4 mm on the 2020s sheet of sibling 731-366; the 245-556 sheet is silent). Both satisfy the 2 mm limit. The adhesive maker also differs between the documents (3M 966 in the leaflet, Nitto on the current sheet and page); the 2001 leaflet predates the current product and the current sheet should be taken as the live specification.
- Current RS NL price and stock for 245-556: nl.rs-online.com refuses the runner and WebFetch; the newest archived NL copy is from 1 Dec 2020 (EUR 34.23); the UK price GBP 30.84 ex VAT is from an 11 Apr 2026 archived copy; RS International shows P.O.A. and "Stock information temporarily unavailable".
- Resistance and tolerance of the RS, Thermosense, ESSKA and Hawco mats: only wattage is published.
- SC774: thickness, adhesive type and rating, resistance tolerance, EU shipping.
- ESSKA 5034HM006000: thickness, adhesive type, maximum temperature, and whether it is the same UK-made mat as RS PRO and Thermosense LM (identical size/wattage table and an "HM150X50-7,5/G" alternative code, but no maker named).
- Thermosense per-size price (the page shows only "as low as" for the range) and current stock.
- Hawco SRM parts: no sheet, no thickness, no lead or adhesive data, maker not named.
- Mouser: nothing could be read; no listing is claimed either way.
- ICstation numbers are from a search listing only.
- The manufacturer behind the RS PRO, Thermosense LM, Hawco SRM and ESSKA HM range ("Country of Origin: United Kingdom" on the RS page) is not named on any saved document.
- Sun Fun Kits wattage was not extracted (both pads fail the footprint anyway).
