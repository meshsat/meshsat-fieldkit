# Cable side of the two wall connectors, findings (4 Sep 2026)

Scope: the mating plugs, contacts, tooling, strain relief and cable for the two fixed wall receptacles of the connector plate (shore DC and solar on a Glenair D38999/20FC4PN, USB host on a Glenair 233-370 M 00-15 2AANH). Every figure below is read from a sheet saved in this folder; the sheet list with URLs is at the end. Nothing in the repository was touched.

How the sheets were obtained: glenair.com serves its catalogue pages and most PDFs to the runner, but two PDF folders (`/mil-dtl-38999/pdf/` and `/mil-spec/pdf/`) answer 403; the same sheets are served from the `connector-accessories-and-assembly-tools/pdf/` and `as85049-qualified-backshells-and-connector-accessories/pdf/` folders, and the socket contact guide came from the Series IV folder after the Series III path refused. The MIL-DTL-38999 specification itself was fetched from the Wayback Machine (`web.archive.org/web/2024id_/...`) because the DLA host redirects the runner to a login page. lapp.com and belden.com refuse the runner (403); Lapp's catalogue host products.lappgroup.com and alphawire.com answer normally. Belden and Igus were therefore not consulted.

## 4. Cable definitions

### 4.1 Shore lead (external, shore DC pair plus solar pair)

- Kit end: Glenair D38999/26FC4SN plug (class F electroless nickel to match the F receptacle, shell 13, insert 13-4, four M39029/56-352 size 16 socket contacts as delivered, key N), crimped with M22520/1-01 and positioner M22520/1-04, contacts seated with M81969/14-03; Glenair M85049/38S13N self-locking straight strain relief (electroless nickel) on the plug's M18 x 1.0 accessory thread, saddle clamp closed on the cable jacket.
- Cable: Lapp OLFLEX ROBUST 210 4 X 1.0 (0021917), 6.6 mm, black TPE, -40 C flexing; the North American equivalent is Alpha Wire 25064 (TPU, 6.58 mm) and the budget one Alpha 5064C (PVC). One length of 2 m from the plug to the split point (proposal; the record only fixes the currents, 4 A per pair, and the 9 to 36 V range; 0.37 V drop at 4 A over 2 m).
- Contact assignment (proposal, the record assigns none): A = shore DC positive, B = shore DC return (the isolated DC_N of the dock), C = solar positive, D = solar return. Lapp cores 1, 2, 3, 4 in that order; Alpha colours black, red, white, green in that order.
- Far end: the four cores split into two tails of about 0.3 m under adhesive-lined heat shrink, the DC pair to the shore supply's connector and the solar pair to the panel's connector. Neither connector is chosen in the record (the dock side is J_DCIN and J_SOLAR on the strip; nothing names the supply or the panel plug), so both stay open (section 5). Ends and length are the two owner choices left in this lead.

### 4.2 USB host lead (external)

- Kit end: Glenair 233-340 plug, electroless nickel, style G6, shell 15, USB 2.0, Type A male front, Type A female back, key N, horizontal, shrink boot option T (770-028): composed code 233-340 M G6 -15 2 A A N H T. It screws onto the 233-370's outer face (1.0000-.1P-.3L-TS-2A) and its USB male enters the feed-through's outer female coupler.
- Cable: a standard USB 2.0 Type A male to Type A female extension, 1 to 2 m (proposal: 1.5 m; a USB 2.0 segment may be 5 m and the internal A-to-A cable from J_WALL1 to the feed-through is about 0.1 m), its A-male plugged into the back of the 233-340 and the 770-028 boot shrunk over its jacket. No Glenair part number exists for the extension; any 2 x 28 AWG data plus 2 x 24 AWG power USB 2.0 extension serves.
- Far end: the extension's Type A female, into which the user's device lead plugs. This is the general host-port answer; when the outside device has an A or B receptacle instead, Glenair's own potted cordsets replace the whole lead: 2330-0015 ME G6 S -15 2 A N H A S -04 (5 ft, A-male far end) or 2330-0069 M G6 N H B -60 (60 inch, B-male far end).
- The 233-345 crimp plug (four M39029/56-348 sockets loose, M22520/2-01 with M22520/2-09) is the third route if a specific USB cable must be terminated in the field; it needs its own shell 15 strain relief (M85049/38S15N, cable entry 6.6 to 11.6 mm) or the T boot.

## 5. What could not be verified, and notes for the record

1. The composed Glenair codes for the USB parts (233-340 M G6 -15 2 A A N H T, 233-345 M G6 -15 2 A N H T, 2330-0015 ME G6 S -15 2 A N H A S -04, 2330-0069 M G6 N H A -60) follow each sheet's part number development exactly, but no sheet prints these strings, and Glenair's electroless nickel code is M on the 233-3xx sheets, ME on the 2330-0015 sheet and both on the 2330-0069 sheet. The D38999/26FC4SN and M85049/38S13N codes are QPL grammar and unambiguous.
2. The 233-370 sheet names no mating plug. The match is by the D38999 Series III shell 15 interface and the USB Type A coupler that all the family's plugs share; Glenair has not been asked to confirm it in writing.
3. The Lapp sheets give no diameter for the 1.0 mm2 insulated core, so the 1.65 to 2.77 mm grommet check is done only for the Alpha cables (2.0 mm). Measure one Lapp core or ask Lapp before the ROBUST 210 is bought; the Alpha 25064 needs no such check.
4. The AS85049/124 shell 13 numbers (C dim 8.7 +-0.8 mm with the saddle bars closed) are quoted as printed; their reading as a cable range is mine, and it is why the /38 was kept despite the sheet's "for new design use AS85049/124" line.
5. The shore lead's far-end connectors (shore supply, solar panel) and its length are not in the record and were not chosen here; no MC4 or supply connector sheet was fetched.
6. The 13-4 contact assignment in 4.1 is a proposal; the record only says "the 38999's DC pair" and "the 38999's spare pair" for J_DCIN and J_SOLAR.
7. Two details of the record that the cable side depends on: the receptacle code 233-370 M 00-15 2AANH says style 00 (slotted holes, front panel mount only, holes on a 23.01 mm square) while the plate cut-out paragraph of the appendix draws the D0 round-hole style (holes on a 24.61 mm square); and the 233-370 sheet gives the panel accommodation as .0625 to .250 inch (1.59 to 6.35 mm). Both concern the plate drawing, not the plug, and are only flagged.
8. Belden and Igus were not consulted (belden.com refuses the runner; Igus was not needed once two makers covered the size). The Wayback Machine holds no copy of the Glenair accessory PDFs that the runner cannot fetch directly, but those were obtained from the alternative Glenair folders named above, so no sheet here is second-hand except the specification.
