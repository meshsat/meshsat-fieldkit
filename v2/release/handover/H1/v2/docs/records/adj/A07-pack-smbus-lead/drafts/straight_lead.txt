A07 straight (pin n to pin n) lead, P J_SMB (JST-XH B4B-XH-A 1x4, 2.50 mm) into E J_SMB (PinHeader 1x06, 2.54 mm)
Sources: v2/ecad/pcb-p-pack-p2/out/pcb-p-pack.net (a0b707f9) and v2/ecad/pcb-e1-dock-e7/out/pcb-e1-dock.net (824ec9d4), read by drafts/pinmap.py -> drafts/pinmap.out
pin | P net (role)                                   | E net (role)                                                        | result
1   | SMBC (gauge clock, R20 100R to U1 pin 19)      | SDA0 (U10 pin 4 = RP2040 GPIO2 = I2C1 SDA, R34 4.7k)                 | clock onto data
2   | SMBD (gauge data, R21 100R to U1 pin 18)       | SCL0 (U10 pin 5 = GPIO3 = I2C1 SCL, R35 4.7k)                        | data onto clock
3   | GND (cell side, upstream of shunt R10)         | SDA1 (sensor bus: U10 pin 6, U14 BME688, U15 BMI270, J_LTG, J_POD, D9, R36) | sensor bus data held at 0 V, whole sensor bus dead
4   | PRES (R14 10k to GND, U1 pin 16)               | SCL1 (same sensor bus, R37 4.7k to +3V3_E6)                          | PRES static 3.3*10/14.7 = 2.24 V > VIH 1.3 V (SLUSC67B 6.12), toggled by every SCL1 clock
5,6 | (none)                                         | GND                                                                  | no ground conductor at all
Pitch and count differ (2.50 x4 against 2.54 x6), so the lead would need two different housings in any case.
