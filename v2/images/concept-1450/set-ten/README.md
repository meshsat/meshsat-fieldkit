# The kit assembled: eighteen views, set ten, rendered 15 September 2026

A concept model of the MeshSat field kit V2 in a Peli 1450 with its 1450PF frame: the seven carrier boards as exported from their KiCad files (A32, B19 placed, C17, D11, E9, E5, P4), the makers' STEP models where the project holds them, and stand-in shapes for the rest. **Prototype design, nothing built.** Rulers in mm, 10 mm ticks, numerals every 50; the floor grid is 50 mm.

**The monitor (Xenarc 709GNK) sits IN the 3 mm aluminium face plate with its glass level with the plate's top face**, 0 mm proud in the model; the project holds it to 1 mm at most. The sets before this one (set eight and earlier) showed it 28.7 mm proud, which was a scene defect corrected on 9 September; those images are superseded.
