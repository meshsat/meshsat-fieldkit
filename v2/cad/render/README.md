# Concept renders of the assembled V2 kit (Peli 1450)

Blender scene of the whole kit as designed on 5 September 2026 (design record 32.40 to 32.42): the Peli 1450 base and lid from Peli's own 1451-931 CAD, the 1450PF frame, the 3 mm aluminium face plate with the backer board C6 under it, the dock strip E4 and block E5, PCB-A A21 with the APRS mezzanine D7, the Compute Module 5 carrier B15, the battery row along the west end wall, the nine end-wall antennas with their pigtails, the upright connector plate on the back wall with both cables plugged, and rulers in every view. Everything sits at the case-frame positions of the design record (X along the long axis, +Y the hinge wall, Z up from the cavity floor). The images in `v2/images/concept-1450/` are concept illustrations of a design that has not been built. The earlier 1520 set (`v2/images/concept/`) is history.

What is real geometry and what is a stand-in:

- The boards are the KiCad layouts exported as GLB (pads and the library models of the parts).
- The case body and lid are Peli's 1451-931 STEP files (`v2/vendor/peli/1450/`) converted to STL. Peli's files are envelope bodies with the cavity but without the outside features, so the end-wall ribs, the two latches, the folding handle, the pressure valve, the hinge, the corner bosses, the padlock protectors and the feet are drawn in the scene from the drawing and the product photographs; they are illustration, not vendor CAD. The base body spans 25.4 mm below the cavity floor (floor and feet as one slab), so the ground plane sits under that slab. `CASE_MODE=model` in the environment draws the whole case from the 1450 numbers instead.
- The frame, the Touch Display 2, the WeAct e-paper and the RockBLOCK 9704 are the makers' STEP models converted to STL; the face plate and the battery module are our own build123d parts (`v2/cad/face_plate.py`, `v2/cad/battery_module.py`).
- The switches follow the named parts (C&K ATP anti-vandal bezels with lit rings, APEM locking toggles with red tips, the NKK light switch with its boot, the Floyd Bell sounder); the sixteen LEDs show as the heads of the Mentor 1282.5004 light guides; the legends, the nameplate and the logo mark are text on the plate.
- The antennas are generic whips of the lengths in the record, an Iridium patch and a GNSS puck; the pigtails inside run from each coupler down the wall to the dock nests (and the two WiFi P2P leads to the M.2 card).
- Rulers: white bars with 10 mm ticks and numerals every 50 mm along the front, back and both sides of the case on the ground, and two standing at the front corners; a 50 mm grid on the ground; a title strip on each image (stamped by `run_render1450.sh`).

Everything the scene needs is in this folder: `glb/` holds the six board exports (case-frame origins), `stl/` the STL conversions the scene uses, `ui.png` and `epaper.png` the screen contents, `scene.py` the scene, `step2stl.py` the converter, `run_render1450.sh` the VM run script. The scene reads `v2/ecad/tools/panel1450.py` for every face position, so the plate, the backer board and the renders cannot disagree.

Reproduce on the build host (Blender 4.2 with CUDA/OptiX, KiCad 9, `~/.venv-cad` with build123d, ezdxf and matplotlib):

1. `kicad-cli pcb export glb --subst-models --include-pads --user-origin <OX>x<OY>mm -o ~/render3d/glb/<name>.glb <board>.kicad_pcb` for the six boards (origins 150x110 for A, B, E1, E5; 297x210 for C, exported as `pcb-c6-backer.glb`; 100x100 for D), with `KICAD9_3DMODEL_DIR` pointing at a folder holding the library STEP models the footprints reference.
2. `~/.venv-cad/bin/python step2stl.py` converts the Peli bodies, the frame, the display, the e-paper, the RockBLOCK, the face plate and the battery module to STL under `~/render3d/stl/`.
3. `python3 make_epaper.py` (needs `qrcode` and Pillow) writes `epaper.png`.
4. `CYCLES_GPU=1 blender -b -P scene.py -- <out dir> all` renders the set: 24 orbit views (`azNNN-elNN-open`, eight azimuths by three elevations with the lid open), eight closed views, and the details (`top-face`, `face-detail-left`, `face-detail-right`, `west-wall`, `east-wall`, `back-wall`, `front-wall`, `cutaway`, `battery-row`, `face-underside`, `stack-no-face`); `orbit`, `closed`, `details` or single view names select a subset; `SAMPLES` and `RESPCT` in the environment trade quality for time (a preview at `RESPCT=40 SAMPLES=48` takes about a minute per view). `run_render1450.sh` does steps 1, 2 and 4 and stamps the title strips.
5. `DUMP_BBOX=<file> blender -b -P scene.py -- <out dir>` writes every object's bounding box for `v2/ecad/tools/stack_heightmap.py`, which feeds the C6 gate.
